## Established event sequence

- Workers 19 and 20 become Nurses at 15,000 ms after Rest, then switch to Forage at 25,000 ms, exactly the 10,000 ms minimum. Each has made ten nest intakes starting at 14,009 ms, holds 222 ug crop protein at departure, has made no gift, and retains that protein at death.
- Worker 21 becomes Nurse at 15,000 ms; workers 30 and 31 at 20,000 and 15,000 ms. All switch to Forage at 35,000 ms. They make 19/19/18 nest intakes and only one protein gift each (54/55/53 ug), retaining 368/368/347 ug crop protein respectively at departure and death.
- The nurse wake's existing half-crop refill branch precedes encounter sampling. Water/carbohydrate absorption continually frees crop capacity, so possessing substantial protein does not stop repeated mixed-food intake. A NutritionDecision does not prove that a recipient was sampled or fed.
- The original autonomous decision resamples Rest/Forage/Nurse/Excavate after the minimum duration. A local demand cue adds a Nurse option but does not preserve existing compatible nursing work. Forage/excavation travel ownership then requires an actual depot return for a routine change, which can delay renewed feeding. No global deficit or hidden food source is involved.

These facts justify separate gift-priority and local-work-continuation ablations, not an arbitrary increase in a nursing weight. Observer replay will additionally expose the actual deterministic local sample at each autonomy timestamp and verify that observation leaves the complete fact stream unchanged. Sampling limitations and unreachable demand remain possible contributing mechanisms; the baseline event journal alone does not encode every contact candidate.

The completed observer replay, with profiling both disabled and enabled, reproduces every original fact byte exactly (SHA-256 above). `seed7_autonomy_observation.csv` records pure reconstruction of the private decision sample at the end of its Reschedule timestamp; that phase changes programs but not inventory, position or life stage. Departing workers 19/20 sampled 4/6 needy larvae at 25,000 ms; departing workers 21/30/31 each sampled seven at 35,000 ms. Thus these exits cannot be attributed to failure to encounter local demand. The `compatible_crop_protein_ug` column sums pairwise possible matches and can reuse the same donor inventory across pairs; it is a contact-cue diagnostic, not available colony stock or a promised transfer quantity.

## Performance attribution before optimization

The retained 120-sample rerun and the separate 36-sample instrumentation-on/off runs have identical scenario inputs and work counts. Profiling-on/off matches all 12 canonical state/trace groups. In the first dense bounded 2,048 sample, candidate sampling consumes 218.531 ms exclusive out of 454.866 ms advancement; 512 uses 24.612 ms. There is no contact-index maintenance in the static advancement phase. Every rank-tree edge currently performs another logarithmic map lookup; physiology/policy and scheduler costs also grow. This supports caching disposable child-node references, while keeping IDs, canonical treap shape, candidate ranks and tree-visit counts unchanged. Map node addresses remain stable through insertion and retained-node rollback. Restore rebuilds references; no pointer is serialized or exposed to a caller.

All samples and category medians are retained in `profile_before`, `unprofiled_before` and `analysis_before`. Host variability is substantial: the full original-source rerun's 512->2,048 median ratio is 2.446x, while the later matched uninstrumented three-repetition control is 6.497x. This means a favorable isolated ratio alone cannot prove a causal optimization or a universal latency guarantee. No slow sample is removed. Instrumented timings contain clock/scope overhead and are not performance acceptance timings.

The high active-program fraction and identical resource-treatment excavation volumes will be analyzed through program residence, legal reconsideration opportunities and timing of actual initial excavation. They are not assumed to be a physiology defect, nor evidence of biological nest-demand regulation.
