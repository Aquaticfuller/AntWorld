# Implementation details frozen before integrated outcome measurement

Document 35 is the controlling protocol; the following resolves omitted fixture details without changing its weights/rates. `s6_fixture.hpp` contains the complete geometry/population/material inputs. All values remain ENGINEERING_FIXTURE.

Local requests retain S3's urgent adult threshold (body energy or hydration below one quarter of capacity). Larval protein shortfall is the brood cue. Within an eligible contacted recipient, actual transfer is capped independently by carbohydrate, protein and water demand; crop capacity still applies. Slight reserve depletion in every adult is not treated as an urgent social request. Larvae with no unmet protein requirement do not signal under this initial proxy; later nutritional needs and supply-limited incomplete development remain reported limitations. The automatic nurse option uses this same local signal and locally available crop/depot ingredients. No physiology rates are changed.

Automatic decisions occur in Reschedule after that timestamp's physical/physiological resolution. All workers' choices are frozen before any program changes commit. The first adult reconsideration is 5 s after enrollment; brood ownership starts its first 5 s wake only on real emergence. Subsequent choices honor the 10 s minimum duration. A manual program change disables automatic reconsideration; disabling autonomy alone leaves the current program running. Death disables both. Routine switches out of Forage/Excavate and entry into either travel program require the actual depot position and no carried food/soil. Stationary workers may choose Rest/Nurse at their current location, including off-plane emergence; their depot inventory cue requires actual local contact. There is no rescue from an inaccessible return route.

The six-axis soil cue samples at most the existing four geometry quanta in each direction, stops at the first solid material and never sees soil behind rock. It only enables an excavation option; existing experienced movement, clearance, energy and material transactions still perform all work. A saturated SDF value is not used as proof of nearby soil. Planar foraging requires a currently reachable local step.

Surplus assay: one donor and two initially empty-crop larvae, 180 s, every seed 1..64. Run a donor initially adult and initially pupa (100 ug retained tissue protein), retaining its identity through emergence. Donor crop is (100,400,400,100) ug; no-protein control replaces 400 ug protein with inert mass. Every worker/brood is enrolled without assigning a Nurse role. All physiological defaults and social weights stay fixed. Geometry quantum 0.0005 m, origin (-0.016,-0.016,-0.016), InitialSolid [0,64)^3; open room [4,60)^3. Donor/depot at q=(31,32,32), larvae at (34,32,32). Range control places larvae at (48,32,32). Wall control replaces the open room with [4,32)x[4,60)^2 and [33,60)x[4,60)^2, separated by immutable rock [32,33)x[4,60)^2. No-response keeps positions/resources but disables exchange. No-protein and no-response retain the entire run; all controls use identical observation windows. Sensitivity uses seeds 1..16 and budgets 8/16/32, without selecting outcomes.

Host limits for integration/longer depletion are one million committed facts, 10,000 events per timestamp and a 256 MiB checkpoint. These are explicit host budgets, not biological carrying capacities. Every record is retained. Any exhaustion or negative ecological outcome must be reported.
