Accessed 2026-11-09. DOI links identify the version of record. `Full text / selected sections` means the relevant methods, results and stated locators were read; it does not claim that every supplementary file was read. Licensed article bodies, extracted text and page images remain in ignored `out/s6_3_2/literature`. Published access receipts contain URLs/hashes, never full texts. Species and experimental unit matter: workers from one colony are not independent colony replicates. No care websites or review-only statements are used to calibrate the model.

## Search and access audit

Queries covered Lasius niger founding/queen care/worker removal; queen and worker respiration, reserves and lifespan; temperature-dependent brood development; nutrition, trophallaxis and networks; age/polyethism/activity; trail/memory/recruitment; nest excavation and thermal structure. Searches used publisher, DOI, PubMed/PMC, institutional and author repositories. Reference chaining was used for B05/B06/B19/B20 and original trail experiments. The dates are access dates, not claims of a systematic-review preregistration.

`experiments/s6_3_2/literature_access.json` retains successful and failed host requests. Wiley403 and some EuropePMC500 responses were not bypassed; accessible publisher HTML or institutional/author copies were used. Host Springer access supplied complete PDFs for B05/B06/B17, not just abstracts. Some downloaded candidates are deliberately unused: their presence in an access receipt does not mean their evidence was adopted. This is a broad targeted audit, not a PRISMA review or proof that no other evidence exists.

Unresolved primary-data needs: dry/wet mass and mobilizable reserves of the specific queen age/condition; per-worker-egg export versus synthesis overhead; whole-individual stage/caste metabolic rates; assimilation throughput at known temperature; queen solicitation and gift timing; quantitative sleep/activity and task-transition hazards; colony-specific trail concentration/decay and contact sensing. Do not fill these gaps with model-fitting values relabeled as measurements.
