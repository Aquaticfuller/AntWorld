## Observed bottlenecks

In the original B02 fixture all 48 caregivers have an in-range displaced item at their first decision 1 ms after release. A first actual handling contact occurs in 8 of 12 queens and in 28 of 36 individual workers. The first contact of the group is a separate endpoint. No caregiver starts an untargeted search action in this fixture. Queens spend a mean 519.1 of 600 s inactive, and worker groups spend a mean 1,527.4 of 1,800 caregiver-seconds inactive. Queens switch selected brood identity a mean 4.58 times and workers 4.11 times per individual. The clock imposes a 60 s inactive act when selection is declined, even when the same reachable approach could continue.

Of 89 recorded transport segment terminals, 85 complete with real movement and 4 are reservation conflicts. The typical actual displacement is 1 mm per 10 s segment. The 44 caregiver-target transport attempts yield only 9 first deliveries. Successful deliveries take 30 to 280 s from first transport start because segments can be separated by waiting and other decisions. After those 9 deliveries, while other items remain undelivered, zero caregivers start untargeted search and only one starts a new displaced-brood contact. Follow-up varies from 20 to 300 s, so missing second contacts and deliveries are censored. The traces support continuing a locally initiated retrieval and testing a bounded departure from settled brood. The open arena traces show no explicit geometry rejection. The prior planner could convert a blocked movement to an inactivity choice before logging, and its actual range and sweep checks still apply in the new study.

B01 55-day absence, addition at 24 h and removal at 48 h have 50-scan overlap means 23.17%, 2.83% and 22.00%. Instantaneous start-of-scan scores are 15.67%, 2.00% and 15.00%, with actual active-care fractions 15.51%, 1.76% and 14.85%. The original observation definition accounts for part of the difference while the two alternate time measurements still exceed the original observed 6.50%, 0.125% and 6.25% scan fractions. The latter are different measurement definitions. After addition, the simulation records a mean 430 opportunities and 21.58 completed worker contacts per queen-hour. Opportunities alone do not induce the cue. On the same six absence seeds the 15, 60 and 240 s inactive intervals produce 60.33%, 25.33% and 4.33% 50-scan overlap. No interval is selected on that basis. Glass-tube transfer, mesh antennation, worker behaviors and real aging remain unmatched.

## Single opt-in mechanism

Introduce a versioned individual retrieval episode. Retain the original perception range, activity threshold, clearance, steps, decision interval, bout duration, worker contact and initial care propensity. In an ordinary decision the existing social-contact priority and initial care propensity apply. If a living same-colony brood item outside the familiar shelter radius is locally seen and unreserved, select among those eligible displaced items before settled manipulation. Exclude a visibly held item only from eligibility. Publish every existing Opportunity fact and retain the existing transactional arbitration for competing starts.

Once the ant starts an episode, retain the selected identity across successful approach and transport segments while it is still an eligible local observation. A continuation does not repeat the initial care lottery or unrelated social activity. Every approach and loaded segment uses the original swept motion and elapsed bout time. Target memory does not confer contact, occupancy, or an updated position outside perception. A completed nonzero transport segment anchors one visited pickup position to the actual started bout. Once that identity enters the known shelter radius, take one return excursion toward that position using the existing bounded movement steps. A newly perceived displaced item can continue the episode, with a fresh pickup record after actual transport. Arriving at the remembered point, a blocked or rejected segment, loss of a pursued target, incapacity, or interruption clears the episode. Normal care resumes after termination. An episode has room for one identity and one position, and contains no colony-wide target knowledge or path search.

Use a separate committed revisit act to distinguish a return search from first-time walking. Persistence is intent across validated finite segments. Each segment retains the ordinary physical reservation and is independently interruptible. The memory, its observed pickup bout, and the pending act must restore atomically. A schema 12 and physics 14 checkpoint identifies the opt-in variant. The default preserves the schema 11 and physics 13 S6.3.3 event and checkpoint behavior. No new numerical biology coefficient is introduced. Displaced-first selection, continuation and single-site return are grade F `ENGINEERING_FIXTURE` assumptions.

Its tilt-induced pile, 25 C colonies, multiple carriers and relative trip counts differ from B02, and it does not supply a matching timed first-contact or queen-trip dataset.
