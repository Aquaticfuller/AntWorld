## Observed mechanisms, not fitted parameters

1. Missing nest-return completion: the new scheduler callback recognized explicit Return-mode arrivals, but the inherited forager can revisit its nest during Explore. The inherited `after_action` already counts a new trip when it next leaves home. These depot visits never notified the new recovery rule. Empty-resource runs therefore retained hundreds of thousands of movements and hit the 2-million-fact guard. This is an inconsistent trip boundary, not evidence for a lower movement rate or a larger memory limit.
2. Assimilation/encounter separation: in seed 1, eight workers, balanced supply, the first larva received repeated 2-ug protein-only gifts. A nurse's actual preceding withdrawals were approximately 47/2/51 ug carbohydrate/protein/water. The six-hour absorption boundary converted nearly all liquid carbohydrate/water into the donor's own stores before its next six-hour nursing decision. By day 7 the first larva had only 52 ug tissue and 0.068 J; it subsequently died, despite stocked food. This exposes the assumption that every step of one local intake/exchange interaction waits a complete task reconsideration period. It does not establish biological optimality of any allocation.
3. The ample initial chamber often produces zero digging under the new space-sufficiency inhibition. Zero excavation can be a correct response in this fixture. A separate compact-space control is required to test whether demand can initiate work and newly created local space can inhibit it.

## Frozen changes and additional controls

- H6: notify the returning individual after any actual unloaded arrival at its known depot, including Explore-mode returns. Preserve the existing per-step movement, finite path, collection, RNG stream and recording. Do not teleport or mark a trip complete away from the nest.
- H7: after a successful local depot withdrawal by a Nurse, allow one immediate follow-up perception (1 ms, the existing action-boundary latency) while the individual remains there. The follow-up samples real local contacts again and may fail or choose another recipient. It does not reserve a recipient, force feeding, bypass assimilation, or repeat an unlimited fast loop. After the resulting gift/rest action, normal six-hour reconsideration resumes. Self-maintenance remains first when urgent. No additional nutrient appears.

## Scientific interpretation limits

The long-term fixture remains deliberately uncalibrated. It is not a finding that real Lasius niger has six-hour nursing intervals or millisecond exchanges. H7 separates encounter completion from coarse task reconsideration without tuning transfer quantity or foraging yield. H6 repairs scheduler ownership at an actual local journey boundary. Retain SD-04 allocation timing and surplus transport questions, SD-06 demand/morphology validation, SD-07 rest/active calibration, S2 recruitment and storage/scaling debt. These interventions will be evaluated by mechanism evidence and complete negative outcomes, not mandatory population growth.
