# S6.3.3 care and observation contract

Authorization: P01/P10 only. Protocol52 was committed before biological implementation (e73e761). B01/B02 establish maternal capability and social plasticity, not the new coefficients or specific contact-memory law. No fitting, maternal survival protection or metabolic/reproductive/thermal correction occurs here.

## Three profile layers and local decisions

`SpeciesCareProfile` contains the frozen numerical hypotheses; `IndividualCareProfile` distinguishes founding/established queen from worker and records declared age. Age/context are provenance labels, not simulated histories or fitted rate multipliers. `EnvironmentCareProfile` records temperature/RH; no thermal response is implemented. Enrollment requires a live adult queen or worker, physiology and local familiarity with the colony shelter. Queen care uses a separate program, with no worker forage/excavation permissions. Pausing/enrolling is a validated transaction.

Care perception gathers same-colony living neighbors within6mm through the existing spatial visibility query. The actor's real body energy and hydration must each exceed the quarter-capacity urgency proxy. This threshold remains F. Only completed two-second close-worker bouts update individual contact memory. Candidate presence alone is an opportunity. A queen's care probability is `p0*(1-i*max(0,1-elapsed/H))`; without a completed encounter it is p0. This finite, fading contact hypothesis is consistent with qualitative reversibility but is not the physiological mechanism identified by B01. No colony population count is consulted. Workers do not use queen social suppression.

Choices at a common Perception boundary precede Resolution arbitration. Individual streams and decision counters are persisted. Uniform bounded random selection does not prefer the smallest entity ID. Numerical RNG salts/precision are engineering identifiers, not biological parameters. Worker interaction is considered first; otherwise locally sensed brood can elicit approach, bounded retrieval toward the familiar shelter, or one of inspection, arrangement, cleaning and actual feeding. Feeding is eligible only when an existing larval demand and transferable crop material exist. The equal categorical choices and retrieval priority are F. If no brood is locally sensed, a cardinal random step searches locally; no brood coordinates are revealed globally. Already-local brood can occupy attention and limit further retrieval: report this limitation rather than tune it from the holdout.

## Motor acts, placement and resources

InspectBrood is a scheduled close manipulation/inspection bout. CleanBrood is a distinct close cleaning motor bout, with no pathogen, hygiene score or invented survival effect. ArrangeBrood displaces the same brood entity by at most0.25mm toward the caregiver. TransportBrood moves the caregiver and same brood entity together by at most1mm toward its familiar shelter. It reserves the target until completion/interruption; there is no instantaneous delivery-to-nest action. Actual displacement is validated against world bounds, the authoritative geometry and loaded clearance. These are discrete swept motions; detailed mandible kinematics and body rotations remain unimplemented. Geometry's lattice can reject nonrepresentable diagonal destinations.

FeedBrood performs the existing conserved crop-to-recipient transaction. A selected attempt is not nutrition transferred. No new absorption law, caste metabolic coefficient, queen reserve mobilization or queen intake program is introduced. Existing queen basal cost remains unchanged, so new active queen bouts do not yet carry a separately calibrated activity cost (P02 open). Cleaning/inspection can be completed without nutrient movement; never equate every care act with feeding.

Target locks, pending schedules, contact memory, counters, placement and physiology use the single-writer undo journal. Target/caregiver movement, death or maturation interrupts incompatible work. A filled wall invalidates ongoing contact immediately. Reservations are released before the act's own committed moves; competing caregivers are arbitrated through existing seeded event order. Failure leaves no partial placement, transfer, reservation or observation.

## Observation definitions and query semantics

`BiologicalObservation` is a committed fact with actor, target, act, phase, bout ID, beginning, duration-known flag, outcome and actual transferred components. Phases: Opportunity, Selected, Started, Completed, Interrupted. Selection and start can differ by validation/competition. Actual scheduled starts and terminal facts define interval durations. An open bout is censored at the query boundary. History windows are half-open `[begin,end)`; intervals are clipped, including those starting before the window. No read advances time or RNG.

`behavior_window` separates known active, known inactive, unknown activity, care-program residence and legacy task residence. Approach is movement, not successful contact. Successful contact counts only completed handling/social/transfer acts. Opportunity counts are repeated local observation opportunities, not distinct social relationships. The finite inactivity bout measures modeled inactivity; it does not establish sleep. Legacy actions are instantaneous committed transactions: their physical durations remain UNKNOWN. Started/Completed at the same commit do not imply an observed zero-duration physical bout. Task residence alone never becomes activity/inactivity.

SQLite indexes the target as an event participant. History filters accept the new fact kinds. Seven appended flow metrics count selected/started/completed/interrupted acts, contact opportunities, completed active/inactive milliseconds. Duration flows are charged at completion and differ from interval-overlap queries; interrupted/open time is available in raw history/window queries. Existing metric IDs are unchanged. Dataset event exports are the specified behavioral/movement/nutrient/stage/death subset, retaining original event IDs; gaps are not deleted Core history. Full Core journals remain in checkpoints during each restore consistency check; historical committed-event counts and checkpoint sizes are recorded.

## Assay fidelity and boundaries

B01 close-worker conditions deliberately use passive, living workers as a local cue control; only queens perform care in those runs. This isolates interaction-dependent suppression and is **not** a complete reproduction of worker behavior. B02 three-worker groups perform actual care. A solid wall lacks mesh antennation; it is an impermeable-contact control, not an exact wire-mesh replication. A square50mm enclosure, seeded age/context and known shelter approximate the experimental arenas. No claim of lifetime aging, callow maturation, nest odor recognition or parent-offspring correlations is made.

The B02 worker fixture retains a paused background queen from the shared setup. Workers' care policy neither selects her as brood nor uses queen-contact inhibition, so she has no modeled influence on their decisions; this is nevertheless a mismatch from the isolated three-worker experimental unit and must not be hidden. The three-larva/three-pupa mixture is a declared fixture, not a reported fixed mixture in B02. The current comparison tests endpoint capability under approximate conditions, not exact assay equivalence.

B01 active scan endpoints include inspection/manipulation, arrangement and cleaning, excluding carrying and isolated oral feeding. The manuscript's51 scans and reversibility table's50 scans are both retained. Source sample-count inconsistencies and partial mesh observations remain explicit. B02 count/first-contact endpoints are compared with all nonretrievers censored at600s. Its deposited retrieval-rate denominator (last minus first retrieval, with special600s rows) differs from the simulator's time-to-all-six endpoint; those quantities must not be called equivalent. B02 remains an unfitted held-out endpoint. All new biological coefficients remain F; no quantitative species validation follows merely from regression tests.
