Status: frozen before implementation. Approved baseline: S5 CONDITIONAL PASS, commit `54fc129a02f1e21af93b09a0bce8d7cb0172e62b`. Branch: `codex/s6-integrated-ecology`. ## Order of work and hypotheses

1. Diagnose and validate SD-04 first. Existing nurses select from a common perception boundary but commit a fixed proportional gift bounded only by crop capacity. Concurrent donors can oversupply protein; an emerged worker does not autonomously redistribute retained surplus. Preserve the seed-43 failure and full S3 baseline before changes.
2. Establish TD-01 baseline and goals before integrated scale experiments. A spatial index still enumerates all co-located candidates for each nurse. Measure the retained S3 runner and matched new dense/dispersed contact fixtures. Optimize actual encounter selection, not by turning off physiology or deleting history.
3. Only after those mechanism checks, connect existing foraging, nutrition, development/death, locally chosen programs, excavation and S5 persistence. No per-seed task assignments or rescue feeding. A negative ecological outcome is valid evidence.

## Reversible implementation decisions

- ADR-S6-01: introduce an explicit opt-in, versioned social/ecology configuration. Historical fixtures keep their original policy for regression and ablation; the integrated S6 fixture uses the new policy. No physiological rate, development duration, pheromone coefficient or energy conversion is retuned. All new response weights, encounter budgets and intervals are ENGINEERING_FIXTURE.
- ADR-S6-02: local demand is ingredient-specific: a contacted larva's unmet retained-protein requirement after its crop, and each feeding stage's energy/hydration deficit after transferable crop contents. Actual commit recomputes current demand after earlier competing gifts and caps transfer by donor stock, recipient demand, crop room and requested work. No global colony deficit or average allocation is available to an individual. Food exchange uses the existing real inventory transaction. Adults' surplus crop protein is transferable, never tissue protein. An enrolled emerged worker can join local behavior and redistribute surplus; no new identity is created.
- ADR-S6-03: bounded contact encounters use a disposable spatial index with deterministic rank selection within local buckets, rather than enumerating a crowded bucket. The index is derived from actual identity/position/life state, updates through rollback journals and rebuilds on restore. Sampling uses a private seed/individual/decision stream, without submission-order dependence or a low-ID preference. Exact distance, colony/stage and authoritative soil barriers qualify candidates; every action rechecks contact at commit. Observer all-neighbor queries may remain exhaustive; routine individual policies must use the bounded API.
- ADR-S6-04: an explicitly enrolled individual chooses Rest/Nurse/Forage/Excavate using only its reserves/cargo, physical nest contact, sampled local nestmates and reachable soil. Use a private deterministic decision stream and a minimum program duration. Foraging and excavation complete experienced return journeys before routine task switching; no global path/food oracle and no cancellation that teleports cargo. Manual pause/switch remains available as an engineering control. Brood enrollment is dormant until actual adult emergence; death cancels every owned program and automatic wake.
- ADR-S6-05: preserve the existing planar foraging substrate and real 3D excavation. The integration fixture places the known nest depot/brood alcove where that plane enters a parameterized 3D cavity. Workers can leave the plane while excavating and must return through actual clearance-qualified steps before changing to planar foraging. Permit geometry-valid off-plane positions for brood and inactive foragers in the explicitly mixed fixture; retain surface constraints while the foraging program is active. Historical validation distinguishes experienced planar foraging from other real movement and still validates every 3D barrier at the event's time. This is not general underground food search, brood transport or mature nest morphology.
- ADR-S6-06: planned checkpoint schema 7 / physics 8; append social configuration, per-individual autonomous ownership/counters and any new evidence state. Keep fixed legacy queue/fact records where possible. Reject older versions explicitly, retain unchanged historical data and document schema-only size deltas in regression verifiers. Pure reads do not consume RNG/time. Every mutation, index update, switch and scheduler change uses the single-writer rollback path.
- ADR-S6-07: use existing S5 committed histories and 60 defined metrics to reconcile integration outcomes. New real autonomy decisions/enrollments have explicit facts and source IDs; they are not successful future actions. Counts and residuals are computed from actual state/events, including dead retained inventory. Save/recover remains an application concern. No history deletion, global food sharing, forced survival or branch-specific biological tuning.

## Integrated experiment

Engineering fixture population follows document 21: one queen, 32 workers, four eggs, six larvae and two pupae, with persistent IDs. Use unchanged physiology defaults: 1 J initial body energy and 1,000 ug hydration per identity, empty crops; the two seeded pupae additionally start with the declared 100 ug retained tissue protein each. Reference external food ingredients total 1,000 ug carbohydrate, 2,000 ug protein and 5,500 ug water plus 500 ug other mass, split into the two declared sources. No food coordinates enter individual state except locally discovered bounded experience. No fixed adult roles are assigned.

Frozen spatial fixture: geometry quantum 0.0005 m, origin (-0.008,-0.006,-0.006) m, InitialSolid box [0,32)x[0,24)x[0,24) in quantum coordinates. Seed chamber [12,20)x[8,16)x[8,16) and horizontal entrance passage [0,32)x[10,14)x[10,14). The known physical depot is (0,0,0) m. All initial identities start there as a dense contact fixture; off-plane brood/contact tests are separate. The foraging plane has origin (-0.03,0,-0.03) m, 7x7 cells, spacing 0.01 m; other S2.2 behavior settings are unchanged. Food at (0.02,0,0.01) m contains (1000,0,3000,0) ug; food at (-0.02,0,-0.01) m contains (0,2000,2500,500) ug. Mirroring negates X. The sealed control omits the passage and surrounds the chamber with non-diggable rock within InitialSolid; food quantities/positions and individuals remain matched. Export all boxes and input values with each matrix.

Frozen automatic-program defaults: reconsider every 5,000 ms, minimum program duration 10,000 ms, encounter budget 16. Rest weight 4 and legal planar Forage weight 8 represent weak local activity. Nurse weight 12 requires contacted demand and available crop/nest nutrients; Excavate weight 4 requires sampled local crowding (at least two other living nestmates) and soil within the existing geometry reach. Own urgent energy/water deficits prioritize physically available food intake. No worker reads global population/task ratios, brood counts or source positions. These weights are engineering hypotheses and are not adjusted after seeing population outcomes.

Use all seeds 1..32 and both mirrored layouts, 600 simulated seconds, four matched resource/spatial treatments: supplied, protein-absent (replace with other mass), all-food-absent, and a geometrically sealed resource access control with the original food stock. Every primary case runs in three modes: normal 1 s observation steps, accelerated large AdvanceTo calls, and repeated S5 save/reload every 60 s. Require canonical continuation equality at common boundaries; compare the same-time metrics and factual history. Runtime speed is host scheduling, not a biology parameter. The save schedule and all retries are explicit.

Record initial/end population by stage/caste, each identity's transitions/death/cause, discovery/collection/delivery, actual nutrient exchange and absorption, crop/body/dead inventories, local automatic task transitions and residence time, 3D movement/digs/soil transport/deposition, void/material history, queues/RNG, storage sizes and all conservation residuals. ## Biological interpretation references

[Greenwald, Baltiansky and Feinerman (2018)](https://elifesciences.org/articles/31730) measured local crop-mediated food regulation in **Camponotus sanctus**, not Lasius niger. It supports studying local transfer feedback; it does not calibrate an exact protein-deficit sensor or this model's coefficients. [Lasius niger food-dissemination research](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6821819/) studies heterogeneous trophallactic networks under a sucrose/starvation protocol. Neither source validates an equal global allocation or our larval protein rule. Ingredient-specific perceived demand and bounded encounters remain explicit ENGINEERING_FIXTURE abstractions, subject to later species-qualified experiments.
