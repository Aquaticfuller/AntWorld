# AntWorld game design (historical v0.4)

## Vision and pillars

A low-operation, persistent ant ecosystem for observation over days, years and generations. The player is a gentle caretaker. There are no mandatory quest lines, fixed victory conditions or scheduled enemy waves. Colonies may grow, migrate, decline, establish descendants or disappear while the world continues.

The three pillars are ecology emerging from local individual interaction, evidence-based data and life histories, and a persistent AI Game Master for observation, analysis, companionship and authorized tools.

## Worlds and starting conditions

- An open evolving natural world, not only a decorative terrarium or level-unlock map.
- Initial habitat: temperate grassland/woodland edge; gardens, forests, dry grasslands, deserts and other habitats can follow.
- First complete world: 24 x 24 meters (576 square meters), expandable chunks, multiple autonomous colonies. A 2 meter chunk is a candidate.
- Lasius niger first; later species differ in diet, founding, excavation, reproduction and ecological interaction.
- Choose a newly mated queen, young colony with few workers (default), or mature colony.
- Ants receive the finest individual detail. Plants, aphids, insects and predators have autonomous population/resource dynamics without requiring equal precision.

## Experience over time

Seconds/minutes: follow an individual walking, contacting, carrying, sharing food, caring for brood, digging, fighting or resting. Days/months: observe routes, development, task allocation, nest growth, weather and ecology. Years/generations: mating flights, descendant colonies, competition, succession and colony lineages.

Offline behavior is configurable: pause, limited evolution or continued evolution, with a return summary. Without a background service, legal catch-up can run on restart. Pause and acceleration remain available.

## Player interactions

Zoom and rotate the surface; view transparent underground spaces or arbitrary sections; follow any individual. Inspect records, colony panels, time series, trophallaxis networks, pheromone/temperature/moisture maps and weather. Favorite individuals or allow limited automatic deep tracking with notification thresholds.

Provide food, water, shelter and permitted environmental experiments. Ants do not accept direct work orders. Text/voice requests to the GM invoke the same validated player tools for queries, camera movement, observations and legal environmental changes. Independent worlds share one GM while retaining separate saves, time and ID namespaces.

## Visual direction

Natural, refined, semi-realistic miniature ecology with clear distant views and convincing macro detail. Do not anthropomorphize ant anatomy. Underground tunnels can slope, bend, branch and cross at different elevations without connecting.

2.5D is a camera style, not a reduction of physical space. Surface, transparency, sections and individual-following views observe the same world. UI is restrained with optional species, age, pheromone, microclimate and activity overlays. Quiet, lightweight narration and full interaction modes support both text and voice without forced camera takeover.

## Ecological loop

Climate/soil -> plants/prey -> resources -> worker foraging -> local food exchange/brood care -> development/new workers -> demand, expansion and competition. Death, waste, weather and neighboring colonies feed back into the system.

Environmental events primarily arise from simulation. GM proposals cannot manipulate outcomes merely for a better story. Natural, player and GM interventions have explicit provenance.

## Statistics, history and long-term interest

Every instantiated ant retains identity, stages, birth, work summaries, major events and death/cause. Selected ants have finer trajectories and interaction history; tracking grants no biological advantage. Population, age structure, resources, foraging, mortality, nest volume, territory, competition and lineage support colony comparison.

Provide time series, heatmaps, life timelines, nest-section analysis and event navigation. Playback distinguishes actual records from interpolation or illustrative reconstruction. Missing details cannot be invented as fact.

Long-term interest comes from species differences, seasons, individual lives, neighboring colonies, resource dynamics, reproduction and shared GM/player history. Relevant notifications include a favorite ant's death, unusual migration, changing colony balance or observed effects after intervention; every factual claim needs evidence.
