# S6.3.1 verification, representation and metrics

## Representation and scientific scope

Protocol 46's sole correction is `ReproductionConfig::maintenance_horizon`. Zero uses legacy AWCKPT9 schema 9 / physics 11, including unchanged enrollment facts. Any positive horizon uses AWCKPTA schema 10 / physics 12, storing eight extra bytes per queen reproduction record. The actual horizon in milliseconds is bound to that queen's ReproductionEnabled fact payload. Old saves retain old behavior; no implicit migration occurs. Mixed zero/positive queens use schema 10 and preserve each individual's contract.

Before committing an egg, current body energy must exceed egg energy plus reproductive dissipation plus basal rate times horizon. Body water must exceed egg water plus floor((water-loss rate times horizon + current integer remainder) / water-loss period). The prior protein funding and geometric clearance rules remain. Checked arithmetic rejects overflowing configurations before mutation. This local computation has constant cost per attempt. Attempt timing and private random streams are unchanged. No worker response, priority, signaling threshold or allocation optimization is introduced.

The optional host observer adds copied scalar records only while its thread-local scope exists. The experiment publishes records after successful AdvanceTo and discards them on failure. Context 0 is nutrition selection, 1 autonomy selection and 2 actual depot-withdrawal perception. A zero sampled-queen value with zero candidates means no contact sample was taken, not that an exhaustive search found no queen. `reachable_queen` is an observer-only physical counterfactual, not information supplied to the worker. Multiple contexts can describe related decisions, so report them separately rather than claiming unique social contacts.

## Account and outcome definitions

Queen accounts start at 1.5e9 nJ body energy, 1600 ug body water and crop (200 carbohydrate,100 protein,200 water) ug in the frozen fixtures. Independent reconstruction applies elapsed basal metabolism and exact water remainder; actual PhysiologyUpdated absorption; NutritionTransferred gifts; AntBorn exports and 1e7 nJ reproductive dissipation. The queen's worker behavior remains Paused, which still consumes basal resources. No metabolic cost is inferred from wall time.

Every observation is joined to the corresponding committed scheduled-source fact. Compare reconstructed energy, hydration, all crop components and alive state with actual observer copies, and with every daily queen query. `queen_events.csv` records selected actual queen-relevant facts and independently reconstructed pre/post states; it is not the whole native journal. Event 34 precedes the corresponding event 35 in the journal: the independent account assigns the debit to the explicit AntBorn evidence. No worker perception occurs between those two facts. `post_maintenance_hours` equals post-fact body energy divided by basal rate and 3,600,000 ms/hour. Unsafe laying means the remaining body energy or water cannot strictly cover the next absorption time; it does not count crop carbohydrate as usable before absorption.

Survival is actual energy/dehydration death time or administrative right censoring at the planned endpoint. Capacity censoring is separate. Restricted mean survival is the mean observed time up to the predeclared endpoint when no capacity censoring occurs. Report all seeds and paired changes, percentile bootstrap 4096 resamples with seed 63101. The six seed IDs are the resampling unit within a matched condition; aggregated worker/supply conditions must retain their matched seed cluster. Report exact successes with a Wilson 95% interval as a descriptive binomial interval, without claiming independent biological colonies or species calibration.

At queen death, replay resource facts to measure depot stock, uncollected food, carried food and living-worker crops at that exact journal position. Finite unreleased reservoirs and physically accessible nutrients are distinct: reserve stock is not yet usable food. Last-48-hour observer summaries distinguish physical opportunities, actual sample inclusion, matching donor ingredients, intended recipients and completed transfers. These are counts, not unobserved feeding rates.
