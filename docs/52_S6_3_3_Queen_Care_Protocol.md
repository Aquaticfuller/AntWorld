## Model contract frozen for implementation

Use an explicitly enabled versioned care contract; disabled worlds must retain exact old numerical behavior, events and checkpoint format. Minimal species profile: local sensing/movement, care-bout/decision durations, care propensity and finite recent-worker-contact modulation. Individual profile: queen founding/established context or worker care capability, familiar shelter and finite contact memory. Environment profile: recorded assay conditions only. Context labels do not confer global worker knowledge or an age-dependent fitted rate.

Queen care is a dedicated capability, never worker Forage/Excavate/autonomy. Locally encountered brood can elicit approach, inspection/contact, arrangement, cleaning, transport or feeding. Arrangement changes position locally; transport moves the caregiver and the same brood identity through validated space; cleaning is a committed motor act with no invented pathogen/health benefit. Feeding uses the existing conserved crop transaction and actual capacity/demand. No flight-muscle mobilization, new assimilation or energy-rate changes. Lack of transferable reserves can prevent feeding.

Only completed close interactions with living workers update a bounded, fading individual social cue. A nearby candidate is an opportunity, not an interaction. No colony WorkerCount branch. Worker removal is physical separation, not death or deletion. Brood targets and carried identity are reserved transactionally; movement, death, maturation, cancellation or geometry blockage invalidate ongoing acts. Simultaneous choices use a common boundary and seeded arbitration, not submission order or smallest ID.

Observe selection, start, completion and interruption separately. Finite modeled acts have measured simulated start/end timestamps; older instantaneous transactions retain UNKNOWN physical duration. Program residence is not active time. Idle intervals of the new program are explicit; unobserved legacy time must not become inactivity. Contacts, feeding attempts and transferred mass are distinct. Queries cannot alter RNG/time. Persist all continuation state and committed evidence, including interrupted/open bouts and interval censoring.
