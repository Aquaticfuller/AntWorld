# S3 physiology, nutrition and continuous identity

Status: protocol frozen before implementation. Baseline: approved S2.2 commit 18b0c9f5d5b5d60504a48ac193dce53c59f001d0. Authorized branch: codex/s3-physiology. S4 is excluded. All numeric biological inputs below are ENGINEERING_FIXTURE, not Lasius niger calibration.

## Scope and reversible decisions

- ADR-S3-01: opt-in per-individual physiology preserves historical S0-S2.2 fixtures. Explicit initialization at unexecuted time zero records supplied crop, body water, tissue protein and energy as fixture inputs. New individuals still use existing lifetime AntId; no replacement at emergence. Egg laying, mating, disease, climate feedback and brood relocation are outside this stage.
- ADR-S3-02: carbohydrate, protein, water and other mass remain integer micrograms. Carried parcels, physical nest stock and individual crop are distinct. Crop ingestion removes actual cargo/nest inventory; contact-qualified trophallaxis transfers donor crop to recipient crop with capacity and stage checks. Eggs and pupae cannot feed. Adult queens may receive but do not execute worker tasks.
- ADR-S3-03: body energy is exposed in joules and stored as integer nanojoules to make integration/ledger arithmetic exact. Basal and active rates are integer nanojoules per millisecond. Water loss is integer micrograms per second with a persistent remainder. Separate explicit sinks record oxidized carbohydrate, dissipated energy and lost water; retained tissue protein and dead inventories are not discarded.
- ADR-S3-04: continuous expenditure is analytic between discontinuities. Nutrient absorption occurs at fixed, persisted pulse boundaries (default 1000 ms). Crop carbohydrate becomes energy with an explicit yield; water enters body water, larval protein enters retained tissue. Absorption is not colony-wide averaging. No new global clock scan or State copy. Per-individual next events stop at the earliest absorption, depletion or stage threshold. Energy/water depletion wins ties with feeding/actions. Timeline rounding is at most one millisecond; host AdvanceTo partitioning must be exact. Pulse-size sensitivity is distinct from this rounding guarantee.
- ADR-S3-05: fixture development durations are 30/60/30 seconds for egg/larva/pupa. Larval progress requires a stated retained protein threshold and positive energy/water. Egg/pupa use seeded or retained reserves. Adult emergence changes only stage and resets stage progress; death is terminal, once, with a reason. No fixed lifespan timer.
- ADR-S3-06: individual behavior can be activated, paused or switched at a committed boundary. Pause cancels owned behavior work but preserves crop, experienced route/memory and physiology; it is not world-time pause. Rest and local nursing remain individual programs. Nursing observes only contacting nestmates and physical nest stock, selecting ties from a private deterministic stream, not proposal order. Foraging retains S2.2 local policy. Switching cancels incompatible pending work atomically and reschedules strictly later; runtime commands are recorded facts, not successful future actions.
- ADR-S3-07: schema/physics increase to 4/6. Earlier saves are explicitly rejected; historical reports/raw data are immutable. Add complete physiology, scheduler ownership, remainders, budgets and behavior state to the checkpoint. Restore validates stocks, energy, identity, evidence and owned queues before swapping worlds. Extend the existing undo journal rather than copying world state.

## Frozen verification protocol

Retain all 56 existing CTest registrations. New tests cover initialization and invalid values; exact energy/water balance; N2 donor loss/recipient gain, zero/full capacity, partial transfer, distance/colony/stage/death and competing donors; cargo/nest ingestion; N1 sufficient/limited protein; all three automatic stage transitions and unchanged identity; depletion before same-time actions; behavior pause/resume/switch with route preservation; local autonomous nursing and deterministic proposal-order independence; partitioned advancement; checkpoint continuation/corruption; allocation and host-budget failure rollback.

Multi-seed matrix uses every seed 1..64, without filtering:

1. Document-21 N1: four larvae and two contacting nursing adults, matched sufficient versus zero-protein supply, 180-second observation. Record actual transfer/intake, each stage transition, final stages, deaths, all nutrient/energy budgets and remainder errors. Initial reserve differences between larvae are fixed-seed fixture variation, shared across treatments.
2. Document-21 N2: matched receiver crop capacities 0/100/1000 ug and supplied/empty donors, multiple competing proposals in forward/reverse submission order. Record each requested/actual transfer and both final inventories. No event is counted as an actual transfer when rejected.
3. Development: seeded eggs followed through larva, pupa and adult under adequate nutrition; retain all original IDs, transition times and failures.
4. Sensitivity: absorption periods 250/1000/2000 ms, matched initial conditions and seeds. Report timing/energy and nutrition effects without selecting the best parameter. Compare one large AdvanceTo against irregular partitions for exact continuation at each parameter value.

Raw CSV includes seed, treatment, pulse size, initial inputs, outputs, event counts, transfer totals and accounting errors; keep failures and non-emergers. Report per-condition means, seed-cluster 95% intervals (fixed bootstrap seed 3003, 4096 resamples), and paired differences. Plots show actual nutrition/development and parameter sensitivity, not inferred species fitness. Deterministic mechanism assertions remain primary; no requirement to force a positive colony foraging effect.
