## Audit and hypotheses

The authoritative contracts remain documents 01, 04, 16-18, 22, 31-40. Preserve all 137 tests, old raw observations and negative findings. S6.2's controlled dense advance ratio 5.4383 passes its original <=6 gate; S6.1's 24.229 anomaly remains unexplained. SD-04 is open, not a transaction defect established by that review.

Current code has no oviposition or endogenous initialization. Physiology initialization is restricted to time zero and treats all initial stores as external inputs. Fixed short experiment periods, integer water loss per second, and 30/60/30-second development are not a multi-month biological model. Repeated initialization scans all physiology and the pending queue (TD-08). Crop protein has no adult use or return path. Foraging immediately resumes after delivery; an infrequent autonomy wake can miss a nest visit. Excavation eligibility requires neighbors and accessible soil but no adequate-space inhibition. Activity statistics measure program ownership, not continuous physical labor. No validated inactivity percentage or excavation shape follows from these rules.

H1: explicit maternal mass/energy transfers can fund repeated birth without new external inputs. H2: finite patch reserves with an exogenous availability cycle permit replenishment without infinite food. H3: completion-triggered recovery and local demand/space inhibition reduce unneeded program ownership; this is not a calibrated activity budget. H4: returning unused worker crop protein to a physically contacted depot can enable later local use, with potentially unfavorable scarcity allocation trade-offs. H5: cached conservative registration budgets and indexed pending lifecycle counts remove repeated full scans without changing legacy decisions or facts.

## Frozen implementation contracts

- Opt-in long-term configuration. Legacy worlds retain schema 8 / physics 9 and canonical semantics; long-term worlds use explicitly distinct schema 9 / physics 10 with strict validation. No unversioned ecological changes. Derived caches are reconstructed and validated on restore.
- Queen-local reproduction wakes: living adult queen, positive physiological reserves, real crop protein and body hydration/energy fund one egg. Eligibility is individual, never population based. Mother pays the exact initial offspring tissue, hydration and energy plus an explicit dissipative cost. Birth allocates one never-reused identity at the queen's reachable position; it is not an external seed. Every transition/death retains this identity. Child inventories and maternal debits are auditable. No mating or reproductive caste production.
- Finite patch reservoir: all nutrient mass is introduced explicitly at setup, partitioned between accessible food and inaccessible reserve. A fixed periodic release transfers the componentwise minimum of remaining reserve and declared allowance. Environment alternates productive and low-productivity intervals independent of ant needs; no global food coordinates enter behavior. No photosynthesis, weather PDE, new species or claim of a complete ecosystem resource cycle.
- Physiology uses the existing exact piecewise integrator and integer sinks. A dimensioned water-loss period permits sub-microgram-per-second rates without floating drift. Long pulse/decision bounds are host policies, not species constants. Eggs/pupae do not receive trophallaxis; larvae and adult queens have explicit local ingredient needs. No forced emergence or death timer.
- A completed or exhausted finite-memory trip notifies that individual's scheduler at its actual depot return. One recovery interval follows completion, then local task selection resumes. This is a testable refractory-rule assumption, not colony scheduling. Existing physical actions, resource costs, collisions and histories remain intact.
- Demand feedback is bounded local contact sampling plus physically contacted depot inventory and local body state. Digging is inhibited when the locally reachable empty sites suffice for sampled nearby bodies; no global desired nest or population target. Rectangular geometry and six-axis approximation remain TD-04.
- Optional crop recycling returns only actual unused adult-worker protein to the contacted depot. Withdrawal is ingredient-aware using self demand and encountered recipient demand; no global mean, completion optimization or hidden nutrition. Uniform eligible recipient choice remains reproducible and ID unbiased.
- TD-08 optimization only removes redundant validation work. Preserve single-writer undo, arithmetic bounds, forced-lifecycle conflict detection, event history and failure rollback.

## Frozen engineering fixture (not Lasius niger calibration)

Every numeric biological assumption here and in the implementation is ENGINEERING_FIXTURE. One game day is exactly 86,400,000 ms; 90 days means 7,776,000,000 ms, not renamed short experiments. Primary physiology: 6-hour absorption/behavior/autonomy periods, 1-hour water-loss period at 1 ug/period, basal/active 1/1 nJ/ms, 2 J capacity, 1.5 J initial adult energy, 1600 ug initial hydration, 1000 ug crop capacity, initial adult crop 200/100/200/0 ug (carbohydrate/protein/water/other), 100 ug larval tissue requirement, original absorption and transfer ceilings. Egg/larva/pupa durations 2/3/2 days. Reproduction opportunity every 2 days costs 20 ug crop protein, 200 ug body water, 0.5 J transferred offspring energy and 0.01 J dissipated energy. Adult queen protein target is the next egg's 20 ug cost. Offspring uses the same physiology except no initial external input. No parameter will be retuned to make survival or growth positive.

Small reachable surface and existing parametric chamber/passage geometry, one initial queen, no initially seeded brood. Workers start with autonomy. Trails disabled in long experiments to isolate this stage and avoid conflating unresolved social recruitment; route memory remains finite. Food is discovered locally. Initial accessible balanced/scarce food is 4000/500/4000/0 ug. Finite reserve per initial worker: 20000/2000/5000/0 ug balanced, 20000/50/5000/0 scarce. Empty condition has no edible stock. Daily release ceiling 4000/400/2000/0 ug; constant environment 100%, cyclic environment alternating 15-day 100% and 0% intervals. Explicit setup nutrients in adults remain present even in empty conditions. Use the inherited motion/geometry constants; do not calibrate them using outcomes.

## Primary literature and limits of inference

[Claustral queen feeding experiment](https://academic.oup.com/biolinnean/article/142/4/397/7342048) supports resource-constrained founding, not the numerical egg budget above. [Colony macronutrient investment](https://doi.org/10.1002/ece3.6520) reports growth/survival trade-offs rather than a single optimal nutrient policy. [Lasius niger food dissemination](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41598-019-52019-6) supports local exchange heterogeneity; its starved, queenless/broodless sucrose experiment cannot calibrate protein-to-brood or queen allocation. These are qualitative constraints. Queen-only claustral nursing, body-protein remobilization, age-dependent mortality, diapause, real activity costs and environmental physiology remain unvalidated or unimplemented and must be reported.
