# Review-only model correction roadmap

No proposal below is implemented or implicitly authorized. Preserve the current Core, all negative datasets and the local maternal reserve guard. Its two-day horizon remains F / `ENGINEERING_FIXTURE`. The P01–P10 identifiers deliberately continue [S6.3.2 proposals](MODEL_CORRECTION_PROPOSALS.md); that older document uses historical A–D grades. This document and the atlas use A–F. A later implementation needs an explicit scoped approval, its own frozen control protocol and unchanged-model baseline.

## Candidate mathematical forms, not fitted values

**Stage-specific development.** A simple candidate inside a measured thermal window is progress `dz_s/dt = r_s(T, population, nutrition)` with stage-specific rate, not one universal Q10. B06's published lower-threshold/degree-day coefficients are source fits, not new estimates here. A piecewise-linear or monotone rate can be compared with that published form after defining egg, larval and prepupal/pupal endpoints. Outside observed temperatures the prediction is UNKNOWN; negative rates must not masquerade as diapause. Larval growth and nutritional sufficiency require their own continuous state, not forced completion. Validation must hold out populations/temperatures and keep daily interval censoring and colony nesting.

**Metabolism.** `P = a(caste,stage,T) * mass^b + measured_activity_cost` is a candidate structure. N27's workers(a1.914,b0.6492) and queens(a0.234,b1.039), with mass in mg and oxygen in uL/h, are mixed-species published fits with device/pooling limitations. They are not approved black-garden-ant coefficients. Start with matched raw mass/oxygen data, clear resting versus basal definitions, and declared substrate-dependent energy equivalents. A mass exponent cannot be identified from worker pools without controlling pool size and individual masses. Distinguish usable stores, structural tissue, crop, assimilated energy and dissipated energy; conservation remains mandatory.

**Local task transitions.** A competing-hazard model `Pr(change within dt)=1-exp(-sum(lambda_j)*dt)` can separate decision-clock resolution from a biological bout duration. Each `lambda_j` may depend on current individual reserves, observed local encounters, recent acts and verified age/role state. Nonnegative rates, finite memory and observable cues are required. Do not add a colony labor quota or encode a desired inactive percentage. Data currently do not identify these hazards; the weighted4/8/12/4 policy remains an engineering baseline.

**Food ingestion and exchange.** N14 publishes a stopping-volume logistic `F(V)=1/(1+exp(h*(V-Vc)))`. Its h and Vc depend on the endpoint/condition and describe ingestion, not absorption or compulsory maximum crop load. Compare a simple satiation hypothesis only in that assay; use N15 or held-out colonies independently. N01 contact duration distributions need observational definitions, correction of suspect printed hazard intervals, and measured mass/duration before assigning flow rates. Demand signals must be locally observable proxies, not a global nutrition allocator. Selective partial feeding can trade completion against distribution; maximum emergence is not an objective function.

**Queen allocation.** A local laying propensity bounded by actual maternal stores and physiological reproductive state is a candidate, with brood/worker encounters allowed to affect the propensity only under an explicit founding-state hypothesis. B01's opposite larval effects in founding and established queens rule out an unconditional universal brood multiplier. Preserve real transfers into eggs and dissipated costs. Unknown egg composition, ovarian kinetics and founding reserve mobilization prevent a numerical cost function now. No survival floor, fixed lifespan protection or food injection is allowed.

**Environment and mortality.** Stage-specific water and energy balances may inform competing death hazards after direct measurement; dry survival alone cannot identify water-loss rate independently of initial hydration. Thermal CTmin,24h lethal exposure and development thresholds are different quantities. N10's single-colony cohorts and N07's ambiguous censor/status codes limit inferential precision. No universal ant dormancy coefficient is justified. Temperature, RH and soil water potential need distinct physical inputs before a response function can be validated.

**Trails and construction.** Start with source-compatible deposited amount, spatial detection and observed turns; a physical liquid deposit volume is not an active-chemical concentration. Use simple local cue-response laws and conditional marking with separate calibration and follow-up validation. No directional chemical arrows or global food coordinates. Construction candidates should use observable local occupancy, substrate, loaded-body clearance and brood microclimate only after those states exist; neither a prescribed nest shape nor a colony target volume is evidence-based.

## Approval and acceptance sequence

First review this evidence, contradictions and the20-design catalog. Then select a minimal independently testable proposal and freeze its data split, measurement contract, parameter ranges, uncertainties and retained negative controls. New fits must report identifiability and colony-level uncertainty, not only simulator-seed variability. A calibration dataset cannot become an independent validation dataset by rerunning the same fitted conditions. Parameters lacking matching data stay F; extrapolation remains explicitly C/D/E as appropriate.

Engineering acceptance preserves exact resource/energy/soil ledgers, stable identity, rollback, event order, checkpoint/RNG continuation, raw history and performance semantics. Biological acceptance separately tests the matched endpoint and domain. Performance remains the frozen <=6x workload gate; no reduced actions, contact frequency or history can improve it. S6.4 readiness is not granted by this roadmap, and no next implementation stage starts automatically.
