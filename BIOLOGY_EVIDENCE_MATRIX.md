# Biological mechanism audit: S6.3.2

Baseline: `accdb4c7037c792ac55e45277c9a16379828b09c`. No Core rule or parameter is changed in this audit. References B01-B22 resolve in [the bibliography](BIOLOGY_REFERENCES.md), which records species, experimental conditions, access and precise locators. The source anchors below identify functions and conditions, not just file names.

Evidence A means an original Lasius niger experiment; B means another Lasius species; C means another ant or general physiology; D means an unsupported engineering hypothesis. A qualitative mechanism does **not** calibrate its implementation. Every current biological number remains D / `ENGINEERING_FIXTURE`. Engineering guarantees (local queries, rollback, stable identity) are not biological evidence. Absence of a mechanism is distinguished from a contradiction, and source-derived predictions are distinguished from measured probes.

## Decision

Direct support exists for founding care/plastic specialization, condition-sensitive reproduction, nutritional constraints, local trophallaxis, route learning, pheromone response and context-dependent excavation. Continuous software identity is an engineering choice. These findings do not support the full current transition system. Q01 is a clear conflict; Q03/F11 omit demonstrated conditional responses; L06 has a large time-scale mismatch. S03, S07, F07-F09 and X01-X03 are specific uncalibrated engineering rules, not biology established by a citation.

The main prerequisites for S6.4 biological readiness are P01-P05 plus explicit acceptance of limited movement/task/geometry scope. Parameter adoption and biological corrections require review of [the frozen proposals](MODEL_CORRECTION_PROPOSALS.md). Neither all inherited tests passing nor a longer-lived queen closes these findings.
