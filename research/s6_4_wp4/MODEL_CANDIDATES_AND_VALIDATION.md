# WP-4 candidate mechanisms and independent validation

## Reference and candidate models

Retain the accepted reference with its local reproductive guard, discrete uptake, fixed debit and fixed stage clocks. Use its original budgets, zero-dose attempts, depleted mothers and premature egg/larval deaths in every comparison. The reference estimates behavior of the current software under an explicit fixture. It has no approved domain for predicting *Lasius niger* founder survival.

| Candidate | Minimum falsifiable change | Identification data and failure criterion |
|---|---|---|
| RM-04 effective maternal reserve | Add one finite, measured net maternal reserve pathway with separate mass/water debits, mobilization and energy yield. Initial reserve input and release must appear as a closed maternal–egg–offspring ledger. Keep conditional laying guard and the exact existing event order as a reference arm. | Separately measure intake, resting exchange, usable tissue reserve loss and export at known egg ages. Test whether a measured reserve state predicts time to next lay and stage-aligned reserve loss, at matched worker access and diet. Reject if the fit depends on gross wet body mass as available energy or changes to both debit and resting cost that cannot be distinguished. |
| RM-04 conditional oviposition | Once material flow is identified, compare the existing hard local gate with a laying hazard conditioned on observed maternal reserve and local brood state. A longer scalar horizon alone is a separate sensitivity control. No worker-count trigger is needed. | Use recorded inter-lay intervals, known handling and local brood exposure, including empty scans. Estimate one reserve effect and prespecified interaction with founding stage only if data support both. Reject if the model fits final egg stock by trading laying against unobserved loss, or reverses a matched intervention effect outside its uncertainty. |
| RM-05 stage-specific viability | First measure an egg energy and water budget over age. Compare a simple bounded stage-specific rate with the inherited adult-derived rate at one admitted temperature, with separate assigned and observed death categories. Extend the measured stage budget only where data identify it. | Follow known-age siblings through hatch and record interval-censored loss or death. Use parallel age-matched destructive replicates for wet/dry mass, chemical reserve and respiration. Reject if hatching improves solely because the cost is reduced without measured reserve or expenditure support, or if the reference still fits all intermediate measurements as well. |
| RM-05 condition-specific development | After confirming budget feasibility, estimate a clock or integrated progress from per-stage age intervals. Compare one condition-specific clock first. A simple temperature response is a separate claim restricted to tested regimes. Compare a discrete larval tissue gate with a bounded dose-dependent progress rule only after dose and growth are measured. | Use full histograms or interval likelihoods under temperature, known food input and instar. Record competing death and arrested development as separate outcomes. The B06 first-observed stage counts can constrain the equivalent founder-level observation. They cannot supply tagged egg trajectories. Reject if matching duration among survivors worsens the observed death or non-completion distribution. |

The first proposed authorization is a **separate matched-condition maternal and embryo feasibility study**, with implementation limited to a separate opt-in assay after the measurements resolve candidate state, units and rates. No candidate should be adopted while it would require an unconstrained energy increase, a forced transition, a reserve-free egg debit or a fitted worker survival target. Preserve BR-02 and BR-05 dependencies. Absolute uptake, demand and expenditure can compensate for one another in colony outputs.

## Prospective protocol and authorization gates

1. Capture starting denominators, verified species and source colony or independent collection cluster. Define first-stage age zero as laid egg time or its observation interval, not collection time. Record mated/foundress status, worker access, brood present, environmental temperature/RH over time, water access, known delivered/consumed food, and all deaths, missing records and treatment changes. Keep first-observed offspring cohorts separate from identity-resolved offspring.
2. For RM-04 use a restricted founding phase with no workers, then a distinct treatment with documented worker access. Collect video-coded nest contact and positive/zero gifts with paired dose, queen time budgets, resting gas exchange, and before/after laying mass with assay-specific repeated measures. Parallel destructive cohorts supply tissue chemistry. Derive exchange-to-energy only with measured or declared substrate/RQ bounds and independent uncertainty for water and energy.
3. For RM-05 record both foundress-level first-stage appearance and identity-resolved cohort survival. Observe a controlled warm regime with standardized hydration, then a second temperature only if response is an explicit claim. Randomize chambers and record realized temperatures. Sham handling and egg/hatch counting errors require their own coded outcomes. Record false-negative detection, stage changes, blocked or delayed progress, dead or missing bodies and terminal observation times. Track delivery, measured assimilation, body composition and first-worker status.
4. Separate development and holdout by natal colony or identifiable independent collection block, with temperature, diet and worker-access strata represented. Collection-day grouping is a conservative alternative if natal colony is unknowable. Never assign individual eggs, repeated scans or treatment splits of the same source colony to both sets. Holdout identities and numerical outcomes stay masked until candidate and thresholds are signed off. The B01/B03/B06/B07 development sources, reused cohorts, inspected spreadsheets and already published summaries cannot become new held-out data. A distinct source can qualify for a limited endpoint only after independence and condition match are checked.
5. Analyze maternal intake/oxidation and before/after reserve change, egg debit and inter-lay interval, and stage-specific cumulative incidence of completion and death. Preserve observation intervals and report separately the fate-unknown counts. For grouped observations use source-cluster bootstrap and a sensitivity analysis for uncertain fate. A known terminal non-event at the observation horizon is right-censored. A disappearance does not certify death. Score event probabilities and distributions separately from conditional completion time.
6. Freeze margins from independent repeatability, instrument error and the smallest scientifically meaningful discrepancy before unmasking. Agree on an estimand-specific interval and uncertainty method in advance, with biological sample size based on independent units. If viable output passes but material flows, stage clocks or failure patterns fail the prespecified margin, keep the mechanism blocked. If bounds are too wide, classify as insufficient rather than equivalent. Engineering separately requires budget closure, unchanged lineage/phase, deterministic observer-off and restart evidence, immutable historical controls and controlled performance.

No WP-4 cohort supplies those independent, matched measurements. The next request can authorize data acquisition and a bounded candidate assay. Adoption of a new official default remains a separate scientific and code decision.
