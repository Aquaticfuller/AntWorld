# WP-4 data requests for independent review

The following drafts are ready for review. No outreach was sent.

## B06 recording sheets

**Subject** Data request for first-brood development in *Lasius niger* (2004)

Dear members of the Kipyatkov and Lopatina research group or collection archive,

I am reviewing the *Lasius niger* first-brood study published in 2004 (DOI 10.1023/B:JOEY.0000033808.45455.75) for a research simulator. We would like to reproduce the original founder-level endpoints while keeping them separate from any later independently collected validation cohort.

Are anonymized founder-level census sheets or an electronic table available for the Borisovka and St. Petersburg 1994 regimes or the Novosibirsk 1987 regimes? We would use the population and queen code, assigned and recorded temperatures, collection date, census dates and stage counts, and the record of deaths, missing visits, developmental arrest and the reason follow-up ended. If individual eggs or identifiable cohorts were followed, could you describe the tracking method and whether age, disappearance, death and successful hatching were recorded separately? The first-observed stage dates alone would already help clarify the published estimand.

We would follow your conditions for access, reuse and attribution and would keep private raw material outside the public repository. If another archive holds the records, we would be grateful for a referral.

Thank you for considering the request.

## Founding reserves and first-brood records

**Subject** Request for founder-level brood and reserve measurements in *Lasius niger*

Dear research team,

We are comparing alternative models of the maternal energy and water budget during founding. We are looking for existing measurements of founding queens across first laying and first-worker emergence. Would your group be able to share a data dictionary or de-identified repeated measures, including verified species, collection cohort, thermal/hydration conditions, feeding assignment and observed uptake, queen wet and dry mass or body composition, brood counts and the outcome for every starting queen?

If your study records individual egg mass or respiration, known-age brood fates, or paired pre/post laying measurements, could you specify the observation intervals and whether samples are repeated or destructive? We will distinguish effects of assigned diet from uptake and will retain deaths and incomplete follow-up. We would be happy to restrict reuse to an agreed validation protocol and cite any formal deposit.

Thank you for your time.

## Video and new-cohort preparation

- Obtain use permission and inventory the original time-stamped *L. niger* B01/B02 films. Re-annotate a prespecified roster with an independent second observer and retain unobservable frames. Use known identity, active handling, feeding start/end, contact, pick-up, drop, stage, and visibility codes. Track queen and worker source groups. Estimate agreement at matched units. Existing videos remain development evidence.
- For a new holdout, agree on collection locations and date clusters, no-worker versus first-worker access, a matched hydration treatment, and a restricted temperature window. Independent units must be randomized and retained regardless of early mortality. Use a masked source-colony split with a separately versioned codebook. Obtain permission before any animal work or sharing.
- Measure end-to-end transfers with known offered dose, actual receipt, failed and zero-dose interactions, crop emptying and body uptake; keep queen and larva contexts separate. Coordinate parallel age-matched specimens for physical and biochemical measurements while preserving the non-destructive survival follow-up. Register humidity, camera detection, handling control, nesting material and missing-event flags.
- Request publisher supplements for the recent acute heat cohorts if the DOI records do not link downloadable tables. They are potential external stress controls. Log treatment, species, sample sizes, repeated observations and absolute timing before reading validation outcomes, and keep the raw file hash and eligibility decision. A transient heat perturbation does not identify a constant-temperature embryo budget.
