# WP-4 engineering and controlled performance

## Observation boundaries

The experiment uses the accepted RM-02 SQLite publisher and the public nutrition, identity, fact and immutable physiology queries. Each case ends with a byte comparison of every canonical fact and the complete authoritative checkpoint against an observer-off run. That run skips observation cuts except where a declared external intervention needs a committed identity. At the middle cut, the live run drains, binds its Sidecar to the checkpoint SHA-256, saves a segmented SQLite world, reloads the complete checkpoint, and reopens the Sidecar with duplicate-cut detection. The offline verifier checks compressed artifact lengths and SHA-256, complete canonical ID order, the Sidecar hash chain, cut state and ledger joins, the durable SQLite prefix's canonical hash, and the maternal/offspring budget at every captured individual.

Per individual, energy input equals starting body energy plus measured oxidized carbohydrate times the fixture yield. This equals energy in the body plus dissipated and exported energy. Hydration equals starting water plus absorbed water less observed loss and egg export. Crop protein pays the protein part of a birth. Maternal body hydration pays its water part. The child receives both, and the world ledger excludes the internal birth credit from external supply. Body protein and oxidized carbohydrate are checked independently. Program-specific basal/active integrals are rate reconstructions between committed transitions. The pooled dissipation remains a cut fact. The reconstruction preserves residuals from clipping or other costs instead of treating them as observed locomotor expenditure.

The supplemental census is a host artifact keyed by world, cut and canonical cursor. Its files are hashed at archive creation. The existing SQLite world and Sidecar have exact restore semantics. An interrupted host projection is incomplete and can be regenerated by replay. It carries no new authority or cross-process transaction. Guard reason and molecular provenance of mixed crop remain `UNOBSERVED`. Host I/O failures fail the assay. The inherited RM-02 failure and retry tests exercise recovery independently of these simulation outcomes.

## Diagnostic archive costs and storage limits

The accepted matrix contains 8,868,731 facts and 9,524 observation cuts. The largest world has 509,246 facts. The largest lineage cut is 14,276 B and the largest canonical projection cut 904,861 B. Maximum queued payload is 1,759,848 B. The largest closed Sidecar is 9,736,192 B, the largest midpoint SQLite world is 6,995,968 B, and peak process RSS reaches 314,658,816 B in a large empty case including its live world and authoritative history. The 104 lossless case archives sum to 433,404,562 B. The per-case phase maxima include a 9.64 ms lineage read/serialization and a 119.79 ms cumulative drain. These were whole-world diagnostics, some collected while unrelated local test suites ran. The admitted observer benchmark above gives the matched latency and RSS ratios.

The broad-scope first pass completed 93/104 cases. Five hit the Sidecar database limit and six failed cut coverage. All 11 failed archives, totaling 59,160,066 B, and all 104 first-pass receipts are retained. The 93 completed histories have identical canonical fact and initial/middle/final checkpoint hashes in the second pass. Available checkpoint hashes in the incomplete cases also match. The second pass applies the already implemented nutrition scope, and its complete canonical archive still contains all facts. No limits were increased.
