In the ecology and reserve assays use daily cuts and an exact midpoint. In the brood assay use six-hour cuts and exact event-adjacent times. In the pulse and clock assays use the explicitly defined scheduling boundaries. Every live case uses a midpoint segmented SQLite save, exact checkpoint recovery, observer reattach and a duplicate-cut check. An observer-off replay starts from the same checkpoint, follows the same admitted external input schedule, may skip pure observation boundaries, and must match final complete checkpoint and canonical history. No observation replays an internal decision.

For maternal budgets retain gifts, crop withdrawal or ingest, realized absorption, maternal water loss, energy dissipation, export to egg, funded tissue, attempt decisions and death. Track every founded egg by stable world/entity identity, retaining stage, committed entry, endpoint and event ID, or right-censoring. Record no-uptake windows, zero-dose rejections and terminal outcomes. Stop risk sets at death. In isolated pulse arms use the known crop input and exact fact order. Ecological gift-to-absorption lags use the next positive component pulse and cannot identify which molecules it used. Report simultaneous facts in EventId order. Profile comparisons describe clock mechanics and the realized survival competition.

For six-seed ecological contrasts use within-seed pairs, 4,096 bootstrap draws and fixed resampling seed 640405. Cluster all births and follow-up within their world. Provide arithmetic means and a percentile 95% interval for paired differences, and denominators for phase-specific probabilities. The short single-seed controls are exact engineering probes and have no inferential interval. Death and right-censoring are separate from hatch and emergence. Retain the count and composition of every attempt, failure, unexpected result or unavailable observation. None of these software seeds are independent biological samples.

## Observation and engineering acceptance

Use the accepted RM-02 Sidecar schema, byte codec, publisher, restart and failure paths. Reuse its activity/care fact scope where it supplies events needed for the budget. Capture a bounded supplemental census of queryable lineage at the same owner-thread cuts. Keep at most 128 individuals with physiology and at most 256 KiB of serialized lineage per cut. Overflow fails the assay, with no dropping of facts. Preserve the publisher's 256-fact pages, 64-ID focal cap, 3 MiB cut and 64 MiB database bound. Save the full canonical history, original/final/midpoint frames, exact checkpoint receipt, both SQLite databases, original input IDs and all auxiliary rows. Verify event and checkpoint identity after toggling observation or subdividing progress and after restoring with the observer. Retain the RM-02 sidecar write-fault/retry/duplicate checks. Check mass by component and energy at every cut and independently reproduce per-actor balances.

Use `COMMITTED_FACT` for transfers, completed/deferred reproductive attempts, birth allocations, positive or zero physiology pulses, deaths and stage transitions. Use `CUT_SNAPSHOT` for immutable physiological query results. This API analytically settles the returned copy at the cut, without a world mutation. Use `RECONSTRUCTED` for debit/credit calculations and program-rate time integrals. Total dissipated energy and export are separate. Actual motion energy, event-internal guard results, molecular provenance inside a mixed crop and biochemical reserve identity remain `UNOBSERVED`. Do not report a projected deficit or lack of one as an observed mechanism.

Use three admitted sessions and the accepted observer gates of P50 ≤1.10×, P95 ≤1.20×, off/off P50 0.90–1.10, foreground P95 ≤5 ms and maximum ≤20 ms, paired incremental RSS ≤16 MiB. Full ecology outputs also report descriptive per-cut, queue, SQLite and replay costs. A failed admission or incomplete matrix is reported as NOT_RUN or INCONCLUSIVE, as applicable. Descriptive measurements cannot substitute for an admitted gate. Run Debug, Release, ASan/UBSan, cross-platform CI and the existing negative, conservation and recovery tests.
