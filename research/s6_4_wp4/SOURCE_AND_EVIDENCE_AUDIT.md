# WP-4 source and evidence audit

The executable projection in [MECHANISM_TRANSITION_PARAMETER_MAP.csv](MECHANISM_TRANSITION_PARAMETER_MAP.csv) includes primary and supporting mechanisms for BR-01 and BR-04. It preserves accepted anchors, DOI/context keys, units, and provenance classes. The following audit asks which observed quantities could constrain the joint maternal and offspring budget. No evidence grade changes.

## Key code-to-measurement mismatches

|---|---|---|
| [Reproduction resource guard](../../cpp/src/long_term_internal.hpp) and [attempt and birth commit](../../cpp/src/long_term.cpp) | Successful birth spends the exported energy plus fixed dissipation, body water and crop protein. The child receives that export as initialized stores. An attempt with no child has no recorded guard reason. | B01 conditional changes in brood output and B03 food treatments do not identify a two-day reserve horizon or discrete egg energy/water debit. Use FT01 only for the existence of tissue remodeling. |
| [Expenditure and absorption](../../cpp/src/physiology_internal.hpp) | Stored crop and metabolically usable body are separate. The reported physiology query returns a settled copy. A positive pulse credits energy/water and larval tissue. | Cross-caste field respirometry cannot estimate each simulated queen's basal versus active cost. | [Gift receipt and physiology phase order](../../cpp/src/physiology.cpp) | Transfer and assimilation are separate facts. Death is evaluated before stage transition or due uptake. | [Development predicate and next wake](../../cpp/src/physiology_internal.hpp) | Egg and pupa do not absorb. Larval progress begins at a fixed tissue threshold. | [Stage and death commits](../../cpp/src/physiology.cpp) and [life history](../../cpp/src/lifecycle.cpp) | Stable identity and exact committed event time. Physical care and life-stage associations cannot identify a care effect by themselves. |
