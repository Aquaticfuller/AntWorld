# R0 experiment protocol

Baseline: `476cfac777124d2f07be100494af48726ca8c31d`. R0 is an opt-in engineering experiment. Neither individual motility nor the long-term demographic outcomes are species-calibrated. Preserve historical B06 `INSUFFICIENT`, S6.4 performance `FAIL`, and the nine `BIOLOGICAL_BLOCKED` findings.

## Matched field and observation conditions

- Start from a single Borisovka queen and zero workers. Use the protected M0 shaft and seeds 1 and 7, the standard worker review intervals, and the same nutrition, earlier developmental rules and physical transaction costs in each pair. Use an additional dogleg observation run to test navigation away from the straight shaft. Inspect both versions for all negative outcomes.
- Advance each long run to day 730 with bounded daily transactions. Record days 365, 450 and 730 from the same trajectory. Record daily before advancing again. No post-initialization releases outside the configured finite source cycle. Apply paired environmental treatments to the same inputs in both versions.
- Continued supply uses the published balanced pulse and enough initialized reserve for 730 daily pulses. Limited supply uses the published 120-day reserve with the same initial food and pulse size. Water-restricted supply releases the same nutrients with one quarter of the water. Seasonal supply uses the sustained reserve with alternate 182-day release spans at 100% and 25%. This tests seasonally restricted input without claiming thermal development, diapause or an ant life-history model of winter.
- Record living stages, births and deaths by stage, cause and time, actual nutrient releases and depot/external stocks, queen body and crop water, usable protein and energy, paid excavation and material balance, cumulative and daily movement distance and overlap at each daily cut. Report timed action intervals where recorded. For other moves, separate the witnessed displacement from unknown duration. A day without a displacement fact is not a day of continuous sleep.
- Paired observation exports use seed 1, the shaft, standard finite 120-day supply and day 90 horizon. Show actual workers that develop in the world. Single-worker/multiple-worker, sealed wall and open-floor comparisons may use declared fixture adults for mechanism tests; fixture adults cannot count as founding outcomes.

## Candidate and engineering checks

- Test a bounded paid worker-only movement to a clear nearby resting site when choosing Rest in a crowded nest. Test local clearance alternatives for brood approach. The historical unloaded speed and maximum speed still bound the candidates. The rest location must remain physically connected and within actual depot contact. Resting occupancy is an axis-aligned body proxy. Transient traffic through the transfer point is permitted. Preserve quiet intervals, ordinary decisions, real contact, reserve consumption, and worker identity.
- Compare the existing demand rule with a count of reachable floor capacity and physically local bodies, including brood load. The support/capacity proxy and brood weighting are `ENGINEERING_FIXTURE`. Digging remains a local autonomous task with existing pressure, access, ability, arbitration, carrying and placement constraints. Evaluate at multiple initial layouts and worker densities. Prefer a null result to a forced excavation.
- Require deterministic final frame and journal equivalence under partitioning and save/restore, exact legacy replay, three balanced ledgers, no successful placement into soil or an occupied berth, no creation of additional workers or nutrients, and completed existing food/care/soil transactions in the observed candidate. Keep failed scenarios. Compare changes in movement, loss, delivery and excavation jointly. No numeric biological acceptance threshold is established by these runs.
- Use one committed movement per accepted segment. Preserve the authoritative endpoints and timed act intervals. OP0 may interpolate only a segment with a verified successful movement, matching start/end and geometry evidence. Sparse archives remain readable. Record history size, throughput and retention cost without replacing the existing controlled performance gate.
