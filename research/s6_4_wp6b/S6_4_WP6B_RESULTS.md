## Implementation and engineering evidence

- An enrolled foundress owns one finite pool of lipid-equivalent substrate and mobile protein. She begins with finite body hydration and a usable energy buffer funded from her own lipid. The regular physiological owner event debits whole lipid units and credits their effective energy. Per-stage energy and hydration charges use exact integer carries. Each actual lay pays its overhead and moves a complete funded egg packet to the newborn's stable identity in one transaction.
- The egg and pupa run on owned stocks. Development and maintenance are separate. Larvae that hit the elapsed clock cap keep paying maintenance. They require assimilated tissue and a positive closed-stage margin before they can pupate. The existing local care act can make an actual reserve-funded larval ration after physical contact. Lipid reaches larval reserves, and protein and water enter crop before uptake. Death resolves before new credit or a coincident transition. On eclosion the same identity enters ordinary worker autonomy.
- All 250 inherited CTest names remain. The 13 new `FND_*` tests cover profile rejection, exact conversion and time carries, refused and rolled-back birth, the half-open lay bout, energy/water/development ties, capped larvae, a strict pupal residue, local dose and simultaneous gifts, eclosion, observed and unobserved equality, SQLite restart, codec errors and bounded work. Debug, Release and ASan/UBSan completed 263/263 locally. The first sanitizer link ran out of disk space and its failed build receipt remains archived. The repeated build completed after a verified relocation of an ignored old build cache. [Local receipts](../../experiments/s6_4_wp6b/tests)
- Twelve independently replayed WP-5 founding controls matched their accepted initial checkpoints, final checkpoints and canonical histories byte-for-byte. Archived WP-4 0.5 J warm-budget embryo deaths, RM-01 technical sentinels, and WP-5 no-first-worker controls remain in their accepted directories. F1 is a separate profile and format. Ten current F1 inputs also reproduced exactly across 90 measured observation on/off and midpoint SQLite recovery runs. ## Frozen simulation evidence

All 1,932 cases were attempted, including 960 ordinary and additional-seed cases, 840 wider inputs, 36 nominal/adverse micro trajectories and 96 seed-worker trajectories. Exactly 36 wider inputs failed preregistered validation. The rejected conditions were low foundress wet mass, low larval basal relative to the other stages, and high pupal basal relative to the larval rate, with 12 rejections each. All other 1,896 runs completed 90 simulated days. The raw output contains 34,418,773 committed facts. The independent reader verifies every input and archive hash and reconciles world and per-individual ledgers at 172,536 fixed daily cuts. No biological failure or right-censor was removed. ## Readiness decision

F1 engineering correctness is supported by exact ledger, identity, transaction and restart evidence. Ordinary micro-to-colony behavior is insufficient. Colony provisioning and first-generation and long-term ordinary outcomes fail the preregistered multi-scale gate. Across the sampled ranges, conditional viability is demonstrated in five no-worker wider worlds, with no robust ordinary viability in either warm population. See the performance report for the original native gates and the separate application export costs.

The next review can consider the local repeated-recipient hypothesis described in the scientific assessment. None of the nine BR questions gains independent biological validation. BR-01 through BR-09 retain `BIOLOGICAL_BLOCKED`. This candidate remains an experiment and cannot replace the default model.
