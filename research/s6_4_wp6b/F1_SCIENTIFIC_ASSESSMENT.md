Each population completed 192 primary care-on worlds and 48 additional care-on worlds. Neither population passed any of the 32 primary or eight check profiles. The fixed decision required 24/32 and 6/8 profiles, with at least 4/6 successful seeds in a passing profile and at least 10/16 at each ordinary factor level. The paired bootstrap interval for first-worker incidence difference is [0, 0] in all four strata because the observed binary outcome is zero in both arms. This degenerate simulation interval contains no information about biological replication or the upper probability of success in untested conditions. ## A falsifiable follow-up

The observed combination of reachable larvae, unused maternal protein, sparse acts per recipient and death before enough tissue is acquired motivates a local feeding-persistence hypothesis. After a committed positive contact dose, retain that already contacted larva as eligible for a subsequent care bout while she remains reachable, alive and has a deficit. Require the same real opportunity cadence, geometry, duration, custody and finite donor and receiver guards. No extra resource, altered absorption clock, forced selection at every wake or global colony target is needed to test this mechanism. The comparison would retain unmodified F1, care off, accessible and occluded recipients, and the entire frozen ordinary envelope. Its mechanistic endpoint is the distribution of repeated real rations and effective uptake per recipient before depletion, with per-stage competing risks and founder spending. If dose concentration does not raise tissue attainment without depriving other larvae or creating unsupported persistence, reject the hypothesis. A separate local oviposition-versus-care allocation experiment would then test the many-eggs failure mode at fixed stock and actual contact frequency. These candidates require new authorization. No next mechanism or parameter change was applied in WP-6B.

The engineering result supports physically funded F1 trajectories. The ordinary micro-to-cohort pattern and long-term robustness remain inadequate for `SIMULATION_CONDITIONALLY_ACCEPTED`.
