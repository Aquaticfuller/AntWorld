## Stage A. Read path and record scope

Use the owner thread at successful `AdvanceTo` cuts. Page `events_after` in 256-fact chunks. Keep exact `encode_event` payloads for scoped nutrition, decision, failure, stage, intake, reproduction and relevant care facts. Keep the actual event and scheduled-source IDs. Use detached focal state and global nutrition ledger cuts. Record snapshot positions, crop, body energy, hydration, tissue protein and loss/oxidation counters. Capture Trophallaxis pending requests only at designated small cuts with at most 512 pending events, and a separate geometric neighbor snapshot for a designated focal actor only when the world has at most 64 individuals and 512 food sources. Distinguish this geometry from modeled sampled opportunities and empirical oral contact. A known matched rejection is zero committed transfer. A future owned request at the final cut is right-censored. An uncaptured choice, missing association or disappearance without known outcome is unobserved.

The sidecar owns a versioned SQLite schema independent of the authoritative store. Bind it to a declared lineage, WorldId, exact scope and starting cut. Store ordered cut metadata, a length-delimited binary observation payload and a digest linked to the preceding cut. Append each cut transactionally. A resume requires an anchor for the final sidecar cut and a matching restored authoritative checkpoint, including its history. Use a new lineage with a declared gap for mismatched or missing records, preserving the original sidecar. A repeated identical live cut cannot create duplicate rows. A lost, corrupt or failed observation cannot change world state or simulation progress. The analysis distinguishes `COMMITTED_FACT`, `CUT_SNAPSHOT`, `RECONSTRUCTED` and `UNOBSERVED`. Per-gift biological assimilation remains unobserved when the crop mixes sources.

Limit focal snapshots to 64 named individuals. Use a 3 MiB maximum cut payload within a 4 MiB observer staging budget, with bounded read pages and an explicit skipped-queue/overflow indicator. Limit the persisted sidecar database to 64 MiB and record its transient journal and peak process memory separately. Keep exact world facts irrespective of observation coverage. Retain an explicit failure receipt if a cut cannot be published. The host must never re-execute contact selection, consume randomness, switch a biology profile to gain evidence, add facts or change an authoritative format.

## Stage B. Engineering and performance acceptance

Run inherited and new Debug, Release and ASan/UBSan CTest and applicable Core CI. The new test matrix must cover role-specific full and partial gifts, typed and unmatched rejections, no selection, explicit zero transfer, capacity/demand/distance/death, multiple donors and targets, same-time stages and uptake, recycling and energy accounting. Include active and missing opportunity profiles, exact on/off and cut/partition state, canonical bytes and RNG/queue/checkpoint parity. Reopen a sidecar at a published authoritative checkpoint, check duplicate reads and deterministic continuation, and exercise faulted publication, corrupted/mismatched anchors, missing sidecars, capacity limits and failed world advancement. All tests use the public API. A confirmed Core error is a separate proposal.

Repeat TD-01 and TD-08 using the accepted scripts, exact RM-01-R1 workloads, frozen binary identities where available, and the same version-2 confirmation and native host telemetry. Each requires its original three admitted sessions and pooled dense 2,048/512 advance P50 ≤6×. [Frozen comparator](../s6_4_rm01/RM01_R1_PERFORMANCE_PROTOCOL.md) [Native admission](../../docs/55_S6_3_3_1_Performance_Instrument_Protocol.md)

The separate observer benchmark uses identical application binary and input per pair. Cells are the original dense contact world with seed 42 and 512 or 2,048 initial workers, and the corrected S6.3.1 depot-32 world with seed 1 through day 90. In the contact cells both arms use the exact 1 s advance cuts through 10 s and the reader observes one terminal cut with the first 64 worker IDs. In depot-32 both arms advance daily, and the reader takes an initial cut and one cut per day, scopes all relevant facts and snapshots the initial queen and workers. Small-case pending and geometry queries are reserved for the separate diagnostic windows, not the steady-state benchmark. Measure sidecar database initialization and final authoritative checkpoint audit separately. The timed on interval includes advancement, capture, encoding and actual committed sidecar writes. Retain exact state, trace, work and continuation checks outside the timed interval. TD-08 remains an independent Core gate.

Use three admitted sessions. Per cell and session take one four-row on/off warm-up block, one four-row off/off control block and twelve retained on/off blocks. Alternate ABBA/BAAB by block and reverse the cell order for session two. Check the pooled off/off P50 ratio in each cell against the predeclared 0.90–1.10 repeatability interval. A failed repeatability or incomplete admitted series is inconclusive. Admit with the original 60 intervals, system and chosen CPU P50 ≤10% and P95 ≤20%, AC and saver conditions, matched HWiNFO mapping and valid temperature, power and frequency. Allow three attempts separated by at least 300 s after a rejection. Keep every attempt. Stop a batch at exhaustion or an interrupted started session. Preserve raw timing, work, process and hardware telemetry, including slow samples. Apply the original bracketing and 10 s maximum sensor gap.

Observer-on/off total-work pooled ratios per cell must be ≤1.10 for P50 and ≤1.20 for P95. Report P99 and a 4,096-draw hierarchical session/block paired bootstrap interval with seed 63201. Foreground capture/submission P95 is ≤5 ms and observed maximum ≤20 ms. Observer incremental peak RSS is ≤16 MiB. A quota, sample loss, identity mismatch or incomplete required trace makes the result ineligible. The budgets measure the reader with all scoped facts and real persistence. Retain results of any failed candidate and rerun with the same workload and thresholds after an application-only fix. A genuinely unavailable host may leave a performance gate `NOT_RUN` or `INCONCLUSIVE`.

## Stage C. Frozen simulated comparisons

Enter after observer engineering correctness passes. A confirmed unrepaired performance failure blocks Stage C. If host conditions leave controlled performance unmeasurable, limit conclusions to the qualified engineering paths and mark the performance gate separately.

- Reuse `s6_3_1_fixture::World` with `reserve_mode=1` and its unchanged ordinary configuration. Run initial workers 8 and 32, seeds 1–6, and all four published treatment families. The finite depot control ends at day 180, and the balanced ordinary, scarce-protein and empty external supply arms end at day 90. Compare diets at the common day-90 horizon. The depot arm uses `positive=true`. The other controls use their existing `balanced`, `scarce` and `empty` inputs with `cyclic=false`. Carry every starting store and cost from the fixture. At day zero and each day capture all scoped event facts and detached states of the initial queen and workers. Retain offspring identities and stage/transfer facts without assuming unobserved offspring body stores.
- For balanced ordinary cases also capture bounded pending and focal queen geometry at actual queued timestamps in the first 48 simulated hours, using a separate trace run with the same seeds and populations. Do not replay RNG to reconstruct choices. Report captured opportunities, selections and transfer counts with explicit denominator coverage. Use an already configured care-profile control to exercise committed Opportunity and Selected records in Stage B without reconfiguring the long-run ecology.
- For dense coverage run the same contact fixture in the terminal focal scope at 512 and 2,048 workers, seeds 1–6, through 10 s. This is coverage evidence. Its separate host times never substitute for the admitted performance test.

Record all input checkpoint and binary digests, build identity, fixture configurations, start/end cut, seed, provenance and status, original sidecars, event counts, per-role committed doses, queen state/time to first later positive pulse, embryo/larval outcomes and colony inventory at death where observable. Compute crop reconstruction only where initial inputs and actual flows reconcile at subsequent cuts. Latency from gift to next observed positive pulse is a temporal association, with competing death and explicit censoring. Keep unsuccessful and negative cases. Use paired seed-level 95% intervals with 4,096 bootstrap draws and seed 63101. Seeds quantify fixture variability. Biology comparison remains scoped to the actual experimental units and measurements.
