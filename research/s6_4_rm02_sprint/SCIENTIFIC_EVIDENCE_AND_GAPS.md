## Simulation comparison status

The measurement and species distinctions above apply to the planned, condition-matched analyses. The Stage C matrix remains blocked by the observed final-drain performance failure. The Stage B long-run witness confirms the observer can retain simulated positive gifts and reconcile focal crop accounting in one engineering fixture. It provides no paired population or diet result. A numerical claim of agreement or discrepancy with empirical bouts would also require a common exposure, time window and observation operator. The existing model emits discrete transfer and uptake facts, while the cited focal experiments record oral contact or downstream group outcomes. Dose and crop-to-body kinetics therefore remain scientifically unidentified. ## Minimum falsifiable candidate for separate approval

Test whether an observable recipient signal recorded at close encounter adds out-of-colony predictive value for recipient choice beyond local geometry, available partners and donor state. Start in adult worker groups because B08 and N01 establish an observation operator there. Predeclare a choice set with a no-choice outcome and compare a geometry/donor-only baseline against one additional prespecified cue term. Use a nested conditional-choice model with a colony-level effect. Keep existing physical reach and exact conservation constraints. A measurable cue effect needs validation in queen and larva strata before applying it there.

Collect linked timestamps, source-colony IDs, donor and available partners, caste and stage, standardized exposure, donor state and candidate cue visibility. Record actual directed contact and its start and end. Measure pre/post crop composition with calibration per claimed component and sensitivity sufficient to distinguish a true zero from a below-limit transfer. Use early and late separate crop/body measurements after a quantified dose. Record onward transfer, water loss and oxidation or respiration where these compete with uptake. Mark competing deaths and censored observation. Hold out entire original source colonies. Determine the minimum independent sample count and acceptable prediction and equivalence margins using a separate blinded instrument pilot and between-colony variation. B08, its Bles-linked context and N01 remain development comparisons.

The first candidate changes only the recipient-choice hypothesis, with separate incidence and dose measurements keeping selection from absorbing their effects. If the cue adds no prespecified held-out predictive value, or is confounded with availability after matching, retain the geometry/donor baseline and the negative result. If true zero or compartment kinetics remain unidentified, keep those terms outside calibration and leave BR-02 blocked. Independent validation and explicit change authorization are required before any biological adoption.
