## Observation definitions

Use the owner thread at successful simulated-time cuts. Extend the RM-02 sidecar identity with an explicit scope marker and retain its bounded event paging, exact canonical record bytes, paired checkpoint anchors, append, integrity and fault behavior. Preserve the existing RM-02 identity and projection. WP-2 includes the RM-02 scope plus movement, rest, soil, behavior and all typed care facts. Capture all relevant event actors for the small experiments. Focal state snapshots cover the initial identities, including brood, with up to 64 IDs. Newly born individuals have birth, stage and death records under the all-relevant projection. Unsampled body stores are `UNOBSERVED`. Use stable WorldId, EventId, ScheduledId, actor and target IDs. Store cuts, inputs and intervention timestamps independently of any claimed act or choice.

- Task dwell reconstructs living, physiology-enabled intervals from actual transitions and death. Report Nurse, Forage and Excavate separately and as the equation's active-program mask. Rest and Paused are program values. Reports never convert these durations to locomotion, rest physiology or sleep.
- A known-start and timed terminal care pair supports an act interval. Known Interrupted acts contribute only elapsed time. Typed Inactive provides an explicitly scheduled inactive interval. Unknown-duration events, unpaired selections, uncovered gaps and external interruptions keep their own statuses. Active bouts open at a window boundary contribute an observed prefix and a right-censored terminal time. Simulated time outside the union of known intervals is `UNOBSERVED` for physical activity. Timed locomotor actuations and manipulation intervals are separate, and discrete committed movement supports endpoint displacement but no continuous path or interval.
- Process pickup, carry, place, return and revisit in event order, joining the same target and carrier. Keep unique placed brood distinct from repeat placement and from a short displacement by arrangement. Track a completed transport segment only with matched committed movement when claiming displacement. Count feeding and components from actual transfer facts or typed results linked by source, with no double counting. Selected, started, completed, interrupted, explicit nonchoice, no observable option and unobserved eligibility are distinct. A care opportunity is a modeled record with a target, without claiming mouth contact or an empirical risk set.
- Report basal and active-program accounting from the published fixture rates and observed alive/program intervals. Use focal body cuts for changes in dissipation, hydration loss, oxidized carbohydrate, received/used components, egg export and body reserves. Preserve zero-dose, no positive absorption, death cause and last positive uptake. All modeled physiology uses actual nJ, µg and simulated milliseconds. Compare endpoint displacement in metres and elapsed intervals in milliseconds. Unknown effort, posture, O₂, humidity exposure and RQ remain unobserved.
