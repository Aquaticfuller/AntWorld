## Primary source checks

The approved local full-text extracts, original supplements and public original articles provide the locators below. The original Batch 1 and 3 receipts retain source identities and any access restrictions. The new analysis uses the available endpoints at their original sampling units.

| Source and evidence class | Verified measurement and experimental unit | Matching decision |
|---|---|---|
| N27, Kervella et al., 2024, direct focal evidence, A | Methods §§2.1–2.5, Table 1 and Fig. 3 on p. 5. Clark 20 °C focal queen O₂ 6.15 ± 2.14 µL/h (8 measurements), worker 0.99 ± 0.23 (33, pool-derived). Sable measurements use a different device and temperature. §3.3/Fig. 5 uses chambers of pooled workers in darkness and reports no statistically supported increase with measured activity in this assay. | O₂ in resting/device-specific exposure and a null conditional activity comparison cannot identify the role surcharge or daily movement duty cycle. Chamber pooling, thermal conditions, food, substrate and respiratory quotient matter. Preserve the device differences and sample hierarchy. [Original article](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jinsphys.2024.104698) |
| B04, Kervella et al., 2025, direct focal evidence, A | §§2.2–2.3, Table 1, Fig. 1A, §4.1 and Data S1 Fig. S5. Resting mass-normalized O₂ and body mass in individual queens and worker pools. Queen 3.62 ± 1.23 nL/min/mg^alpha in the original table. The Methods and Table 1 report different oxygen sample counts, retained in the source register. | Body-mass-normalized oxygen cannot directly set per-ant nJ/ms or a cost ratio between program labels. Distinguish pool mass, per-individual flux and mass-specific scaling. Substrate/heat conversion and condition-matched locomotion remain needed. [Original article](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12573726/) |

[Original article](https://doi.org/10.1111/j.1439-0310.1983.tb00088.x) |
| B3-T06, Depickère et al., 2004, direct focal evidence, A | Methods pp. 243–244, Figs. 1–2 and Tables 1–2. Queenless workers choose marked vs clean tubes (20 workers per trial). Five-minute counts repeat within trials. Marked-site attraction appears earlier and more often. Overall selected-site occupancy at 60 min did not differ detectably. | An aggregation/choice endpoint supplies a cue hypothesis for site use. | B3-T09, Quque et al., 2023, direct focal evidence, A | Methods Biological material and statistical analyses, Figs. 1–4. 0–1 and 11–12 month nanitics crossed with bait-defined nest/foraging groups. All focal behavioral sources establish the phenomena in their original contexts. The measured quantities above keep their experimental units. Any congener or other-ant reference in the frozen evidence projection retains its separate species classification. The selected sources leave the live movement/expenditure relationship, water loss and energetic brood benefit without a matched independent data series. WP-2 therefore tests the model and observation operator with frozen engineering fixtures while preserving `BR-03`, `BR-05` and the separate `BR-02` evidence gates.
