# WP-2 scientific interpretation and next experiments

## RM-03

The simulation exposes an energy-accounting and observation mismatch. In the activity fixture, depot-32 has 32.4% active-program exposure and no recorded displacement among workers seeded as adults. Empty-32 has 1.4% active-program exposure and about 215 committed moves per worker-day. Neither arm has observed continuous locomotor or stationary duration. Most long active dwell comes from Nurse, including many exact six-hour episodes. The core settles the configured surcharge according to the adult program mask, so dwell itself predicts modeled expenditure. A comparison of the surcharge with the same model's deaths cannot identify the energetic cost of a movement or a decision. [Executed diagnosis](MECHANISM_DIAGNOSIS.md#rm-03-activity-waiting-and-expenditure) [Equation](../../cpp/src/physiology_internal.hpp#L38)

The B3-T01 short scans give direct evidence of heterogeneous, act-dependent contributions among marked workers in young colonies. The marked-tube choice in B3-T06 supports location-specific context for aggregation. B3-T09 associates age and bait-defined role with molecular profiles. These endpoints justify separate role, age and exposure measurements. They cannot be treated as 24-hour inactivity, locomotion, sleep or task-cost measurements. Thus no percentage of simulated Rest can be judged equivalent to a species-wide rest fraction. [T01](https://doi.org/10.1111/j.1439-0310.1983.tb00088.x) [T06](https://doi.org/10.1007/s00040-004-0739-z) [T09](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9814961/)

N27 measures an absolute resting oxygen-flux context with device, temperature and pooling constraints. Its activity comparison in dark pooled-worker chambers has no supported activity effect within that assay. B04 compares mass-normalized resting metabolism in a different preparation. A higher absolute queen flux and a lower normalized queen flux can both occur with unequal mass and pooling. Neither endpoint isolates the cost of carrying, nurse manipulation, hydration loss, or the additional cost of laying. The current 1 nJ/ms basal and 1 nJ/ms program surcharge in these fixtures correspond to 3.6 mJ/h each by unit conversion. Comparing those energy rates to oxygen flux requires specified substrate or simultaneous CO₂ and an appropriate calorimetric conversion. These values remain engineering fixtures. [N27](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jinsphys.2024.104698) [B04](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12573726/)

Deaths with unabsorbed crop carbohydrate are a simulated timing/depletion result. They leave water, resting cost, dietary conversion, reserve and reproductive allocation confounded as biological causes. The observer can distinguish received food, absorbed carbohydrate, body dissipation, exported egg energy and observed time for the existing model. Any later calibration needs a matched flux trajectory and an explicitly observed activity interval. Increasing initial reserves or changing egg costs could mask the same phenotype without identifying activity expenditure. [Focal mass and energy records](../../experiments/s6_4_wp2/analysis/body_cuts.csv.gz) [Separate nutrition evidence gate](../s6_4_rm02/VALIDATION_PROTOCOL.md)

**Smallest testable candidate.** Compare a basal plus elapsed-role model against a basal plus one independently measured physical-activity term. Retain separately observed handling and idle intervals, rather than imputing their cost from a worker label. Begin with adult worker conditions, prescribed food exposure, temperature and humidity and matched body mass. Measure individual movement/handling continuously with simultaneous gas exchange (O₂ and CO₂) and water loss at a resolution sufficient to estimate lag. A queen/laying cohort needs measured reserves and egg exports before any extension to reproductive cost. Freeze the activity ethogram, sensor calibration, conversion, missing-frame, death and activity-window rules before estimating. Use repeated within-individual contrasts in multiple source colonies and leave entire source colonies out. Require held-out prediction of energy and water trajectories, including cases with a task label and sparse actual movement. A term without stable added predictive value leaves the simple baseline open. Direct measurements of force and effort remain separate from motion time.

## RM-06

The B01 development replay recovers the direction of addition and removal effects and retains a positive excess of scored care in the addition window. The estimate differs by +0.875 pp with a descriptive 95% interval of 0.083 to 1.667. Handling is observed as an intersection with a timed interval. This layout contrast locates a large part of the timing sensitivity upstream of pickup. It shows why fitting transport speed from the first-retrieval discrepancy would be underidentified. The range cannot calibrate the present group, load, search, placement or queen coefficients. In the paired 21-day worlds the care-on arm moves the displaced target into shelter, while the same arm can have fewer positive feeding events and less queen carbohydrate absorption. This separates the engineering motor capability from care's effect on nutrition and brood fitness. Final pupation, placement counts and survival cannot individually identify contact response, handling delay, reserve use or intake. A simpler contact-memory plus bounded motor remains an admissible candidate if a condition-matched comparison validates the separate stages. [Integrated results](MECHANISM_DIAGNOSIS.md#rm-06-brood-handling-and-feeding)

**Smallest testable candidate.** Test whether a local exposure-conditioned search/engagement gate predicts latency to contact better than the fixed clock under matched starting geometry. First separate physical discovery, contact, pickup, held path, release and re-search with tracked queen/worker and brood identities. Randomize measured initial position and origin with geometry and illumination fixed or crossed prospectively. Include unreachable and hidden targets and no brood. Predeclare failures, interruptions, never-seen targets and censoring. A separate worker-access test should compare mesh, actual allowed contact and absence with real callow behavior and the same scoring operator. Use B01/B02 only as development comparators and retain held-out independent queens and whole trios from separate source colonies. Select one timing or local engagement change only if an out-of-colony residual persists with matched exposure. Require prediction of latency and count together, along with negative controls and protection against increased handling with reduced real feeding.

## Evidence and authorization

The needed data are condition-matched *Lasius niger* movement and energy/water measurements, identified local choice/exposure records, staged handling video and actual brood intake or benefit. The strongest supported claims concern the existence of task heterogeneity, oral feeding, brood retrieval and socially responsive queen care under published conditions. The observation system and engineering fixtures support conditional comparisons with these studies. Rate, threshold, action cost, material preference, benefit, true rest and sleep parameterization remain open.

`BR-03` and `BR-05` remain `BLOCKED`. `BR-02` remains `BLOCKED` for independent available-partner, positive and zero dose, and separate crop/body time course data. The host proposals preserve failed and censored observations. A separate review must authorize any post-commit Core telemetry addition or biological change.
