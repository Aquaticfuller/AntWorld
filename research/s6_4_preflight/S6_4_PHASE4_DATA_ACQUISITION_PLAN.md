## Minimum eligible independent set

Each full protocol needs the conditions, contrasts and units below, with independent biological replication in every primary stratum. Use a complete assigned cohort with exposure, entry, event/time bounds, last observation, censoring and treatment and source IDs. Replicate whole independent queens, source colonies or independently assigned colony blocks as specified. Repeated ants within a colony, reused actors, eggs from one queen, tracking frames, videos, scans and software seeds remain nested observations. For a paired experiment keep the complete matched block intact.

At least two independent units are necessary to observe between-unit variation in a primary stratum. Before the independent set is opened, use calibration-side between-unit variation, observation error, event rates and attrition to determine the number of groups that can resolve the prespecified 95% uncertainty and acceptable error band. For sparse or censored outcomes use design simulations or an appropriate exact method at the biological assignment level. The structural minimum does not guarantee precision. A wide interval, missing necessary stratum or inability to estimate the primary contrast leaves the gate open. Require condition-matched controls with failures observed. Shared cohort observations can support several distinct endpoints and still count as one cohort. [Frozen evaluation method](BIOLOGICAL_VALIDATION_PLAN.md#comparison-and-uncertainty)

## VAL-BR-01 | Maternal reserves and laying

**Existing** B03 Appendix S1 has per-queen day-49 outcomes and four death annotations, and Appendix S2 has diet contrasts. B01 Fig. 4a supplies stage-specific egg count responses. FT01 records separate tissue snapshots. The first-use candidates Fecundity and the juvenile-hormone repository may offer egg counts, group outcomes or fertility measurements in their own contexts. Their published materials do not establish absolute usable reserves and exported mass/energy per egg. [Accepted source receipt](BATCH1_SOURCE_VERIFICATION_RECEIPT.md) [Fecundity study](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-021-82559-9) [Juvenile-hormone archive](https://doi.org/10.17632/dkf9g6prpm.2)

**Minimum and route** Independently observed single foundresses and early-worker queens under admitted worker access and provision contrasts. Link measured starting and subsequent reserves, actual intake and uptake, per-egg exports, laying and viable brood to queen and stage. Ask source authors for documented reserve or compositional series if already recorded. Collect linked reserve and flux measurements if absent. Complete matched raw set `NOT_FOUND`. Route `PROPOSED_COLLECTION`. **Bounded claim meanwhile** describe egg count and diet contrasts by published context and model funding behavior; reserve-conditioned fecundity remains unverified.

Complete matched raw set `NOT_FOUND`. Route `PROPOSED_COLLECTION`. **Bounded claim meanwhile** oral-event and supply-ledger behavior in its documented context. No calibrated dose, signal, lag or survival.
