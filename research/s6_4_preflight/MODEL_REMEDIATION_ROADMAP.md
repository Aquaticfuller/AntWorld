# Scientific remediation roadmap

This roadmap converts the nine open Phase 2 blockers and the accepted Phase 3 protocols into ten implementation packages. RM-01 is the only package recommended for immediate coding after independent Phase 4 approval. It is an isolated technical assay for BR-04. RM-05 holds the biological development work for that same blocker. The other packages have explicit data and evidence entry conditions. The package graph orders causal evidence and coupled implementation. Data acquisition and independent protocol preparation can proceed in parallel.

## Approval rules

- `GO_FOR_ISOLATED_ENGINEERING_EXPERIMENT` applies to an expressly approved package and test envelope. Retain old defaults and published artifacts. Establish correctness, replay, conservation and applicable performance before interpreting results. Engineering sentinels carry their fixture provenance.
- `GO_FOR_BIOLOGICAL_MODEL_ADOPTION` requires matched measured inputs, a constrained model with identified or justified effective parameters, an untouched independent validation comparison and engineering acceptance. Then obtain affirmative approval for the intended conditions and any default change. A published fit, an isolated code experiment and an independently tested biological prediction receive separate results.
- All nine biological blockers are `BLOCKED` until the full scoped criteria have passed independent validation. A partial result may support the listed restricted claim and reduce uncertainty about one component. It leaves the approved v1.0 coverage in place. [Gate assessment](S6_4_SCIENTIFIC_GATE_ASSESSMENT.md)

## RM-01 | Isolated egg timing

**Entry** independent Phase 4 approval. Reuse measured population-specific B06 egg means through an opt-in app-level resolver and current persisted field. Verify clocks, death priority and resume. Produce paired old and candidate histories with the unchanged-budget deaths and separately labeled timing sentinels. The computation of embryo depletion is a required adverse prediction to test. Record it before any viable embryo proposal. No additional independent data are required to start this technical task. [Exact code and test contract](S6_4_PHASE4_FIRST_IMPLEMENTATION_GATE.md)

## RM-02 | Recipient choice, actual dose and uptake

**BR-02 requires mechanism separation.** Use `resolve_nutrition_wakes`, `resolve_nutrition_action` and `absorb` as ownership boundaries. First trace actual opportunity, eligibility, selected recipient, positive transfer and absorbed amount. Replace any unsupported assumption of exact recipient knowledge with a locally available cue or a documented effective conditional law that survives matched tests. Use a conditional incidence and positive-dose model bounded by stores and capacities. A measured delay or simple uptake rate can suffice. Distinguish a numerical pulse from an identified lag. Obtain linked focal dose and uptake observations before changing the biological policy. A read-only trace can be tested under separate approval while data are acquired. [Protocol BR-02](INDEPENDENT_BIOLOGICAL_VALIDATION_PLAN.md#br-02--recipient-selection-transfer-and-uptake)

## RM-03 | Activity, task choice and physiological cost

**BR-05 requires mechanism separation.** Establish actual act/wait intervals independently of the role stored by `change_behavior` and eligibility in `resolve_autonomy_wakes`. Measure resting and incremental expenditure and water loss with caste, mass, stage and exposure. Where the current active role debit fails that comparison, base the cost on observed work or an independently supported effective activity class. Coarse states, piecewise rates and bounded task dwell may meet the target. Dose and deprivation, role eligibility and time at risk remain inputs. A motion-only archive can test motion. It cannot set energy, sleep or role-switch parameters. An observer/off-on check is admissible as a separately approved technical precursor. [Protocol BR-05](INDEPENDENT_BIOLOGICAL_VALIDATION_PLAN.md#br-05--time-use-task-selection-and-state-dependent-expenditure)

## RM-04 | Maternal reserves and laying

**BR-01 requires a local stage-conditioned allocation rule.** Use the received/assimilated supply from RM-02, independently constrained cost from RM-03 and maternal/worker care context from RM-06. Separate usable reserve, per-egg exported material and energy, maternal dissipation and actual laying events in `execute_reproduction`. Test a small reserve-conditioned model of successful laying with identified terms. Retain the original local guard and ordinary, scarce and adequate-access controls. A simple budget and conditional oviposition rule may suffice if matched flux and output agree. A fixed attempt clock or larger food stock alone cannot demonstrate the missing reproductive response. Required measurements include linked foundress reserve, body state and actual egg exports and outcomes. [Protocol BR-01](INDEPENDENT_BIOLOGICAL_VALIDATION_PLAN.md#br-01--founding-reserve-and-reproductive-allocation)

## RM-05 | Viable embryo and active stage development

**BR-04 requires mechanism change for the coupled biology.** Use RM-01 to establish timing and recovery invariants. Use RM-04 for supplied embryo resources, RM-03 for stage-specific expenditure and water loss evidence, and RM-02 for nutrition in interacting stages. A B06 warm egg-duration reference is derivable under Phase 3 assumptions. It cannot calibrate embryo survival. A simple measured condition-indexed active clock is admissible within a supported regime. Identify juvenile stage boundaries, nutrition conditions and competing losses before expanding it. Require independent hatch incidence and timing as well as later stages. Keep nonprogression, failed embryos and the original energy/water settings in the comparator. Stage-specific viable physiology cannot start from an arbitrary reserve increase or reduced loss. [Protocol BR-04](INDEPENDENT_BIOLOGICAL_VALIDATION_PLAN.md#br-04--active-thermal-and-nutrition-dependent-development)

## RM-06 | Active care and timed retrieval

**BR-03 first tests an explicit simplification and matched calibration.** Observe opportunity, stage, active intervals and pickup/carry/place outcomes in matched layouts. Use B01 and B02 only for development. Fit identifiable act times and support measured access, leaving published markers and denominators intact. Change the worker-contact response or care motor only where a matched residual discrepancy identifies a need. A short local response and explicit handling stages can suffice without broad sensory or foreign-brood claims. Report non-retrieval and interrupted custody. Scoring and trace fidelity can be checked before acquiring new independent cohorts. [Protocol BR-03](INDEPENDENT_BIOLOGICAL_VALIDATION_PLAN.md#br-03--active-care-and-completed-retrieval)

## RM-07 | Local food discovery and return

**BR-06 first tests a bounded local rule.** Test verified cue/absence, barriers, route memory, loaded return and failed searches with matched trajectories and timed delivery. Preserve local information and physical reach. Calibrate identifiable observation and motion quantities. Use a simple exploration and private-memory policy when it predicts the matched outcomes. If the current exact-nearby-mass observation or route rule fails the causal control, replace the specific mechanism indicated by the trace. Do not tune final success to compensate for missing detection or actual load. An observer can test available versus used cues before receiving held-out cohorts. [Protocol BR-06](INDEPENDENT_BIOLOGICAL_VALIDATION_PLAN.md#br-06--local-discovery-food-collection-and-individual-return)

## RM-08 | Scout-to-naive recruitment

**BR-07 requires an observed causal chain.** Use the local search and return baseline from RM-07, contact and delivery distinctions from RM-02 and observed departures/work from RM-03. Implement a local marking/exposure and activation rule if those events are absent from the current policy. An effective measured age response is admissible for a scoped assay. Keep experienced returns and unmarked, prepared cue and real scout conditions distinct. Published behavioral e-folding does not set a chemical half-life. Require independent group contrasts of actual naive activation, discovery and return before adoption. Role and trail occupancy are diagnostic. [Protocol BR-07](INDEPENDENT_BIOLOGICAL_VALIDATION_PLAN.md#br-07--scout-to-naive-recruitment)

## RM-09 | Active-window stage and cohort risk

**BR-08 tests whether the bounded existing risk rule is sufficient.** Use measured provisioning, costs, viable births and development from RM-02 through RM-05. Estimate any identifiable residual risk from stage- and cohort-appropriate data with known exposure and censoring. Change survival mechanisms if measured residual loss or age dependence exceeds the admitted prediction limits. A justified negligible-background-risk or simple competing-risk rule may suffice for a narrow supported window. Biological inference requires true losses and follow-up with conditions. Keep the observed exposure to simulated depletion distinct from unobserved causes and preserve all deaths. [Protocol BR-08](INDEPENDENT_BIOLOGICAL_VALIDATION_PLAN.md#br-08--cohort-survival-nonprogression-and-competing-outcomes)

## RM-10 | Limited autonomous 3D construction

**BR-09 tests the local digging rule and changes it if matched outcomes require it.** Use observed work and waiting times from RM-03 plus controlled soil and initial geometry. Measure material pickup/deposit and initiation/resumption before fitting a response to group size or available space. Retain authoritative 3D and material conservation. Coarse connected occupancy with a local, physically reachable rule can suffice. Published 2D area and partial CT metrics bound hypotheses. They leave transport and matched autonomous response open. A geometry reader and material ledger comparison can be tested independently of biological claims. [Protocol BR-09](INDEPENDENT_BIOLOGICAL_VALIDATION_PLAN.md#br-09--limited-autonomous-excavation-and-coarse-3d-structure)

## Release sequence

1. On approval, implement RM-01 as one independently reviewable code change. Finish the old-budget and sentinel traces and engineering checks before further scope is opened.
2. In parallel, acquire the matched component data for delivery (RM-02), observed time and cost (RM-03), care (RM-06) and local search (RM-07). Submit the smallest implementation request with measured endpoints once the corresponding entry condition is met. An observer-only proposal can be considered without waiting for a fitted model.
3. Use those results for maternal allocation (RM-04), causal recruitment (RM-08) and excavation response (RM-10). Establish embryo and juvenile constraints for RM-05 with the measured export, uptake and expenditure. Earlier technical results inform method and do not close a biological blocker.
4. Use matched integrated stage and cohort data to decide residual risk (RM-09) and biological adoption at the proper scope. Require independent clearance for all nine claims and the inherited engineering gates before proposing Core Readiness.

The causal evidence dependencies are explicit in the [authorization matrix](S6_4_PHASE4_IMPLEMENTATION_AUTHORIZATION_MATRIX.csv). The [backlog](S6_4_PHASE4_REMEDIATION_BACKLOG.csv) links the nine decisions to their accepted mechanism, parameter and conflict identities. An apparent fit to one final worker count cannot identify food, energy, fecundity, development and survival simultaneously. Record independent intermediate quantities and retain mismatch diagnostics before changing any dependent module.
