## Evidence and claim scope

The accepted audit covers 75 mechanisms, 99 state transitions, 249 distinct parameter records with 369 use contexts, 58 publications, 61 source contexts and 12 cross-domain findings. This is coverage of the frozen source and accepted evidence. It does not measure agreement between a future model and independent observations. The reviewed single-colony founding and early-worker active window still includes local foraging, social recruitment, trophallaxis, maternal provisioning and reproduction, brood care, development and survival, work and rest and limited autonomous 3D construction. Deferred phenomena remain outside claims for that window. Multi-colony architecture and the large-world product target retain their established scope. ## Required decisions before Core Readiness

1. Independently review Phase 4 and expressly authorize RM-01 if accepted. Run the old-budget arms, labeled sentinels, deterministic resume, conservation and inherited performance gates. Retain the original failed cohorts. Report the technical comparison independently of the biological question.
2. Accession the identified or newly collected matched data by biological lineage, freeze development and held-out assignments and document uncertainty and error limits. Start each biological package only after its data, causal prerequisites and implementation scope receive separate approval.
3. For each claimed mechanism, compare predeclared held-out endpoints with original groups, controls and censored or failed cases. Meet the engineering gates and the scientific comparisons. Record an explicit adoption decision for the proposed tested conditions and a separate default-model authorization where relevant.
4. Clear all nine roots within the accepted active window and integrate them using frozen observability, resource conservation, long-run, recovery and controlled performance evidence. The integrated comparison must check intermediate flux and behavior as well as colony counts. Return for a separate Core Readiness decision.
