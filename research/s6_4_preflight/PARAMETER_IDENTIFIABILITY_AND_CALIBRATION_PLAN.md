# Parameter identifiability and calibration plan

This review evaluates the 249 distinct parameters and rules in the accepted Phase 2 audit. The [parameter table](S6_4_PHASE3_PARAMETER_IDENTIFIABILITY.csv) carries each current value, unit, declaration and source span, mechanism, blocker association and provenance. The [evaluation rules](S6_4_PHASE3_PARAMETER_EVALUATION_RULES.csv) give 40 explicit scientific assessments covering all 110 Phase 2 measurement, sensitivity or non-identifiability priorities. The remaining 139 parameters keep their input or engineering roles. The existing 369 use records and 25 context-dependent applicability decisions remain linked. All current values retain F and `ENGINEERING_FIXTURE`.

## Feasibility at the currently accepted evidence level

| Status | Keys | Decision rule |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| `DIRECTLY_ESTIMABLE_NOW` | 0 | The measured quantity, conditioning, units and parameter semantics align directly and provide a credible estimate. Measured treatment inputs are classified separately. |
| `DERIVABLE_WITH_DECLARED_ASSUMPTIONS` | 1 | A scoped parameter can be derived from an observed endpoint after documenting the approximation, conditioning and units. |
| `REQUIRES_ADDITIONAL_DATA` | 28 | There is a measurable target, but the accepted data miss a necessary quantity, condition, stage, timing or sampling level. |
| `CURRENTLY_UNIDENTIFIABLE` | 56 | The current parameter or rule is latent or is entangled with other mechanisms in the available endpoints. A different measurement, structural simplification or explicit fixed reference is needed. |
| `ENGINEERING_OR_EXPERIMENTAL_INPUT` | 164 | Preserve a declared input, ablation, numerical or software setting. Assess the behavioral consequence and numerical fidelity where relevant. |

The 26 empirical priorities give one conditional derivation and 25 further measurement requirements. Of the 46 sensitivity priorities, three require context-matched detection or contact data, 18 have no identified current coefficient, and 25 are controlled policies, numerical or representation choices. All 38 Phase 2 non-identifiable priorities retain that disposition. Phase 2 priority and Phase 3 feasibility answer different questions. An experimental radius or temperature can be measured accurately while the behavioral response to it remains unknown.

The 25 shared context-dependent keys retain each original `NO` or `NOT_APPLICABLE` judgment in `UsePlansJson`, with accepted mechanisms, domain and explanation. An option can be a fixed ablation in one analysis and a test of a behavioral consequence in another. Compare outcomes under the declared options. Match known geometry, supply, starting stage and cohort as inputs. Keep empirical estimates and engineering ranges separate. [Phase 2 context decisions](BIOLOGICAL_GAP_PRIORITY.md#parameter-dispositions)

For a parameter row, join `ComparatorSourceContextIds` to [data availability](S6_4_PHASE3_DATA_AVAILABILITY.csv) for actual species, assay, units, original precision, DOI and locator. Those selected comparators help interpret the endpoint. `AcceptedSourceContextIds` preserves the original parameter citations. The plan's measurement and holdout links can extend beyond an existing parameter's accepted associations where they represent a proposed test of a downstream effect. `PlanningOnlyBlockerIds` identifies these links explicitly. Each `VAL-BR-xx` is an acquisition requirement until a matched dataset and a frozen independent split exist.

## Identification and range workflow

1. Define the estimand and units at the same stage and observation level as the source. Align actual chamber temperature, exposure, act boundaries and elapsed-time origin. Preserve the difference between a direct measurement, a related proxy, a published aggregate and a model outcome.
2. List the other state and parameters that can change that endpoint. Use held-fixed initial conditions and complementary measurements to separate the effects. Before an eventual fit, test whether distinct admissible parameter sets produce observably different predictions. Use exact invariances where available, or local sensitivity rank and profile curves under the declared candidate model. Simulation seeds characterize numerical precision and sensitivity.
3. Set a physically and experimentally admissible range using measured bounds and uncertainty, or documented scenario geometry, reserve constraints and recording resolution. Disclose all conversions and assumptions. Missing empirical bounds stay marked. Retain current controls as explicit sensitivity arms. A broad prior or a good aggregate fit alone supplies no identification.
4. Prefer an effective observable rule or fix a reference scale when several current fields vary together. Separate intervals for observation and numerical stepping from measured physiological or behavioral rates. Document any unobservable term fixed for interpretability or engineering and test downstream sensitivity.
5. Freeze candidate selection and calibration sources before receiving withheld outcomes. Compare the agreed outputs, failure cases and conditional strata on independent groups. Cross-validation within exposed sources can guide development, while `VAL-BR-xx` groups determine independent validation. ## Priority couplings and usable quantitative evidence

| Process | Measured counterpart and practical separation |
| --- | --- |
| BR-02 delivery and uptake | B08 and N01 support contact occurrence and bout timing in their respective definitions. Positive receiver dose and absorbed component mass need matched time series. A pulse that is limited by crop or headroom reveals little about its configured rate. With an unsaturated pulse, mass allowance depends on rate times interval, so endpoint mass alone cannot resolve kinetics. Measure receipt and crop-to-body time independently before replacing a pulse with an effective uptake model. |
| BR-05 task time, power and hydration | In a budget of the form `E_used = b * elapsed_time + sum(delta_act * time_act)`, active program residence cannot identify actual active time or the power increment. Separate measured resting power and disjoint timed acts by caste, cohort and mass, then constrain activity effects. In the model's units 1 nJ/ms equals 1 µW. A conditional oxygen conversion from N27 or B05 needs the substrate assumption and matched instrument and stage. It supplies a scale comparison and leaves the shared lineage coefficient unresolved. Water-loss period is a numerical setting. Loss rate needs independent hydration measurements. |
| BR-04 development | B06 Table 2 p. 169 provides a condition-specific egg mean near 24.7 °C. Converting days to ms and replacing a live-egg constant timer with that mean is a declared cohort approximation. Larval time in the Core starts at nutritional eligibility and the published prepupal plus pupal endpoint does not isolate the Pupa field. Retain cold nonprogression and the difference between laying-to-hatch and flight-to-first-worker. Fit any later temperature and nutrition law with separate stage landmarks and observations under multiple admitted active conditions. |
| BR-01 reserves and reproduction | First-worker egg exports, actual body reserves and maternal energetic dissipation require separate measurements. A simplified budget with initial reserve, absorbed energy, maintenance and export can give the same time to depletion when intake and costs compensate. Under constant unfed conditions the lifetime can constrain the ratio of usable starting reserve to cost. Laying and emergence totals alone leave absolute reserve and cost unidentified. Use reserves and timed allocation separately before replacing the existing local guard. |
| BR-03 active care | Positive scans combine selection, duration and the observation window. B01 intervention and B02 count evidence constrain their own outcomes. The recency law, action probabilities, pickup duration and route speed need separately observed opportunities and acts. R1C's timed loaded runs give a trained-worker scale with two workers and repeated trips. They leave queen, apparatus and handling effects open. |
| BR-06 and BR-07 search and recruitment | Overlapping B2-F01/F03 tracks have one data lineage. Condition on actual search and cue availability for trajectory or decision comparisons. Deposits, effective field scale, threshold and response can compensate in choices. Fix or measure the reference cue scale before fitting response. P15's 47 min behavioral e-folding estimate yields `47 * ln(2)` min for that fitted proxy only. It cannot set the integer field's decay independently. |
| BR-08 and BR-09 survival and excavation | A censored survival or development count conflates exposure, body state and stage progression. Independent cause and exposure measurement precedes a background hazard. Excavated volume combines initiation, opportunities, work duration and transport. Use substrate, work and mass trajectories, and separate geometric resolution from actual access. Published 2D shape and restricted CT data constrain the hypotheses without identifying a software contact threshold. |

The first package uses the single derived egg-stage candidate and preserves the comparator and negative arms. Its independent cohort is still missing. No parameter fit, new effect estimate or new biological test was executed for this phase.
