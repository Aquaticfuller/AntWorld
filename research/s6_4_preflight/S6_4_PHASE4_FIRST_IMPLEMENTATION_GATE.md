## A | Egg Timing Technical Assay

Use B06 Methods pp. 166–168 and Table 2 p. 169. Its nominal 25 °C regime used water access without food and had mean egg temperatures near 24.7 °C. The egg rows report 16.7 ± 0.07 d for Borisovka (n=138) and 15.7 ± 0.08 d for St. Petersburg (n=130). Table 2 labels these uncertainties as error. The published counts do not resolve founder-level independence. Both summaries are exposed in the audit. Use each as a separate fixed-condition reference for an elapsed-time clock. Exact input conversion of those rounded means gives 1,442,880,000 and 1,356,480,000 ms. Keep the 2 d long-term control (172,800,000 ms) and the seconds-scale header fixture in their original roles. The conditional mean is the supplied assay duration. The candidate does not model a thermal curve, within-cohort variability or survival. [B06 primary source](https://doi.org/10.1023/B:JOEY.0000033808.45455.75) [Accepted comparison](EVIDENCE_CONFLICTS.md#b1-c05--development-time-and-diapause)

The first change may add the proposed `cpp/apps/antworld_s6_4_egg_timing.cpp`, an app-local profile resolver and test target, and the build wiring. `resolve_egg_duration` takes an explicit selection of the Borisovka Table 2 row, St. Petersburg Table 2 row or legacy comparator. Record reported temperature and population as provenance. Reject free temperature interpolation, mismatched source/population, unsupported conditions and invalid units or integer conversion before world construction. The runner sets `PhysiologyConfig::egg_duration` via `WorldRuntime::enable_physiology` for the initialized egg or queen. `execute_reproduction` already copies the maternal config for a funded egg. Read the effective value through `WorldRuntime::query_physiology`. Use the committed `AntSeeded` or `AntBorn` for entry and `StageChanged` or `AntDied` for the outcome. Verify egg → larva and egg → dead resolution through the existing `next_physiology` and `execute_physiology` rules. Death retains precedence when depletion and hatch fall at the same boundary. The persisted configuration and stage progress already live in the checkpoint codec. The profile and provenance are kept in a run manifest. The production runtime, biological defaults, schema, event types, ingestion, maternal funding and post-egg development stay frozen in this package. ## B | Biologically Viable Egg Development

Before any physiology or default-model change, obtain actual egg-age and population records at measured temperature/moisture, usable embryo reserves, energy demand and water retention/loss by stage, and linked hatching, nonprogression and death outcomes. Use direct measured egg quantities or a separately justified observable effective compartment. Adult and larval crop kinetics do not identify embryo uptake. Treat nutrition effects on the queen and larvae separately from embryo metabolism. Establish actual egg exports through BR-01, supply and uptake for the interacting stages through BR-02, and condition- and stage-specific rates through BR-05. A prospective cohort can record several of these endpoints. Shared cohorts remain one biological lineage.

Freeze a model and admissible conditions after calibration, with independent `VAL-BR-04` foundress clusters. Compare hatch intervals conditional on observed hatch and full-cohort hatch/death incidence with matched cadence and censoring, using the independently justified margins from Phase 3. Preserve unknown and competing deaths. Satisfy both endpoints before recommending any viable warm-egg claim. Broader development additionally needs later-stage and nutrition/temperature validation in the admitted active envelope. Keep the cold/diapause context and the source population boundary explicit. No initial egg energy increase, cost reduction, disabled mortality or input adjustment is accepted as a substitute for that evidence.
