# Independent biological validation protocols

The protocol index contains one design per open root blocker. Each source link carries its accepted DOI, page or section, figures or tables and original sample unit. The source register preserves the actual temperature, humidity, stage and treatment information and records unknown conditions. The nine `VAL-BR-xx` entries are prospective holdouts with no matching independent observations registered for the full protocol. Use the [execution and uncertainty plan](BIOLOGICAL_VALIDATION_PLAN.md) for pre-registration and scoring. ## BR-01 | Founding reserve and reproductive allocation

**Comparator and initialization.** B03, journal pp. 398–400 and Appendices S1–S2, follows separately housed foundresses in a diet and disturbance design at 24.5 ± 0.5 °C. Use the 240 recorded queen endpoints, including four death annotations, for a development comparison of offered diet and day-49 outcomes. B01 §2.2 and Fig. 4a support a conditional larval-context contrast. Its established-stage group bounds transfer to early colonies. FT01 Methods, Table 1 and Figs. 5–9 show reserve-related tissue observations at 25 °C in destructively sampled unfed founders. B05 sexual-stage calorimetry is context for units and compartments. A candidate assay starts each founder at recorded stage and exposure time with observed body composition or a predeclared distribution conditional on measured mass, temperature, moisture, feed and brood. Keep access to workers, offered food, acquired food and maternal uptake as separate inputs or observations. [B03](https://doi.org/10.1093/biolinnean/blad149) [B01](https://doi.org/10.1111/1365-2435.14536) [FT01](https://doi.org/10.20362/am.013003) [B05](https://doi.org/10.1007/BF00299234)

**Outcomes and release.** Compare reserve trajectories by component, time to first oviposition, repeated actual egg output, viable brood and survival. For recorded repeated counts use queen-level blocking with a count model or block bootstrap. For first laying use interval-censored time and treat queen death before laying as a competing event. Include queens with zero output, preserve documented exclusions separately and keep survival-conditioned terminal summaries explicit. Independently sample a new founder/early-worker cohort for `VAL-BR-01`, with queen and shared natal or rearing blocks where known. Freeze precision bands for egg counts and timing using observed scoring repeatability and condition contrasts. Use reserve-measurement repeatability for flux margins. Diagnose incorrect food delivery, assimilation, maintenance or egg export before changing oviposition. Existing sources constrain direction and magnitudes in separate contexts. They leave the joint flux and reproductive comparison open.

**Instrumentation.** Record individual pre/post allocation body stores, assimilated nutrient doses, egg export and rejection reason at real reproduction boundaries with stable maternal and egg IDs. Sample reserve trajectories from read-only snapshots. Event-level sampling is bounded by actual attempts and observations. Verify event schema/size and replay or retain the decomposition in an externally versioned sidecar.

## BR-02 | Recipient selection, transfer and uptake

**Comparator and initialization.** B08 Methods, Table 1 and the event supplement describe five source-colony runs of queenless and broodless groups under deprivation then sucrose at 22 ± 3 °C and 60 ± 5% RH. N01 pp. 178–181 and Tables 1–2 describe fed-to-starved worker bouts from four colonies and separately report forager meal size. The mouth-contact criteria differ. R1L, pp. 154–158 and 161–164, observes regurgitation in brood-carrying contexts. B07's isotope study in *L. neoniger* cannot furnish a focal dose. For a matched experiment initialize the actual group/recipient stages, fasting or feeding history, known component dose, access windows, geometry and measured storage state or ranges. Scope a worker-only comparison to its own composition. A separate queenright early-colony comparison must observe queen and brood provision and uptake. [B08](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-019-52019-6) [N01](https://doi.org/10.1007/s00040-010-0133-y) [R1L](https://www.dictionnaire-amoureux-des-fourmis.fr/Publications/1981-Lenoir-Sociobiol.pdf)

**Outcomes and release.** Resolve the stages opportunity → contact → compatible → selected → transferred component mass → absorbed by time. For each source-colony block compare receiver-specific cumulative probability of a positive gift, conditional dose by component, time to transfer or uptake and total body intake in a declared window. Use a two-part analysis for gift incidence and positive dose and a competing-risk time analysis when death prevents transfer or uptake. Include unsuccessful interactions, ineligible contacts, zero gifts and administratively censored recipients as defined by the observable. Bound missing traces with explicit completeness sensitivity. Freeze mass margins from instrument/assay resolution and biologically meaningful change in supply. Freeze timing and incidence margins from repeated groups. Use published oral-bout comparisons for development and `VAL-BR-02` for independent colonies with matched transferred and absorbed quantities. Failure at one stage diagnoses access, local choice, act completion or kinetic assumptions separately. Ordinary and adequate-access simulation arms and scarcity outcomes are controls for diagnosis. [Historical maternal supply](../../S6_3_1_RESULTS.md) [Scarcity](../../S6_2_RESULTS.md)

**Instrumentation.** Record opportunity and candidate filtering only in the opted-in validation reader. Record committed transfer amounts, identities and absorption by time and component. Join sampled compatible contacts to committed events without treating absent records as zeros. Keep a bounded output buffer and measure scan/ledger size and restore cost in admitted dense workloads.

## BR-03 | Active care and completed retrieval

**Comparator and initialization.** B01 §§2.1–2.2, Fig. 1c and Data S1 score active handling in the first 10 s of each minute and manipulate worker access, addition and removal. Use exact intervention and recording time, caste, stage, brood treatment and observed geometry. Maintain the Methods versus deposited scan and removal denominators. B02 §§2.2–2.3, Fig. 1 and Dryad pair own and donor brood within each queen or worker trio in a 50 mm dish with a familiar tube. Match first-generation trios separately from later trios. Use the known temperature and humidity limits, time origin, count and exposure. Freeze an admissible brood-placement envelope before comparing. R1C trained loaded paths support motion scale under their own apparatus. [B01](https://doi.org/10.1111/1365-2435.14536) [B02](https://doi.org/10.1002/ece3.4475) [B02 paired data](https://doi.org/10.5061/dryad.qm114cj) [R1C](https://doi.org/10.1163/156853951X00304)

**Outcomes and release.** Compare per-queen positive scan fractions and arm differences with queen and any tray/colony clustering. Compare per-group 600 s retrieval counts and paired origin effects, with a group-level bootstrap or a paired count model. Time-to-event evaluation uses the same marked act on both sides. Preserve predeclared observation limits, failed approaches, interrupted custody and zero retrieval. For loss to follow-up record the last observation. Keep the old first-retrieving marker and committed placement distinct. Freeze count and scan bands from repeated biological scoring and meaningful group differences, and timing bands from annotation precision and repeatability. The exposed B01 and B02 comparisons are development data. `VAL-BR-03` needs new observed queen and trio units with matched scans, anatomy/assay context and timed successful placement. On mismatch inspect opportunity, handling definition, response to worker access and elapsed motion before modifying a general care probability.

**Instrumentation.** Export active action intervals, assigned role, contact, pickup, carrying, placement and outcome with entity identity. Build exact first-10-s scans and administrative windows outside the scheduler. Keep sampled care traces separate from full fact storage and measure observer overhead at dense care scale.

## BR-04 | Active thermal and nutrition-dependent development

**Comparator and initialization.** B06/B3-P05 pp. 166–172, especially Table 2 p. 169, reports first-brood stages and temperature treatments for two founder populations. The nominal 25 °C arm has actual stage-specific means. The egg rows near 24.7 °C are the first package's reference for the condition-specific cohort mean. Use known elapsed time from laying, true founding history, measured constant or observed temperature at the brood, substrate and moisture, and documented feeding. Under unfed water-accessible founding, do not replace unknown egg reserves with a nominal adult budget. An egg-only timing assay and a viable developmental progression assay have separate evidence requirements. B01 Fig. S4 is time from nuptial flight to first observed worker and includes laying latency and cadence. B07 counts or diet treatments constrain feeding context and cannot identify a stage clock. [B06](https://doi.org/10.1023/B:JOEY.0000033808.45455.75) [B01](https://doi.org/10.1111/1365-2435.14536) [B07](https://doi.org/10.1002/ece3.6520)

**Outcomes and release.** Record individual stage intervals and queen-clustered cumulative incidence of hatch, stage progress and emergence with competing death. Use interval-censored stage or multistate comparisons at the real observation cadence. Keep cold nonprogression and stage landmarks that cannot be aligned explicit. Compare an estimated egg duration conditional on hatching along with unconditional hatch probability and timing from the full enrolled cohort. Set timing margins from the biological recording cadence, between-queen variation and the smallest discrepancy meaningful for founder progression. Set distinct failure-incidence margins. Freeze these before `VAL-BR-04` release. Both B06 population summaries are already exposed. The holdout needs independent queens and observed cohorts, matched warm temperature and nutrition and linked event intervals. A broader claim requires independent coverage of additional admitted active regimes and feed conditions. If a fitted clock misses the cohort, check clock origin, population, nutritional eligibility, death and measured temperature before considering a new rate law.

**Instrumentation.** Join committed egg birth, stage change and death to brood and queen IDs, the observed stage time origin and assigned assay regime. Write a run manifest that records resolved timing and data provenance. Reuse committed facts for duration extraction. Measure event density, snapshot growth and resume determinism in a longer stage window. [First implementation package](S6_4_PHASE3_FIRST_REMEDIATION_PACKAGE.md)

## BR-05 | Time use, task selection and state-dependent expenditure

**Comparator and initialization.** B3-T01 pp. 214–216 and 225–230, Figs. 1 and 6, reports observation series on five young colonies and tagged workers. B3-T06 separates aggregation and resting-site choice. B3-T09 pooled age and role classes support cohort contrasts without individual longitudinal transitions. N27 Table 1 distinguishes the Clark 20 °C and Sable room setups. B04 Table 1 and its supplement use mass-adjusted respiration and separate housing and chamber temperatures. Keep sexes/castes, body mass, age/cohort, diet, observation schedule and activity state explicit. Set observed resting or active exposure conditions by assay, with similar contact and role opportunities. [B3-T01](https://doi.org/10.1111/j.1439-0310.1983.tb00088.x) [B3-T09](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-022-26515-1) [N27](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jinsphys.2024.104698) [B04](https://doi.org/10.1111/mec.70127)

**Outcomes and release.** Compare actual active/inactive bout fractions, transitions conditioned on eligible opportunities, and matched oxygen or usable power and water flux. Treat within-colony observations and repeated workers as nested. Fit or compare a multistate model with dwell times or stratified group summaries. Estimate resting and activity-related costs on actual measured intervals after accounting for mass, temperature and conversion uncertainty. Handle recording gaps as missing intervals and incomplete bouts as censored. Death ends the risk set with recorded cause. Freezing the ethogram, time-resolution and energy repeatability determines separate margin bands. Keep the instrument, cohort and gas conversion separate when transferring a rate. `VAL-BR-05` requires independently observed colonies and matched activity/flux, not pooled samples treated as individual time series. Inspect role versus completed act, budget unit errors and dwell/eligibility before changing task weights.

**Instrumentation.** Export start/end of committed acts, movement/waiting, role assignment, eligible switches and exact energy and hydration deltas. Compute actual activity duration at the observation layer. Avoid per-frame facts, measure sampled trace cost and bound any physiological observation retention.

## BR-06 | Local discovery, food collection and individual return

**Comparator and initialization.** B2-F01 Dataset S1 reports movement on flat canvas at 26 °C and 50% RH among its assays. B2-F03 Data and supplemental tables reuse 69 horizontal tracks and add light treatments. Keep both sources in a shared allocation group for the overlapping trajectories. B2-F04 §§2.1–2.3 and Tables 1–2 observe food acceptance and intake after exposure, and B2-F05 §2 and Fig. 1 separate intake and loaded behavior. Predeclare unmarked arena geometry, start poses, barriers, light, temperature, humidity, experience, cue availability and actual food positions/masses. The simulator must discover food under local information. Keep unknown routes and failed opportunities unobserved by the actor. [B2-F01](https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0076531) [B2-F03](https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0327957) [B2-F04](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jinsphys.2014.03.010) [B2-F05](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.crvi.2008.10.005)

**Outcomes and release.** Score path versus chord metrics consistently with sampling resolution, time to local detection and actual contact, conditional intake and mass, and loaded successful arrival. Compare discovery and return cumulative incidence, restricted latency and per-ant path statistics, clustered by source colony and repeated actor. Include no discovery and no return, right-censor at the matched observation stop, and keep death and tracking loss distinct. Match known cue/absence and blocked-path controls. Freeze distance, timing and probability bands based on tracking resolution, group repeatability and an ecologically relevant access contrast. `VAL-BR-06` requires independent new source colonies, independently assigned matched arenas, and complete search-to-return observations under the prespecified conditions. If load or contact differs, diagnose movement, access and ingestion before changing sensory reach or memory.

**Instrumentation.** Record local cue exposure and successful contact, intake, transit and depot arrival on opted-in actors. Derive tracks at an observer cadence outside biology. Measure time and size on long paths and preserve the order and snapshot consistency of movement facts.

## BR-07 | Scout-to-naive recruitment

**Comparator and initialization.** B2-P08 pp. 60–62 distinguishes recruiter and recruit journeys. B2-P09 pp. 560–562 and Tables 1–3 uses 1, 4 and 8 days of deprivation with 20 min scout windows. B2-P04 Exps. 1–2 gives acute branch or arena responses, B2-P03 S1 Table route learning after cue removal, B2-P12 Exp. 1 a tactile-transfer test, and B2-P15 pp. 752–756 ages marked trails. Set naive and experienced exposure, stock colony, scout number, deprivation, time since marking, cue preparation, orientation and opportunity in separate recorded treatments. Preserve the distinction between prepared cue and a real scout's trail. [B2-P09](https://doi.org/10.1007/s00040-011-0177-7) [B2-P04](https://doi.org/10.1007/s00040-026-01106-9) [B2-P03](https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0149720) [B2-P12](https://doi.org/10.1007/s00040-017-0583-6) [B2-P15](https://doi.org/10.1007/BF01201674)

**Outcomes and release.** Track mark incidence and count, independent naive activation/departure, first-choice and route response, discovery and return, and any defined delivery outcome. Use a colony-blocked treatment contrast, with ant/path/observation clustering and time to discovery or return. Count failures, censor incomplete observation and keep death or tracking loss visible. Report acute response and completed recruitment separately. Use biological repeatability and a prespecified meaningful change in actual naive discovery/return to set effect bounds. No-effect or weak arms remain part of the plan. `VAL-BR-07` needs independent source-colony groups with a matched cue intervention and complete causal-chain observation. Prior branch counts and decay estimates are development comparators. Do not use prepared-cue success to pass the colony-level endpoint. On failure check marking, cue persistence, availability, private experience, in-nest activation and actual search. [Archived scout controls](../../S2_2_RESULTS.md)

**Instrumentation.** Record cue origin and age, committed deposits, sampled cue at decisions, naive state and encounter, departure, return and delivery. Keep route samples local and bounded. Quantify extra observer load with trail/care disabled and enabled and replay determinism.

## BR-08 | Cohort survival, nonprogression and competing outcomes

**Comparator and initialization.** B3-P01 pp. 19–20 and Table 1 follows callow worker cohorts at 22 °C. Natal-colony workers are pooled in cohorts and cannot each serve as independent treatment units. B01 Data S1 records founding outcomes, and B3-P10 contains different soil and founding exposures. B3-P12 overwintering conditions lie outside the default active claim. Start from actual stage and age or time since entry with measured environment, intake, worker access, body reserves if observed and life-history cohorts. Match a validated active-window comparator before using its observed risk as a target. [B3-P01](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.exger.2016.09.008) [B01](https://doi.org/10.1111/1365-2435.14536) [B3-P10](https://doi.org/10.3389/fevo.2020.00076) [B3-P12](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jtherbio.2015.02.012)

**Outcomes and release.** Compare stage- and cohort-specific survival at a fixed active horizon and restricted mean survival. For stage transition use competing risks with left entry, interval-observed endpoints, right censoring and recorded known causes. Treat uncertain causes as unknown and report cause-classification sensitivity. Use full queen/cohort follow-up and colony-blocked comparisons. A rare-event or inadequate-sample arm yields uncertainty or an inconclusive status. Define risk margins from independently meaningful survival changes and calibration-side cohort repeatability. `VAL-BR-08` needs independent early-stage cohorts with linked food, water and temperature exposures and complete censoring. Existing groups cannot separate hazard from supply and clock differences in the Core. Diagnose maternal/juvenile funding, activity and intake before attributing discrepancies to background age risk.

**Instrumentation.** Record stage entry, individual/natal identity, actual exposure, last-seen time, right-censor status and death cause. Maintain a post-processed risk-set table sourced from immutable outcomes. Durable history should support cohort queries without adding per-tick life records.

## BR-09 | Limited autonomous excavation and coarse 3D structure

**Comparator and initialization.** B3-N01 pp. 434–436 and Tables I–III records worker/group changes in controlled moist substrate. B3-N02 2D experiments and B3-N03 50-worker sand/cohesive treatments constrain initiation and shape comparisons in their own dimensions. B3-N04 separates pellet pickup/deposition and above-floor construction. B3-N05 sections 2.1–2.6, Figs. 2–3 and Table S3 report repeated CT in soil columns. Soil is maintained at 70% field capacity, with different surface and mid-depth temperatures. Its 17 colony replicates and repeated CT frames have distinct levels. Raw CT data were restricted in the accepted review. Enter observed occupancy, three-dimensional substrate and material density, soil water, disturbance/space condition, observation schedule and independent early group size. Preserve 2D geometries and their endpoints in separate comparators. [B3-N01](https://doi.org/10.1023/A:1011163804217) [B3-N02](https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.0902685106) [B3-N03](https://doi.org/10.1098/rspb.2010.0176) [B3-N04](https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.1509829113) [B3-N05](https://doi.org/10.1111/1365-2656.14040)

**Outcomes and release.** Compare initiation and resumption, removed volume through time, carried/deposited mass and coarse connected occupancy with fixed physical scale and segmentation. Analyze repeated snapshots within colony or source colony block, with declared substrate and group contrasts. Keep trials without construction, censored initiation, partial scans, material collapse and interrupted runs. Missing scan means unavailable volume at that time. Select repeatable shape and connectivity metrics with resolution-based error, repeated measurement/segmentation uncertainty and biologically meaningful material/group contrasts. Use `VAL-BR-09` for independently observed early groups and matched volume/transport/occupancy time series. Existing group-size, pellet and CT results constrain possible mechanisms and are incomplete for this fit. On mismatch separate opportunity and work/transport from initial geometry, soil assumptions and scale-sensitive shape metrics.

**Instrumentation.** Export committed excavation, carry and deposit facts with physical volume or mass and actor/location. Compute reproducible occupancy and connectivity snapshots at the assay cadence. Measure geometry read time, memory and sidecar growth. Preserve material conservation and compare published-cut restore at each sampled checkpoint.
