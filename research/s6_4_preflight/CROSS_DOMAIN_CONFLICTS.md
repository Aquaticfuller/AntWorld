# Cross-domain conflicts and scope differences

This register reconciles the three accepted reviews at `4dbafd087592b45d1b29101d67d35b400e388516`. A **model discrepancy** compares a specified implementation or archived model output with a measured behavior. An **endpoint or context difference** explains why two results can coexist. A **source inconsistency** remains unresolved in the publication or deposited materials. An **identification gap** describes what the available data cannot discriminate. None of these labels changes an accepted batch record or assigns a Phase 2 priority.

## INT-C01 | Maternal and worker care have distinct observation rules

**Model discrepancy and endpoint difference.** B01 and B3-T08 are the same worker-access study. Worker addition suppressed queen care and removal restored it. On the first-10-seconds-per-minute active-care definition, the observed no-worker, added-worker and removed-worker means were 6.500%, 0.125% and 6.250%. The archived model means were 2.833%, 1.000% and 6.667%. The separated treatment allowed antennation, and hand feeding did not detectably suppress care in the tested setup. This leaves the contact cue, timing law and colony-stage dependence unresolved. The model's opportunity events, passive carriage and program occupancy would answer different questions. The temporary worker-contact memory in `resolve_care_wakes` supplies an executable hypothesis for queen care, with no measured time constant or worker task-switch coefficient. [B01 and frozen comparisons](EVIDENCE_CONFLICTS.md#b1-c01--queen-care-changes-with-worker-access-with-residual-scan-errors) [B3-C05](BATCH3_EVIDENCE_CONFLICTS.md#b3-c05--queen-care-plasticity-and-ordinary-worker-task-selection)

B02 used a 50 mm dish, a familiar tube and paired own- and foreign-brood trials. The archived line-layout model returned six items in 600 s for queens and first-generation trios. Deposited means were 3.772 and 4.850. The signed differences were +2.228 items (95% interval 1.791–2.684) and +1.150 (0.757–1.564). Archived first placements averaged 67.521 and 11.968 s. Deposited first-retrieving markers averaged 212.788 and 128.370 s. These markers have different definitions. Initial coordinates, full trajectories and per-act timing are unavailable. B02's origin null in this setting and R1L's conditional retrieval preference concern different settings. B02 contributed to model development. ## INT-C02 | Foundress care, reserves, provisioning and egg production

**Missing feedback and identification gap.** B01 Fig. 4a reports a sign reversal of the larval effect on egg counts between founding and established queens. The funded attempt in `execute_reproduction` uses resource and clearance guards without that interaction. FT01 documents tissue remobilization in unfed foundresses, with a separately labeled supplemental illustration from *Pachycondyla crassinoda*. Neither determines reserve flux into individual eggs. B05 reports calorimetry and respiration at stages relevant to production of sexual females. Its cumulative estimate cannot give a foundress's per-egg debit. B03 and B3-P03 are the same feeding experiment. The offer, subsequent day-49 offspring counts, and adjusted diet contrasts do not measure amounts delivered by workers, assimilated into body stores or allocated to eggs. The open provisioning and remobilization questions remain coupled to the predicted timing of first workers, which changes the set of caregivers. B08 and N01 record oral contacts and bout durations under different operational definitions. A bout, a forager meal and delivered component mass are distinct endpoints. In particular, the 0.6 µL melezitose intake estimate is neither maximal crop capacity nor a worker-to-queen transfer. B01's suppression experiment also supplies no such dose. Exact local deficits, crop input, depot selection and worker-task eligibility share program variables. Energy balance in a run establishes bookkeeping for those variables. ## INT-C03 | Stage timing, dormancy, age and survival

**Missing life-history mechanisms and endpoint difference.** B06 and B3-P05 are one experiment with population and temperature treatments. The 25 °C summaries give about 41.1 and 39.9 d for first-brood development in two populations. At 17 °C larvae entered diapause and the later durations are absent. B01's 62.88 ± 2.52 d first observed workers start from nuptial flight among 17 worker producers. This includes laying latency and the observation schedule. Fixed stage clocks, eligibility at eclosion and deterministic energy/water depletion cannot jointly express these endpoints. B3-P01 follows callow workers in pooled natal-stage cohorts. Its early/intermediate lifespan difference and unresolved late-age difference give no queen hazard. B3-P06 has one focal colony for the active/dormant comparison, where age and season co-vary. B3-P12 reports a worker overwintering effect and a queen temperature null in the retained groups. Acute tolerance, seasonal state, exposure survival and ongoing loss rates require different endpoints. The worker survival association after queen loss in B3-P12 rests on four queen deaths in the retained colony assay. B3-P08 manipulates a queen cue and measures worker ovary activation. Neither gives a colony-extinction timer after queen loss. B3-P07's 68/80 detections before and after flooding include eight gains and eight losses among the matched plots. This field endpoint with changed bait cannot distinguish natal colony survival, recolonization or detectability. ## INT-C04 | Local sensing, route learning and social recruitment

**Causal gap and model discrepancy.** `sense` exposes exact food availability within a bounded local query. Its extent and information content are engineering choices. Contact-conditioned feeding experiments do not establish that sensory reach, target knowledge or resource comparison. A stored route or connector records the individual's visits. Carthy's trained laden-worker arena supports visual cue use in a restricted setting, without showing that a route is stored as exact cells. The archive used by B2-F03 shares the 69 horizontal tracks from B2-F01. Their overlapping observations do not provide independent replication for persistence or search, and empty-walking tracks do not give loaded brood speeds. [B2-C01–C06](BATCH2_EVIDENCE_CONFLICTS.md#b2-c01--a-local-resource-query-is-stronger-than-the-measured-sensory-input) [B2-F01 reproduction](BATCH2_SOURCE_VERIFICATION_RECEIPT.md)

P04 observed immediate trail responses in the assay, including 348 of 398 first choices on the marked branch. P03 reports no detected learning benefit on the unmarked test after trail removal under its protocol. Immediate choice and later independent route memory are different estimands. P09 and B3-T04 describe the same scout/nestmate deprivation trials, with contact-associated exits and evidence for relay marking. The increasing proportion of scouts marking across deprivation levels was nonsignificant. P12's focal tactile experiment did not detect transfer of the correct turn. Contact-related activity and information about which direction to take can therefore differ. The accepted simulation's local following and correction findings leave colony-level benefit unverified. The `FollowTrail` label supplies no counterfactual evidence about a chosen step or activation of a worker inside the nest. P15's recipients used trails marked by a different colony in the assay, a direct counterexample to universal exclusion by exact colony identity. Broader acceptance rules and magnitudes are unresolved. ## INT-C05 | Pheromone chemistry, mark counts and cue lifetime

**Physical quantities and missing processes.** Gland extracts, scout marking incidence, mark count, choice probability and trail-cell integers use different measurement scales. Portha (B2-F08/B3-T07) and the P05/P06 experiments support context-sensitive decisions or mark counts. ## INT-C06 | Program choice, observed activity and energy expenditure

**Incompatible observation scales and energy identification gap.** The 97.6% active-task residence in the historical ecological run is a role-duration measurement. `Rest` may take food or recycle protein. A role with an active debit may also remain stationary. The short activity scans, aggregation and resting-site experiments in B3-T01, T05 and T06 do not measure sleep by arousal threshold or rebound. The tagged-worker results and B3-T09's pooled age × role molecular data do not give transition probabilities or a fatigue law. Food-associated head width and the null group-efficiency result in B3-T03 use different outcomes and levels of aggregation. Queen inhibition in B01 does not supply worker role weights. `resolve_autonomy_wakes` can include the same `nurse_weight` in ordinary exchange and integrated-care alternatives at one wake, conditional on eligibility. Thus task availability, dwell time and actual performed acts all affect a comparison with a field ethogram. [B3-C01–C05](BATCH3_EVIDENCE_CONFLICTS.md#b3-c01--program-occupancy-motion-clustering-and-rest) [Program selection](../../cpp/src/ecology.cpp#L197)

`expenditure` applies piecewise costs keyed to the model role. The exact identity 1 nJ/ms = 1 µW gives a 1,000 µW header basal value and a 1 µW long-term fixture override in the reviewed scenario. Conditional oxygen equivalents from N27 are about 34.34 µW for queens and 5.53 µW for workers with the reported assumptions and table inputs. B04 uses mass-normalized respiration and separate sample groups. Assay, caste, temperature, body mass and gas-to-energy uncertainty prevent transferring these estimates to one basal coefficient or an increment for all active tasks. Gross sucrose combustion and usable conversion into body energy are also separate quantities. [B1-C07](EVIDENCE_CONFLICTS.md#b1-c07--respiration-chemical-energy-and-the-source-cost-model)

## INT-C07 | Material movement and nest form

**Engineering boundary and missing biological validation.** The Core's material ledger, clearance, spatial index, SDF and cache invalidation support physical consistency. The host route API is separate from the local autonomous trace. Local reachable space, exact geometry, frontier choice and soil carriage can produce connected voids. ## INT-C08 | Environmental conditions, resources and season

**Scope and structural gap.** A finite scheduled food input represents controlled provisioning, as in the diet experiments. A configurable schedule does not supply a measured seasonal prey or aphid renewal law. The model's assay geometry metadata, supplied food amounts and constant temperature or water fixture contexts cannot stand for underground microclimate, individual thermal choice or hydric gradients. B3-N05 independently records distinct surface and mid-depth soil conditions, with water held at 70% field capacity. Its treatment-specific CT outcomes are colony measurements, with architecture, evaporation and population changes co-occurring. B3-P09 desiccation, B3-P12 winter exposures, and B3-P06 thermal response do not establish stage-specific evaporation, flux or seasonal resource inputs. Any transfer from these studies must preserve the exposure and the measured endpoint. ## INT-C11 | Shared parameter interpretation and applicability

**Context difference without source-value conflict.** All 99 shared keys agree on source expression, default or fixture value, units, scope, role class and anchors. For 25 keys, an accepted use calls calibration inapplicable while a different use records insufficient evidence. For example, `AntSeed.position` specifies a seeded condition in founding scenarios and influences real proximity and opportunity in a behavior assay. `SocialConfig.exchange_enabled` controls an ablation and also changes the set of candidate programs in ecological runs. `FoodMass.protein_ug` carries a component unit and may set an offered dose in a fixture. A known experimental input is different from a physiological response coefficient. These keys have one identity each, with each interpretation and its comparator retained in `UseAssessmentsJson`. No review establishes a calibrated value, and no shared-source citation establishes transfer between uses. [Global parameter table](INTEGRATED_PARAMETER_EVIDENCE.csv)

`WorldConfig.contact_distance_m` illustrates overlap without a change in calibration applicability. Its 0.002 m default gates interaction in several domains. Batch 1 compares oral and brood-care contacts, Batch 2 compares encounter-conditioned foraging, and Batch 3 compares social responses and construction encounters. Batch 2 and Batch 3 cite the same Mailleux scout assays through B2-P09 and B3-T04. The experimental outcomes are contacts and exits under different deprivation conditions. They supply neither independent replications between reviews nor a measured distance threshold for all interactions. ## INT-C12 | Compensation between mechanisms

**Identification gap.** The shared parameter reviews show that worker and brood counts alone cannot resolve the effect of changes in resource release, oral transfer, assimilation, role-dependent expenditure, stage development or maternal egg allocation. Greater supply or slower expenditure can delay depletion and change available worker time without representing improved delivery to the queen. Contact distance, candidate budgets and program dwell can change the number of eligible nurses, actual encounters and attempted excavation. Material clearance can independently change which attempts commit. The resulting aggregate curve cannot establish a causal pathway. A similar ambiguity holds for traffic on a learned route with both private and trail cues. The existing per-act, physical and population records make these alternatives distinguishable in principle if matched biological measurements exist. Engineering replay and multiple software seeds cannot supply the biological sample.
