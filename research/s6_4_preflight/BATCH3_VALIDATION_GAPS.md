## Parameter identifiability

All 139 in-scope records retain their frozen F/`ENGINEERING_FIXTURE` provenance. Some engineering fields have no biological calibration target. The biological comparators define observable units and candidate tests. None directly identifies a current coefficient in its simulated setting.

- Estimate activity observation and eligibility separately before estimating relative choice propensities. A count of nutrition actions cannot identify a task dwell duration when clock, opportunity and success can each change.
- Compare environmental input, energetic state, developmental delay and stage losses separately from adult worker count. Foundress and mature-colony exposures need their own risk sets.
- In a spatial comparison, fix the physical assay and vary numerical resolution independently. Include cross-checks of geometry, dry/wet material and opportunity endpoints without using the SDF or voxel size as a biological coefficient.
- Use the source records, measurements and applicability statements to choose independent validation datasets after this domain review. The later cross-domain integration and independent calibration/validation plan remain separate deliverables.
