# Biological validation execution and evidence plan

This plan uses the accepted Phase 2 commit `c3cb3d0c02940420fd9e0bb4028628c7a9b19a9b`. It defines inputs, comparison rules and evidence to retain when implementation and scientific testing receive authorization. All nine root blockers are open.

## Scientific claim envelope

The initial v1.0 biological acceptance envelope is a single *Lasius niger* founding or early worker colony in controlled, active, non-overwintering conditions. Match queen and worker stage, population where known, brood and cohort size, actual measured temperature and humidity, provision, geometry and substrate. The required claims still include autonomous local exploration and successful foraging, a matched scout-to-naive recruitment context, reproductive allocation, active brood care, nutrition and uptake, worker time use, development and survival, and autonomous limited 3D construction. A narrowly scoped first correction exercises one component of this envelope. The remaining claims keep their own clearance requirements. [Accepted scope](S6_4_PHASE2_READINESS_SCOPE.md) [Nine protocols](INDEPENDENT_BIOLOGICAL_VALIDATION_PLAN.md)

Winter and seasonal diapause outside the admitted thermal regime, multiyear weather and resource renewal, pathogens and disease, natural disasters, the complete sexual and mating cycle, mature or inter-colony ecology, sleep physiology and detailed underground microclimate require separate claims. Mark affected exposures out of scope before outcomes are inspected and report their inclusion status and causes. Keep nonprogression, attrition and interruptions inside an admitted treatment as outcomes. The long-term design retains multi-colony architecture, the expandable 24 × 24 m first world, true 3D and authoritative C++ simulation. ## Dataset intake and execution order

| Stage | Evidence to freeze and retain |
| --- | --- |
| Intake | Register source, DOI or archive version, access restrictions, immutable bytes hash, sample and exclusion definitions, units and clock origins. Record unavailable material explicitly. Assign stable pseudonymous keys for source colony, queen, brood cohort, actor, repeated observation and matched treatment. |
| Allocation | Group correlated observations before assigning datasets. Link natal sources, repeat actors, duplicate exports, repeated frames, shared controls and reused trajectories. An independent evaluator freezes validation group IDs and file hashes before fitting. Keep previously inspected development data and public outcomes marked as exposed. |
| Calibration | Freeze the candidate rule, source anchors, parameter definitions and units, scenario match, allowable ranges, processing code, random seed policy and optimizer or estimation rule if any. Estimate within the calibration partition. Report non-identifiability and poor fits with uncertainty. |
| Validation release | Freeze the exact model revision, parameter bundle hash, transformations, metric definitions, priority endpoints, match checks, uncertainty estimator and equivalence or prediction bounds before outcomes are released. Supply only admissible initialization and exposure metadata to the model. |
| Execution | Keep the unmodified comparator, candidate, all admitted conditions, all biological units and software seeds, emitted committed facts and failure classifications. Record tool versions, commands, input/output hashes, exit status and sample or run IDs. Retain a failure if the model stops, misses the event or dies. |
| Review | Independent review compares the frozen endpoints and accepts or rejects each scoped claim. Development, engineering correctness, performance and independent biological validation have separate result fields. A new model choice after opening validation outcomes returns those outcomes to development use. |

Use publication or dataset IDs as lineage, then the actual assignment unit for inference. Split at independent queen, source colony or experimental group as required by the protocol. When a stock colony supplies both arms, preserve that block and put the entire block into one partition. Place all repeated acts, scans, paths, cells and frames from an actor or group in the same partition. B01 and B02 are exposed development comparisons. Keep B2-F01 and B2-F03 together for split purposes because some trajectories are reused. Group aliases and duplicate archives consistently. Assign software seeds to Monte Carlo evaluation, repeatability or robustness. The biological sampling unit and sample size come from the observed animals. ## Comparison and uncertainty

- Score simulated outcomes at the experimental observation cadence and with the same event definitions and eligibility. Match actual input conditions before comparing. Report missing input provenance and structural mismatch as blocked or incompatible. Preserve reported source uncertainty with its original definition. A literature SD, SE, range and between-colony interval are different quantities.
- Freeze the effect or prediction target, the independent unit, paired or stratified contrasts and multiplicity policy. Report a two-sided 95% interval for each primary contrast when the independent units and method support it. Resample or permute whole independent blocks. A source with insufficient blocks gets descriptive summaries or an explicit inconclusive status. Quantify simulation precision over fixed seeds separately and propagate both sources of uncertainty for a prediction comparison.
- Set each admissible margin from the smallest discrepancy that would change the claimed biological interpretation. Estimate the measurement floor from calibration-side repeat scoring and recording resolution. If that floor exceeds the proposed margin, improve measurement or mark the endpoint unavailable for acceptance. Predeclare separate count, latency, incidence or composition margins and the relevant direction. For a claim of equivalence, require the entire prespecified confidence interval for prediction error to lie inside its admissible band. For a directional intervention claim, require an effect consistent in direction and magnitude with the matched experiment and report null and negative arms. Wide intervals are inconclusive.
- Account for failure, death, loss to follow-up and administrative cutoff explicitly. Use risk sets from the appropriate entry landmark and event flags with left/right interval limits. Distinguish right censoring, interval-observed events and competing deaths. Report completeness, causes, censoring sensitivity and conditional denominators. Keep a true zero measurement distinct from an unobserved value.
- Pin script and environment versions and leave a receipt for every future offline reconstruction or fit. This phase performs provenance and planning checks. Earlier raw data recomputations retain their original receipts. ## Observation and performance contracts

Derive outputs from committed facts, checkpoint state or a read-only observation stream. Keep decision opportunities, proposed actions, outcomes and resolved physiological quantities distinct. Bind observation records to world and actor identities and capture units, schema, observation schedule, censoring and completeness. Preserve no-op and failure cases where they carry experimental information. Use an explicitly opted-in analysis reader or bounded observer for dense event streams. Verify observation and replay do not change biological state, action order, random streams or conserved quantities.

Every future remediation compares the existing admitted workloads with observers disabled and enabled, including elapsed advance time, event counts, allocations, peak memory, history bytes, capture and publication stalls, restore time and query latency as applicable. Use the controlled-host admission and workloads where the frozen gates apply. Preserve time windows, work identities and all failed admissions. Document any extra analysis sidecar, format change or recovery rule before implementing it. Engineering results are reported independently of biological predictions.
