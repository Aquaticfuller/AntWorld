## Source inspection before literature review

| Mechanism | Current execution and information boundary |
| --- | --- |
| `SEN-01` | Surface cells index passability. Legacy mode requires the cell centre. Continuous mode checks the swept segment through cells and geometry. Both are world representations. |
| `SEN-02` | `sense` searches a bounded breadth-first neighbourhood of reachable surface cells. It reads exact nonempty food identities and first edges there. Continuous approaches can use the selected local source position. There is no detection probability, antennal contact model or food plume. |
| `SEN-03` | An ant reads decayed scalar values only for legal neighbouring tiles and only under its exact `ColonyId`. That is a software channel with no model of receptor response or chemical identity. |
| `SEN-06` | The wake dispatcher supplies no visual, vibrational or environmental sensor stream. Social contact queries belong to other task resolvers. They do not supply neighbouring worker trajectories, discoveries or traffic cues to `choose_behavior`. |
| `FOR-01` | Setup or a physiology program enrolls eligible adult workers. Initial entry starts unloaded at the actual depot with carry capacity. Home is supplied to the private trace and the initial heading is seeded. |
| `FOR-02` | Cargo at home triggers delivery, and cargo, Return mode or a trip/trace limit away from home triggers trace reversal. Without a legal reverse edge the proposal rests. Local food, memory and search are considered later. |
| `FOR-03` | Minimum reachable graph distance wins among local food cues. Uniform ties use physical cell ordering and per-actor RNG. Taste, concentration and reward quality do not enter the ranking. |
| `FOR-04` | A four-direction weighted draw favours new cells and straight continuation. The predecessor is normally excluded when alternatives exist. A single qualifying trail signal can permit reconsidering it. |
| `FOR-05` | A successful collection can snapshot one loop-erased outbound trace. The ant follows a legal successor in that private trace until expiry or invalidation and reverses the current trace to return. No landmarks or home-vector integration are present. |
| `FOR-06` | Collection validates stage, contact, stock, carrying room and loaded clearance. A single commit moves proportional bulk constituents into external cargo. The forager uses the step clock. Ingestion and trophallaxis are separate programs. |
| `FOR-07` | A physically reachable colony depot takes the entire cargo on delivery. An unloaded mixed-world return can also traverse the saved connector to the depot. |
| `FOR-08` | A movement proposal takes effect at resolution subject to position revision, elapsed speed, swept geometry and, in legacy planar mode, adjacency. There is no walking gait, turning cost or load-specific speed law. |
| `FOR-09` | A bounded search from the depot looks for a reachable surface exit using world height, quantised clearance and traversable neighbours. It stores one path per worker and checks each segment during traversal. It can examine geometry ahead within the search budget and therefore represents an engineering navigation affordance. |
| `FOR-10` | Ownership, wakes, phase ordering, action rejection and stopping are scheduler rules. A blocked action can be retried. The fact of a retry or a `Rest` proposal is not a physiological latency or adaptive search policy. |
| `PHE-01` | Successful cross-tile moves with pre-move food cargo add a capped increment to the destination tile. Connector and same-tile moves do not use this branch. There is no decision about whether to secrete or how much. |
| `PHE-02` | Stored tile values decay independently with elapsed time and a shared exponential law. Reads apply the cutoff and periodic maintenance erases empty entries. There is no diffusion, spatial spreading or physical dose. |
| `PHE-03` | A signal on a legal step adds a clipped normalized weight only after higher-priority choices. A visible signal on the drawn step determines the `FollowTrail` label, even in the zero-response control with the field still enabled. |
| `PHE-04` | Other workers can affect that field through actual deposits. There is no separate recruit state, copying of destinations, social-contact input to the foraging decision or trail-driven departure from the nest. Recruitment and group route formation remain outcomes to test. |
| `PHE-05` | Read-only summaries count traversals, recorded cues, collection/delivery and abstract field units. An observed cue can be counted without collection. Visited edges, cue discovery and deposited-minus-current units do not feed the next individual choice. |

The default planar policy has no global lookup for food by an ant. That boundary is narrower than biological perception. A reachable but occluded patch can be exposed through graph samples around a corner, and the decision reads exact local stock existence. The exact depot, shared colony key and connector search are separate affordances. Same-boundary workers choose from frozen observations before action proposals commit, which preserves execution order without simulating the time course of sensing.

## Full-text group A | Perception, movement and foraging

The Methods, Results and named figure/table sections were checked from the actual full text. These studies distinguish unloaded locomotion, recognition and route use, food contact, ingestion, and the choice to continue or return. The [source register](BATCH2_SOURCE_FULLTEXT_REGISTER.csv) gives populations, treatments, nesting of observations, precise pages and the reading scope.

| Source IDs | Primary work read | Result applicable to the audit |
| --- | --- | --- |
| F01, F03 | Khuong et al. on incline trajectories and Bonavita et al. on search trajectories and light | Freely walking workers show direction-dependent structure when frame and context are specified. F03 reuses F01's 69 horizontal tracks and adds a separate 60-worker paired-light assay. Reused trajectories count once as biological material. [F01](https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0076531), [F03](https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0327957). |
| F02, F09 | Carthy on visual direction and Bernadou and Fourcassié on substrate coarseness | Visual perturbation changes some trained returns, with the pooled white/red null retained. Mean walking speeds differed between fine and coarse sand with no demonstrated spontaneous texture choice. [F02](https://doi.org/10.1163/156853951X00304), [F09](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jinsphys.2007.12.001). |
| F04, F05, F08 | Detrain and Prieur on sugar sensitivity, Mailleux et al. on loads, and Portha et al. on food type and brood | Separate contact from drinking, and crop intake from cargo. A returning worker can drink at a second source. Food type and brood changed some observed actions. The experiments do not give a hidden-food detection radius. [F04](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jinsphys.2014.03.010), [F05](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.crvi.2008.10.005), [F08](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.anbehav.2003.10.016). |
| F06, F07 | Grüter et al. on conflicting information and Czaczkes et al. on complex routes | Workers use private information, with accuracy rising across visits. Route structure and trail treatment affect accuracy. Stored exact replay and abrupt expiry are modeling choices. [F06](https://doi.org/10.1007/s00265-010-1020-2), [F07](https://doi.org/10.1242/jeb.076570). |
| F10 | Devigne and Detrain on collective exploration and area marking | Weak branch biases can arise without food, and activity changes over time. The marks and conditions differ from food-trail assays. [F10](https://doi.org/10.1007/PL00012659). |

Direct values with interpretable units include F09's 2.63 and 2.21 cm/s means by substrate, F05's 51 ± 12 s first drinking bout and F06's 74.6% versus 95.3% correct branch choices after one and three visits. The first two compare realized motion or ingestion to engineering clocks and a ceiling. The third compares an assay probability to a deterministic remembered successor. None calibrates the existing numeric settings.

## Source access and checks on original measurements

ETH and UZH Swisscovery requests and the UZH publication index were used for discovery. Their logged catalogue responses do not constitute full-text access. The four user-supplied originals were opened and inspected. Bestmann's Wiley PDF carries the ETH Zürich access footer. The local hashes, DOI links, attempted routes, successfully read portions and unavailable supplements are recorded separately in the [access log](BATCH2_SOURCE_ACCESS_LOG.csv). The repository contains original summaries and sourced factual measurements.

- Khuong Dataset S1 was read again from its original archive. Use every tab-separated flat track, divide it into consecutive complete 1 s windows with 25 intervals at 25 Hz, sum the frame displacements and separately take the start-to-end chord. Across 5797 windows, calculate each track's two mean rates, then take the median of each across 69 tracks. Convert coordinates from m to mm. The resulting 50.643 and 38.133 mm/s depend on the metric and frame noise. They differ in definition from the prior atlas's one-second subsampling and net-over-track results. [Dataset S1](https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0076531.s005).
- For P03, the author supplement reports the unadjusted final unmarked contrast and the article reports the adjusted null. The workbook's cleaned data have 4930 bifurcation/trip rows from 260 ant labels within eight colony labels. Trip-seven blanks are not failures or successes. [P03 analysis supplement](https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0149720.s001), [P03 data supplement](https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0149720.s002).
- For P04, count rows and average `Proportion_Frames_MiddleZone` by path length and pheromone status in `Exp 1`. Counts for short/marked, short/solvent, long/marked and long/solvent are 26, 25, 25 and 26. The figure and workbook resolve the nominal counts in Methods. Sum `correct` by treatment in `Exp 2` to recover 348/398. For P06, group numeric `Total_phero_eye` by `phero_or_not` in S2 and count numeric segment records by runway in S3. Recovered means are 6.9004 and 7.4408 acts for marked and unmarked trials. These are descriptive reproductions. Neither author's mixed models were refit. [P04](https://doi.org/10.1007/s00040-026-01106-9), [P06](https://doi.org/10.1007/s00040-024-00995-y).
