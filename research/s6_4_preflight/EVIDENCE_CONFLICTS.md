# First-batch evidence conflicts and unresolved comparisons

The identifiers in this register join the mechanism, transition and parameter reviews. A contradiction concerns a reported observation and a specified executable model or measurement definition. An identification gap concerns a value or causal explanation that the available endpoints cannot resolve. Previous comparisons keep their frozen conditions and denominators.

## B1-C01 · Queen care changes with worker access, with residual scan errors

**Directional agreement with quantitative and causal gaps.** In B01, worker addition reduced active-care scans and removal restored them. The deposited focal means, as percentages of positive scans per queen, are 6.500 at 24 h without workers, 0.125 at 24 h after addition and 6.250 at 48 h after removal. There are 16 queen labels in each of these raw arms. The reviewed model gave 2.833, 1.000 and 6.667 with the same primary 50-scan calculation. The differences are −3.667, +0.875 and +0.417 percentage points. The first ten seconds of each minute score active manipulation or active relocation. Passive carrying, sampled Opportunity and total task residence are separate. The Methods mention 51 scans and 15 removal queens, while the used deposited rows have 50 scans and 16 removal labels. Preserve both descriptions. [B01 Methods §§2.1.4, 2.2.3–2.2.4 and Fig. 1c](https://doi.org/10.1111/1365-2435.14536), [Dryad](https://doi.org/10.5061/dryad.pnvx0k6wd), [frozen comparison](../s6_3_3_3/BIOLOGICAL_VALIDATION_AND_UNCERTAINTY.md).

In B01 §2.2.12 the mesh admitted antennation and blocked close interactions. Queens separated by mesh differed from those with worker access (p=0.001), with no detected difference from those without workers (p=0.79). The experiments with worker cuticular extracts, former worker occupancy and frozen workers also did not detect inhibition under those conditions. The reported Methods, figure caption and deposited mesh design use different group counts. The current law refreshes one timestamp on completed worker contact and reduces the new-care draw with a linearly decaying multiplier. The assay cannot isolate a single worker-to-queen cue from worker care of brood, actual nutrient transfer or contact timing. It identifies neither the six-hour horizon nor the shape or amplitude of inhibition. The hand-feeding manipulation did not produce a detectable main effect (p=0.29) or time interaction (p=0.17) on care. It did not quantify worker-to-queen dose. [B01 §§2.2.3, 2.2.9–2.2.12, 3.2, 3.6, Figs. 1b and 2b–c](https://doi.org/10.1111/1365-2435.14536), [care selection](../../cpp/src/care.cpp#L274).

## B1-C02 · Brood retrieval count error and marker alignment

**Quantitative discrepancy in the frozen line-layout assay.** The B02 re-extraction averages the two brood-origin trials within each queen or worker trio and preserves the 600 s no-event marker. The sample contains 79 queens, 70 first-generation trios and 50 later trios. Methods state 79, 67 and 50. The author's script distinguishes 70 first-trio labels, 67 matches to the queen cohort and 61 productivity matches. It also includes preliminary unpaired origin tests, separate from the paper's paired p-values of 0.5027, 0.5152 and 0.8146. Keep the grouping and estimand explicit. [B02 Methods §§2.2–2.3 and §3](https://doi.org/10.1002/ece3.4475), [deposited CSV and script](https://doi.org/10.5061/dryad.qm114cj).

Across origin-paired units, the 600 s retrieval means are 3.772 for queens and 4.850 for first trios. The reviewed line-layout model returned 6 in both groups. Previously archived signed count errors were +2.228 items (95% interval 1.791–2.684) and +1.150 (0.757–1.564). The first successful placement proxy in that model was 67.521 s and 11.968 s, versus deposited first-retrieving markers of 212.788 s and 128.370 s. Placement and the raw first-retrieving marker are not frame-aligned. The study specifies a 50 mm dish, a familiar tube and six larvae/pupae, but provides no exact initial coordinates or corresponding full pickup and placement trajectories. Layout sensitivity remains evidence about model uncertainty. B02 was used to develop the mechanism and is not a held-out biological validation. [B02 Fig. 1 and Dryad](https://doi.org/10.5061/dryad.qm114cj), [frozen comparison and spatial uncertainty](../s6_3_3_3/BIOLOGICAL_VALIDATION_AND_UNCERTAINTY.md).

## B1-C03 · Brood-dependent egg production differs by founding stage

**Missing feedback with a directly observed sign change.** In B01 Fig. 4a, larval presence reduced egg counts in founding queens and increased them in established queens. The study compared 18+18 founding and 15+15 established queens with workers absent in the manipulation. The time-by-larvae interaction was reported at p<0.0001 in each stage. The current attempt uses crop protein, body energy and water, a maintenance window and geometric clearance. It has no brood-conditioned or founding-stage-dependent egg-production feedback. The opposite biological effects cannot be reduced to a single multiplier on funded birth. B03 measures 49-day productivity after changing the offered diet and handling, not per-egg expenses or actual worker provisioning. [B01 §2.2.14, §3.8, Fig. 4a](https://doi.org/10.1111/1365-2435.14536), [B03 pp. 398–399](https://doi.org/10.1093/biolinnean/blad149), [funded attempt](../../cpp/src/long_term.cpp#L74).

## B1-C04 · Founding reserve remobilization and single-egg budgets

**Missing compartment and flux identification.** FT01 documents post-flight tissue remodeling in unfed foundresses at 25 C. At three-day sampling resolution, flight-muscle organization changes by days 6 and 9, loss of muscle contents is marked by day 18, and adipocytes expand by day 24. That evidence does not trace assimilated protein into an egg or larva. B05's dry-body calorimetry and approximately 689 J cumulative estimate for raising a flying sexual queen describe different compartments and a different endpoint. The fixed seed stores, 20 ug crop protein debit, 200 ug body-water debit, 0.5 J exported egg reserve and 0.01 J dissipation are engineering values. Long-term controls used a two-day maintenance window in the retained local guard arm, distinct from the zero header default. Early founding, active maintenance and mature-worker supply are separate. None of these papers resolves the cause or adequacy of worker provision in the historical 90-day and 180-day queen survival cases. [FT01 pp. 3, 6–13, Table 1](https://doi.org/10.20362/am.013003), [B05 pp. 20–23](https://doi.org/10.1007/BF00299234), [reference scenario overrides](../../cpp/apps/s6_3_1_fixture.hpp#L52), [preserved S6.3.1 results](../../S6_3_1_RESULTS.md).

## B1-C05 · Development time and diapause

**Timing scale mismatch and absent thermal response.** In B06 Table 2 at nominal 25 C the Borisovka and St Petersburg egg means were 16.7 and 15.7 days at actual 24.7 C. Larval means were 10.2 and 10.6 days at 24.8 C. The combined prepupal and pupal means were 14.5 and 13.6 days at 25.4 C. Total means were 41.1 and 39.9 days at 25.0 C. At nominal 17 C larvae entered diapause. Stage sample sizes count brood nested within founding queens. Current header durations of 30/60/30 s are test-scale defaults. The S6.3.1 example sets 2/3/2 days. The egg clock in that example is 14.7 and 13.7 days shorter than these two reported means, a scale difference under unmatched conditions. The larval clock begins after the model protein gate, whereas the paper measures elapsed development. The combined prepupa+pupa endpoint cannot isolate the model's Pupa timer. Temperature, geography and diapause are absent from the transition law. [B06 pp. 166–172, Tables 1–2](https://doi.org/10.1023/B:JOEY.0000033808.45455.75), [stage and rate defaults](../../cpp/include/antworld/core/physiology.hpp#L8), [reference scenario](../../cpp/apps/s6_3_1_fixture.hpp#L52).

## B1-C06 · Oral contact and resource transfer measure different quantities

**Direct transfer observations with no dose match.** B08 used five queenless and broodless groups, a four-day deprivation and sucrose. The mean observed event count was 99.0 ± 17.4 SD per run. Workers classified by observed ingestion accounted for 60.2 versus 38.8 events on average in the donor categories (p=0.02). Foragers by that definition are not the source Forage state. N01 reports 29 ± 28 s and 31 ± 27 s (mean ± SD) for fed-to-starved sucrose and protein worker bouts, with 66 and 52 bouts nested in groups. N01 uses a >3 s oral-contact criterion and B08 uses >5 s plus posture and minute sampling. N01's printed fitted stopping-rate intervals and unit note are internally ambiguous. Its 0.6 uL melezitose ingestion estimate (n=23) measures a meal, not maximal crop capacity or delivered mass. R1L separately scored regurgitation to a queen and to larvae, without component mass. None provides a conversion to the 100 ug request/limit, a 16-candidate cap, or a one-millisecond post-intake rearm. Exact recipient deficits, pure-protein depot recycling and Nurse-only autonomous giving remain hypotheses. [B08 Methods, Table 1 and event supplement](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-019-52019-6), [N01 pp. 178–181, Tables 1–2](https://doi.org/10.1007/s00040-010-0133-y), [R1L pp. 157–158](https://www.dictionnaire-amoureux-des-fourmis.fr/Publications/1981-Lenoir-Sociobiol.pdf), [nutrition action policy](../../cpp/src/physiology.cpp#L223).

## B1-C07 · Respiration, chemical energy and the source cost model

**Quantity and context mismatches.** N27 Table 1 gives adult queen and worker rest-context means of 6.15 ± 2.14 and 0.99 ± 0.23 uL O2/h in the 20 C Clark setup. The worker estimate uses pooled measurements. Sable estimates at 24–26 C use another instrument, so the two conditions do not identify a temperature slope. The N27 text gives 0.98 for the Clark worker mean where Table 1 gives 0.99. B04's queen 3.62 ± 1.23 nL/min/mg^alpha is mass-normalized, with different counts in Methods (6 queens and 22 pools) and Table 1 (8 and 24). B05's 5.18 ± 1.06 uL O2/h for adult sexual females is another life context. Keep all three cohorts separate. [N27 pp. 2–7, Table 1 and Fig. 3](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jinsphys.2024.104698), [B04 §§2.2–2.3, 4.1 and Table 1](https://doi.org/10.1111/mec.70127), [B05 p. 23, Table 2](https://doi.org/10.1007/BF00299234).

The source unit `1 nJ/ms = 1 uW` is exact. Using B05's assumed 20.1 J/mL O2 for a conditional scale check gives `power_uW = flux_uL_per_h * 20.1 / 3.6`. N27's Clark means then imply about 34.34 and 5.53 uW, versus the 1000 uW basal header default and 1 uW long-term worker example. This is an order-of-magnitude check under a substrate assumption. Program residence adds a fixed premium in the source and neither resting oxygen use nor gross body mass identifies that premium. The configured carbohydrate yield `1,000,000 nJ/ug` equals `1 kJ/g`. The magnitude of the solid-sucrose combustion enthalpy divided by molar mass is `5643.4 / 342.2965 = 16.49 kJ/g`. The fixture uses about 6.1% of that pure-sucrose combustion reference. That calculation does not identify the ant's in vivo absorption or ATP yield. [Physiology configuration](../../cpp/include/antworld/core/physiology.hpp#L8), [reference scenario](../../cpp/apps/s6_3_1_fixture.hpp#L52), [B05 energetic calculation](https://doi.org/10.1007/BF00299234), [NIST sucrose thermochemistry](https://webbook.nist.gov/cgi/cbook.cgi?ID=C57501&Mask=6F#Thermo-Condensed).

## B1-C08 · Dietary composition and congener tracer evidence

**Species and scale boundary.** B07 records colony growth and worker traits in *L. niger* under varied offered diets. The 1:16 protein:carbohydrate diet gave the lowest overall growth. Food preference and colony growth have different endpoints. The Fig. 7 pulse experiment is entirely *L. neoniger*. It used five enriched and three control colonies, sampled at 24/96 h, and did not support preferential larval nitrogen allocation relative to carbon. It stays `CROSS_SPECIES_EXTRAPOLATION`, Atlas C, and cannot calibrate the *L. niger* tissue threshold, allocation hierarchy or contact mass. [B07 §§2.2–2.5, 3.1–3.4, Figs. 4, 6–8](https://doi.org/10.1002/ece3.6520).

## B1-C09 · Transport preference, orientation and route timing

**Different experiments bound different mechanisms.** B02's origin test found no significant difference under its dish conditions. R1L detected differences in repeated and autumn choice tests, and described delayed adoption after transport. This is compatible with context dependence and does not establish a universal exclusion or acceptance rule. Carthy's directional perturbations show context dependence. The 12 complete normal-light returns in Tables II and IV, from two trained workers, yield 11.75–29.89 mm/s using distance in inches × 25.4 / elapsed seconds. The printed speed heading is inconsistent with the raw columns. Repeated paths are not independent workers. The model's preferred loaded worker speed is 4 mm/s and its header speed cap 10 mm/s. Path averages in a trained visual task can constrain the scale, while no fitted caste/load law follows. Exact search latency, pickup and placement times, queen loaded speed and the causal basis of one-site revisits remain unidentified. R1L's suggestion of a recruitment trail during brood retrieval is a hypothesis. [R1L pp. 155–157, 161–164, 172](https://www.dictionnaire-amoureux-des-fourmis.fr/Publications/1981-Lenoir-Sociobiol.pdf), [R1C pp. 290–296, Tables II and IV](https://doi.org/10.1163/156853951X00304), [inherited path transcription](../../experiments/s6_3_3_2/literature/carthy_loaded_returns.csv), [care motion defaults](../../cpp/include/antworld/core/care.hpp#L6).

## B1-C10 · Hydration and delivered nutrition under incomplete conditions

**Insufficient matched measurements.** Wet cotton, supplied water, RH metadata and offered diets document environmental inputs. They do not identify hydration capacity, individual evaporation, uptake flux, body water at death, sugar assimilation or larval uptake by instar. The current discrete loss period and fixed pulse rates carry no humidity or temperature law. No cause-specific death rate follows from an observed cohort survival fraction. The founding survival control and nutrient-deprivation assays constrain different parts of the model. [B01 §§2.1–2.2, §3.1](https://doi.org/10.1111/1365-2435.14536), [B03 experimental details](https://doi.org/10.1093/biolinnean/blad149), [B06 Methods](https://doi.org/10.1023/B:JOEY.0000033808.45455.75).

## B1-C11 · Missing biological stages and role differentiation

**Ontogenetic and task gaps.** B01 distinguishes newly eclosed workers, founding queens and established queens. B02 uses known first-generation age and a separate later cohort. R1L observes worker-level overlap and variation in carrying and foraging. Neither initial care context metadata nor inherited worker parameters constitute age-dependent rules, larval instars, a distinct prepupal state, callow maturation or sexual caste production. The engineering actor-ownership contract remains useful for deterministic switching and does not imply permanent worker specialization. [B01 §§2.1.3, 2.2 and Fig. 4a](https://doi.org/10.1111/1365-2435.14536), [B02 §2.2](https://doi.org/10.1002/ece3.4475), [R1L pp. 155–158, Table 1](https://www.dictionnaire-amoureux-des-fourmis.fr/Publications/1981-Lenoir-Sociobiol.pdf).
