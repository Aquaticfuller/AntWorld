The registered evidence supports the occurrence of directed oral exchanges and some context-dependent worker activity. The model's availability screens, exact access to receiver deficit, fixed cap, absence of oral-bout duration, discrete uptake rates, energy yield and ordering of exhaustion versus uptake remain model assumptions or engineering fixtures in their recorded contexts. The early 48 h inactivity and 5.9976 h median carbohydrate lag are measurable properties of this fixture. A fitted contact duration, a donor meal volume or a colony growth curve cannot calibrate these quantities. The depot, ordinary and scarcity runs show how resource delivery, task engagement, gifts and uptake can separate. In ordinary-32, all six queens die by day 90 despite committed gifts to queens and retained external carbohydrate. All six depot-32 queens are alive at day 180, with an earlier 90-day difference in absorbed carbohydrate and delivered doses. The depot also changes access and supply, and activity and expenditure vary over the simulated trajectories. This supports diagnosis of a missing or delayed pathway within the fixture. There is no matched empirical estimate of these survival or intake differences. ## Minimum candidate for separate scientific approval

**Hypothesis.** A prespecified, visible receiver solicitation signal at a close encounter improves held-out prediction of the direction and choice of an oral interaction beyond measured exposure, donor state and the available alternatives. The existing fixture highlights why the experiment must start with a documented opportunity to act. It also motivates measuring the delay between delivery and usable stores separately from choice.

1. Use a worker development cohort with a protocol bridge to B08 and N01. Predeclare the observation window and coding rules. Track the available partner set, position, proximity, initial donor stores, stage, caste, worker task context, actual cue visibility, recipient identity, no-choice observations, contact onset/offset and observation loss. Preserve the repeated events within individual and source colony. Source-colony identity is the unit for split and uncertainty.
2. Independently measure component dose in paired pre/post observations with a detection limit. Distinguish observed zero, below detection, interrupted or rejected attempt, missing and censored. Dose and choice use separate outcomes. After a quantified transfer, measure crop and body compartments in a timed sequence, with observed onward transfers, respiratory loss, water loss and competing death. Queens and larvae need condition-matched strata in founding or early colonies before a worker result can be applied to them.
3. Predeclare a local-choice model with geometry, donor state and an outside/no-action option. Test one prespecified cue term with source-colony blocking. Use blinded instrument repeatability and between-colony variability to fix a useful improvement margin and holdout size before estimating the cue. Keep the existing mass, reach and compartment constraints. B08, its Bles-linked data and N01 are development evidence. Hold out independently acquired source colonies and whole repeated trajectories.
4. For a later proposal, require held-out prediction improvement and appropriate calibration against the predeclared interval, with preserved zero and competing outcomes. A cue without reproducible improvement, or confounded with encounter availability, leaves the current modeling hypothesis unconfirmed. If the dose and body time course remain unresolved, keep those coefficients uncalibrated. Evidence about one link cannot approve simultaneous changes to task selection, cap, reserve, uptake and mortality.
