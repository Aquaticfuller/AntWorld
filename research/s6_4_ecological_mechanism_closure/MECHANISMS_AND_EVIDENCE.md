## Physical time and excavation

`WorldRuntime::resolve_wakes` and `resolve_excavation_wakes` schedule walking at `ceil(1000 × segment length / speed)` milliseconds, with a minimum of one millisecond and the world's speed limit. The configured speeds are 0.005 m/s unloaded and 0.0025 m/s for a positive food load or owned soil parcel. Body clearance remains actor- and load-specific. The entire swept segment is checked against the current authoritative solid and ordered edits. Food pickup and delivery retain their existing handling clock. Excavating a cut retains the configured 30-minute workload. An autonomous owned depot drop uses the separate 5,000 ms handling clock at both scheduling and commit. Direct soil commands retain their inherited work minimum. The first run exposed a real `TooFast` rejection at drop despite completed travel. Its raw failures and the same-roster rerun are kept separately. ## Scout departure and retrieval

`record_recruitment_cue` accepts a committed positive autonomous deposit with at least 50 µg of carbohydrate. The cue contains the scout, location at deposit, clock and delivery event. There is no food identity or direction. A nestmate only sees it if it samples the living scout through local contact and can reach the signal point geometrically at that cut. The signal must still be within its age window. On an otherwise eligible wake with a non-forage baseline, an isolated private draw may change the program. The ordinary local search, memory, loaded route and resource transactions still decide every subsequent outcome. The assay's `0.35` draw, `50 µg` gate, `120 s` assay age, and `6 h` integration age are D. The long integration age is an explicit sensitivity boundary for the six-hour decision cadence. It has no chemical half-life interpretation. [Local decision](../../cpp/src/ecology.cpp) [Signal publication](../../cpp/src/ecological_closure.cpp)

Mailleux et al. studied a single scout and 20 minutes of nestmate departures in three mature queenless *Lasius niger* source colonies, after one, four or eight days of deprivation. Their observational contact categories include exits among nestmates without a recorded scout contact. Some food receivers deposited marks before visiting the food source. The feeder was removed for the departure test, so those data support departure and possible information transfer rather than a quantified successful return by the same recruit. The authors discuss a chemical trigger and separate effects of deprivation. Le Breton and Fourcassié show changes in search trajectory following encounters with returning foragers. Other experiments measure the scout's crop/volume and trail-laying decision. These motivate a minimal local cue hypothesis and reject interpreting the chosen fixed mass, age or probability as a measured trigger. The simulated scout initially explores by fixture design. Receiver autonomy uses the ordinary local task policy. [Mailleux et al., pp. 560–566, Fig. 1 and Tables 1–3](https://doi.org/10.1007/s00040-011-0177-7) [Le Breton and Fourcassié](https://doi.org/10.1007/s00265-003-0704-2) [Crop threshold experiment](https://doi.org/10.1242/jeb.02461)

The `ForagingDecision` fixture's appended semantics record three bits, 1 for nearby trail presence, 2 for participation in a weight or bounded correction, and 4 for a chosen edge with positive trail weight. A blind `trail_weight=0` branch now uses `Explore/Search` on that edge with only the presence bit. An old recorded `FollowTrail/Trail` retains its original semantics. A marked location, a weighted choice, a changed departure program, and a loaded return are distinct endpoints. The independent audit links scout delivery, cue, contact, change of task, departure, local source discovery, positive debit, loaded motion and actual deposit in order, in one worker's forage episode. It retains activation without return, no activation, interruptions, deaths and right censoring. ## Brood care, finite water and paid activity

At an already accepted care decision, the founding mother may sample a locally observed larva eligible for a first protein ration. The path requires the original stage, colony, reach, shelter and transaction checks, no conflicting care lock, a payable packet and no prior crop/tissue protein above the child's own initial store. The existing short hatch cue and the bounded repeat-care memory still operate. The competing choice is a private conditional 0.5 draw with no future knowledge. The action begins and finishes through existing care and physiology transactions. Observations distinguish a committed opportunity, selected feed, start, positive material, later assimilation and elapsed timing. The observer has incomplete information about failed private candidate draws. [Care choice and action](../../cpp/src/care.cpp) [Ration payment](../../cpp/src/founding.cpp)

The water assay declares 1,200 µg of pure water underground at initialization. The mother can debit a physically reachable source only at an existing physiological pulse and only up to 25 µg or remaining crop room and deficit. That food mass enters the crop, with a distinct later committed uptake into hydration. The wall control has the same dose and a real blocked pocket. Adult depletion and dehydration keep their priority. This is a finite contact-access water mechanism with no air, humidity or cotton model. B01 supports facultative founding care and sensitivity to actual worker context. B02 supports physical brood retrieval under its own observation conditions. Neither identifies the local first-ration preference, a water dose or the deprivation hazards used here. [B01, Methods and Figures 1–3](https://doi.org/10.1111/1365-2435.14536) [B02, arena and 600 s observations](https://doi.org/10.1002/ece3.4475) [Water source and transaction](../../cpp/src/ecological_closure.cpp)

The existing activity surcharge is charged from an owned physical action's scheduled interval, or the actual begin of timed care, until completion, interruption or competing death. Program dwell and idle waits are independent. Resolving a point transaction, such as untimed worker exchange, creates no invented handling duration. A failed scheduled segment may still have consumed the planned action interval. Its paid time is recorded separately from a movement that changed the actual position. The model advances position at segment commit and has no intervening continuous posture or kinematics samples. Paid work while the program remains `Paused` includes founding care. This is an interval energy accounting correction. It is not a separate assay for real resting, sleep, fatigue or per-action metabolic rate.
