# B06 timing gate and protected-game scope request

**Decision requested. Review only.** Preserve the original B06 timing gate, both count readers and the decision `INSUFFICIENT`. Consider a separate engineering and simulation-use decision for a clearly supplied and protected game scenario. This request has no effect on the current gate.

## Existing requirement

The WP-6A F1_GATE asks for condition-specific descriptive 95% intervals on all four daily census endpoints, within the published reference ranges expanded by one census bin. At least 12 complete worlds per population across the ordinary grid must contribute. The `exact` and `at_least` readers retain the first observed living larval count as the target. The order of stage and death events, missing outcomes, overshoots, competing depletion and fixed daily observation are retained. The earlier audit reproduced both readers on a fixed 444-world set with 1,686 first-census larvae. None of those first cohorts completed in its entirety. In the original primary closed-stock comparison, 0/192 complete count matches per population contributed to timing acceptance. ## What can be compared

The warm B06 study used 40 mm chambers, regularly moistened cotton, darkness and no added food, with approximately daily inspections. The warm Borisovka and St. Petersburg groups used 20 and 22 individual foundresses. Tables 1–2 and the Methods report the population summaries and stage-specific temperatures near 25 °C. The stages have distinct assay counts. The reported observations lack a usable per-foundress daily raw table, quantitative cotton water exposure, individually resolved mortality and measured nutrient input. No new raw B06 census dataset was obtained. The accepted local full text has SHA-256 `b8f897d22096c1d073a00f992ac7f6700e67c12ae1a79476632d38999a53e3de`. [Kipyatkov et al., pp. 166–169](https://doi.org/10.1023/B:JOEY.0000033808.45455.75) [Acquisition status](../s6_4_preflight/S6_4_PHASE4_DATA_ACQUISITION_PLAN.md)

In the protected game assay, workers retrieve external food and water from a limited physical source with 120 scheduled release doses. The 55-day mechanistic water study instead initializes a finite local ration and uses actual source debit and subsequent uptake, with identical ration behind a wall. Neither exposure reconstructs cotton water potential, humidity, renewal or microclimate. The simulation's programmed stage clocks are held fixed. Individual transition times among successes are selected durations. The census waits for simultaneous living counts and may include later offspring. Distinct identities, diet, water or protein shortfalls, delayed/failed transitions and maternal allocation can all affect the count endpoint. The parallel census and event-time analyses in the ecological evidence preserve those separate estimands.

Demonstrated simulation failures include early loss of the first larval cohort and replacement of lost individuals by later broods in some nominal count matches. Access to water and a first protein ration can be intervened on in the Core. The missing biological exposures and individual histories limit attribution of the paper-to-model discrepancy to a particular stage rate, moisture condition or interaction. No result from a source used in model development is used as independent validation. A matched future experiment needs daily colony records with interval-observed transitions, known-source foundresses, moisture/intake observations, losses and censoring, followed by a separate matched validation set.

## Candidate approval scope

The existing F1_GATE would continue unchanged for claims about the B06-like isolated, warm, moist-cotton assay. A separately named protected-game decision would use the frozen 150-day joint protocol for an opt-in warm foundress with no seeded workers, two reference profiles, two voxel geometries, two maternal reserves, finite external food and a retained negative panel. Its minimum is at least 4/6 per population per geometry and reserve cell, with each loaded trip, uptake, paid maternal and worker rations, later funded births and eclosions, adult work and survival through days 120/150, and same-parcel autonomous cut/move/drop checked. Absent and physically blocked inputs must yield zero offsite collection/delivery. The protein-free arm must yield zero worker-sourced larval protein. Limited-supply and lower-reserve failures remain visible. SC-06 local activation and the engineering gates remain separately required. Out-of-scope seasonal, mature and field claims remain subject to their own evidence.

If approved, add a named protected-use decision and link it to the immutable new experiment records. Keep the historic F1_GATE record, exact and at-least statistics, original denominators, failures, nine biological blocker statuses, and default and historical model versions. The proposed scope carries no B06 match or species-level validation. Current application performance and the joint result are reported separately.
