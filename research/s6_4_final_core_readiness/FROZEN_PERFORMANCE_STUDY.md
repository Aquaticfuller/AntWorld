# Performance measurement-system study and prospective estimator

The published performance qualification remains unchanged. This document specifies a retrospective diagnostic and an offline positive/negative-control study before either is executed. No samples are removed and the candidate does not reclassify any historical collection.

## Historical reconstruction

Read the six complete native application collections named in the final freeze. Require the same canonical input, event count, RNG and initial/final/fact digests within each cell. Recompute the original medians, linear P95, control median ratio, paired peak RSS and all observed foreground cuts, and compare cell by cell against the accepted decision files. Report intersection and union of failures across each pair of historical matrices over shared cells. On each wall-time surface report all eight session-wide A/A label permutations and all three leave-one-session A/B values as diagnostics. Read the independently admitted instrumented collection by phases. For each A/B pair compute differences in wall time, internal application time, cut, advance, encode, SQLite save/restore, nested VFS synchronization, process residual and thread CPU. Show the most expensive original restore and foreground observations and their workload signatures. Differences across original and instrumented builds are not causal phase estimates. Memory peak is recorded. A per-allocation timer or per-operation kernel scheduling log is unavailable in these runs.

## Candidate estimator and offline falsifiers

For an isolated prospective study, randomize complete on/off and disabled/disabled pairs in balanced ABBA/BAAB order within each cell and separately randomize cell order in each admitted window. One window contains four complete pairs of each type per cell. Windows are separately admitted and retain the unchanged system/core P50 and P95, power, temperature, frequency, counter validity, retry and telemetry obligations. Measure real native SQLite publication and synchronization, close, reopen and recovery, and include all common and optional I/O plus the full child-process life in the measured surfaces. Publish every case, order, raw record and authority signature. Prespecify the actual window count after a separate non-qualification pilot. Do not reuse the pilot in the formal count.

Candidate estimates are ratios of pooled marginal P50 and P95 for both application and process. Resample entire admitted windows with replacement, preserving all within-window paired labels, order and cross-cell covariance. Use one-sided 95% upper confidence bounds for the two A/B limits, and paired window-resampled 90% two-sided intervals for A/A equivalence wholly inside `[0.90,1.10]`. Publish two-sided 95% ratio intervals as well. All components of all cells must pass to claim a global pass. This is an intersection-union decision. Localizing a statistically demonstrated failure on any of multiple cells or metrics requires multiplicity control. Before measuring, set the family as 34 cells × 2 surfaces × 4 directional decisions (median, tail and both A/A directions). Use one-sided familywise 0.05 Bonferroni lower/upper bounds for such statistical failure claims. With 272 tests use at least 199,999 joint window draws or a separately validated tail-interval estimator. Directly observed hard violations of correctness, foreground maximum 20 ms, foreground P95 5 ms, or paired peak RSS increment 16 MiB retain their separate frozen decisions. Incomplete, telemetry-invalid, or precision-limited results are `INCONCLUSIVE` under the candidate. Report P99 as a diagnostic and the observed maximum over all retained cuts, including its specific case, window and event cursor. A finite sample cannot prove an unbounded hard real-time guarantee.

The offline simulation compares the original six observations per arm in three windows against 32, 64 and 96 independent windows with four observations per arm. Generate 240 independent trials for each cell scenario from NumPy `PCG64`, seed `640941`. Generate original and candidate estimators on their respective designs. Quiet and clustered regimes use log-standard deviations `(0.03,0.02,0.03)` and `(0.10,0.05,0.07)` respectively for window, pair and residual. Application and process have a shared session term, separate paired draws, and process residual standard deviation 1.5× application. Freeze the ordinary A/B multipliers at 1.00, 1.05, 1.10, 1.14 and 1.25. For the tail-only 1.05 case add a monotone term equal to twice the positive excess above the population log-time P90, keeping the known median and computing the exact population P95 from the normal model. In the maximum-violation case force one retained 30.4863 ms cut. The 1.10 boundary is reported separately from strict good and bad scenarios. Diagnostic screening uses 399 window draws for the candidate pass/precision classification only, not for multiplicity-adjusted failure inference. Report a Wilson interval for each simulated pass probability, all raw trial decisions, observed coverage of the known P95 ratio by both 95% intervals, and an independence illustration plus dependence-free bounds for the 34-cell pass probability. These are synthetic operating characteristics. They cannot identify host variability or prove the prospective estimator fit for release on real hardware.

For real qualification, precommit a single full matrix and time budget. Run a separate 12-window instrument pilot, then use held-back control windows (one frozen set of 12, independent of the pilot) to check interval coverage against controlled positive and negative timing injections. Require power of at least 90% to qualify a true 1.05 median and at most 1.10 tail, and one-sided false acceptance at most 5% at a true 1.10 median or 1.20 tail, with upper confidence bounds on misclassification reported. Define a target tail log-interval half-width of 0.05, estimated at the workload with the widest pilot interval, before selecting the 32/64/96-window candidate. If the fixed maximum design cannot provide the declared precision or stable A/A behavior, record the proposed procedure as unqualified. Freeze any revised design prospectively. No new procedure replaces the published gate without an independent decision. A controlled foreground violation remains a failure under either procedure.
