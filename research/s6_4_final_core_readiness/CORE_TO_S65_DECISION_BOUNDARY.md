# S6.4 and S6.5 decision boundary

The project scope is the accepted one-colony active-season, protected warm founding and early-worker review envelope. The world design still calls for an extensible true 3D multi-colony simulator. The only current bounded engineering claims apply to declared v7 opt-in profiles with real finite resources, local agency, constrained access and published historical authority. Nominal-reference clocks and actual ambient conditions are separate. The added fast-shaft diagnostic admits only the exact homogeneous geometry, clearance, clocks, diet and population inputs in its protocol. A 150-day censored result gives no later survival guarantee. ## Scope exclusions and design ownership

Natural winter and diapause, pathogens, mature nest morphology, full sexual caste and dispersal behavior, broad geographic/soil/climate transfer, deep navigation under permanent reconfiguration and multi-year mature colony outcomes have specific evidence gaps. They do not by themselves block the limited active-season game envelope. A short reference chain in known 3D geometry cannot justify claiming those capabilities. Local foraging, resource competition and exchange, functional care, viable development and depletion, physical excavation and meaningful social recruitment remain in the proposed core envelope. Independent approval is required to remove an admitted capability from that scope.

S6.5, if later approved, is the CLI/Web observation platform. It may query a committed event history, read versioned snapshots and receipts, and display validated aggregates, failure states and pending requests separately. In the confirmed architecture any authorized future operator command enters the existing validated single-writer input path with stable identity and receipts. The platform would not become the owner of biology, fabricate facts, promote an experimental profile, control task choice, or rewrite a saved world's model. It would retain explicit scope and history provenance, observer backpressure, measured end-to-end costs and reproducible restoration. Unity, parallel/GPU simulation and a Game Master behavior controller are outside this gate.
