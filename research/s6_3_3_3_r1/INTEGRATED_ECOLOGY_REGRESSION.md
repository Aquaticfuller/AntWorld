## Paired measures

- Maternal receiving sums the actual incoming transfer payloads. Self-ingestion includes crop withdrawals from the nest and cargo if present. Absorption sums actual physiological updates and can draw from initial reserves as well as later intake. These are separate streams.
- Alive days end at the committed death event or are right-censored at 21 days. Death causes are retained. Survival is evaluated as observed under finite supply and is not an assay target.
- Care time sums elapsed started care bouts with known duration through completion or interruption. Tactile time counts inspect, arrange, clean and completed positive timed feeding. Placement remains a discrete committed endpoint. An instantaneous observer's unknown duration is kept separately. This engineering endpoint does not alter the B01 scan definition.
- Worker changes count distinct ordinary program transitions and divide by adult worker-days where applicable. This is separate from physical motion or a true activity budget.
- Differences match seed, workers, food, representation and the requested model or treatment contrast. Percentile intervals resample the three paired seeds per stratum, 4,096 times with analysis seed 63331. With three seeds they describe sensitivity of these engineering fixtures. They do not provide a population confidence claim about the species.

## Biological evidence retained

The engineering controls do not identify a loaded speed, grip duration, contact radius or queen social inhibition parameter. Carthy's trained-worker assay uses visual perturbations and familiar apparatus. Its initial looped approaches and later shorter paths on pp. 280–282 constrain orientation hypotheses. Tables II and IV use two trained workers under differing illumination. The existing unit and comparability caveats remain. Khuong's separate canvas trajectories measure unloaded workers across slopes. Lenoir's pp. 154–157 document repeated retrieval in a connected glass setup and describe brood-specific recruitment as a hypothesis. His donor and seasonal assays on pp. 161–164 delimit context-specific acceptance. The X-ray CT images of two natural mounds in Khuong et al. 2016, Fig. 1 and SI Methods, document gallery structure. They provide no measurement of a worker's connector search radius or loaded shaft speed.
