# Prospective WP-6B multi-scale acceptance

This protocol defines a reviewable first implementation experiment. WP-6A executes only arithmetic feasibility screening. `M` rows in the [acceptance matrix](MULTISCALE_ACCEPTANCE_MATRIX.csv) cover the individual mechanism, `C` rows cover interactions, and `L` rows cover colony histories. Rows distinguish measured endpoints, qualitative evidence and engineering criteria. Matrix outcomes stay separate from engineering integrity. Every scoring rule below needs approval before biological code or outcome inspection.

Use a stable identity timeline with first positive feed, both debit and receipt, realized uptake, stage-enter/exit, end state and death reason. Keep committed facts, cut snapshots and derived intervals distinct. Observed dose, unused reserve, elapsed progress and active behavior are separate. Zero acts, rejected acts, outside-visibility inputs, deaths, disappearance and observation stop get their own outcome codes. The loss of body stock at death may be zero if it is compartmental rather than the cause. Report retained material at death. Use the existing history and WP-4/WP-5 budget and stage readers, with opt-in adapters for any new typed facts approved in WP-6B.

For B06, implement a comparison reader of fixed daily censuses at the same relative survey time. Record first eggs and first larvae, including their count `k` at the first larval census. Record the first subsequent observation reaching `k` pupae and `k` workers. Flag overshoots and repeat the analysis on exact-count observations as a sensitivity check to the study's matching-number rule. Record `k` and the last observation for missing thresholds. Label overlapping earlier cohorts as a count endpoint. Preserve known-age simulation transitions as a separate dataset. Report egg count at the first positive laying census and the first 24 h after committed laying. B06 supports multiple early eggs and records onset in 2–8 d across treatments. Fine-grained spacing and bout durations remain unmeasured. Include B06 20/20 Borisovka and 22/22 St. Petersburg warm-foundress results with their denominators and published errors. Use the separately reported population ranges and means for elapsed timing among observations. No equivalence between these means and individual survival or an isolated clock proof is claimed. ## Decision rules

1. **Engineering** requires exact ledgers, legal physical contacts, a fully conserved initialization, one stable identity per lifetime, reproducible ordering and checkpoints, and all inherited CTest and actual performance controls. Any regression in observer-off or exact equality, manufactured material, a forced hatch or worker, or an unaccounted transaction invalidates the candidate. Existing hardware admission and TD-01/TD-08 ≤6× gates remain unchanged. [Engineering test plan](WP6B_IMPLEMENTATION_PLAN.md)
2. **Pattern validity** requires the in-scope `M`, `C` and `L` rows marked `F1_GATE`. On ordinary care-on worlds, require at least one first worker by day 90 in at least four of six worlds in at least 24/32 profiles **for each population**. In addition, each factor level must have at least 10/16 profiles meeting that per-profile rule. At least six of eight additional-check profiles must reach the same four-of-six criterion per population. These thresholds qualify robust engineering behavior. They are neither a fit target nor a prediction of founding success in the species. Keep first-worker likelihood and stage transition times separate. With zero external food, feeding-disabled offspring have no assigned larval nutrient acquisition. The chosen tissue target must force failure to pupate in that specifically constructed control, while deaths and censored larvae remain allowed. In matched low-stock or contact-limited tests, record the mechanism of failure even where a different individual succeeds.
3. **Timing** compare each population's median daily observable egg, larva, prepupa+pupa and total durations to its reported ranges (allow one census bin for the discretization mismatch). Require a descriptive 95% interval within the expanded range for each estimable condition-specific endpoint. Report dispersion, successful counts, and losses. Require at least twelve independent simulated worlds per population contributing complete daily endpoint observations across the ordinary grid. A missing endpoint or a too-wide interval stays `INSUFFICIENT` for that claim. These are development checks because clocks use the paper's measured endpoints. They cannot upgrade biological validation. A late nutrient-limited transition is analyzed as such and retained in the input denominators. Keep the B01 conditional time and proportion alongside the comparison without a mismatched quantitative pass threshold.
4. **Multiscale conditional use** requires mechanisms, timing and viable trajectories together. Verify the stock trajectory and feeding chain in every normally successful no-worker replicate, including an actual positive local maternal ration before the first nutrient-qualified larva pupates. Reject a candidate which passes first-worker incidence by an unsupported trigger, by suppressing adverse branches, or by hiding a dependence on one profile or seed. Preserve valid nonzero failure, reserve decline, finite-resource limits, pressure on care and eventual decline. The rest of the game retains its separately reviewed readiness. Failure of a deferred research row keeps that scope unqualified. Candidate scope and authorization change only on independent review.
