# Finite maternal reserves and viable first brood

## Condition and evidence

The reference profile is an isolated mated *Lasius niger* foundress in a protected warm chamber during the active season. Use the nominal 25 °C B06 Borisovka and St. Petersburg rows as distinct development contexts. Water in the published apparatus was supplied through moistened cotton. The initial implementation represents a humid sheltered condition using a declared finite hydration stock and losses. Uptake from cotton and a humidity-response function remain unmodeled. A no-water or low-hydration stress changes stocks or the declared loss treatment and has no inferred ambient RH. Founder care requires the existing reachable shelter and real locally detected brood. Worker access and finite feeding are distinct comparison arms. [Source details and existing raw-data gaps](../s6_4_wp4/SOURCE_AND_EVIDENCE_AUDIT.md)

## Shared state and accounting

Use a minimal per-identity reserve record alongside the existing physiology. The founder owns dry lipid-equivalent substrate `F` in µg and mobilizable maternal protein `P` in µg. Her hydration, active energy buffer `E`, crop and cumulative losses remain in the existing physiological compartments. Offspring own their **separate** lipid stock and body energy, structural protein and water. A larva obtains positive new stock only from an actual committed ration or an ordinary existing food transaction. An egg receives a funded packet at laying and a pupa relies on material already acquired. No reserve can be consumed twice or reused after death. The inferred neutral-lipid equivalent is a bounded modeled substrate. It is distinct from empirical dry fat measurements, total wet mass and the four existing food components. The **same existing Adult worker** rules and defaults apply after emergence, apart from accounting for any remaining finite natal lipid in opt-in workers. No new biological caste, instar or emergence bonus is needed.

Seed a declared wet-mass check at setup. `lipid + reserve protein + body hydration + any crop + structural minimum <= measured or declared wet mass`. The structural remainder is inert and non-transferable. A single declared seed introduces the backing substrate. Any initial `E` for an enrolled individual is funded from that individual's declared lipid at the initial commit. Body energy generated by this debit is an internal conversion, outside the legacy external initial-energy sum. Seeded engineering controls keep their existing independent identity and exact ledger.

The ration is an effective local delivery of tracked substrates. B01 and FT01 support care and finite reserve use, with the physical composition and route of individual meals still unresolved. F1 therefore assumes a bounded contact delivery. It does not infer a measured lipid dose or distinct trophic-egg strategy. Preserve the inherited adult/crop carbohydrate conversion as a separate engineering rule in early-worker controls. If that rule limits provisioning, diagnose the boundary before proposing a change to BR-02 or BR-05.

At a scheduled refill, transfer an integer number `f` of available µg from the individual's lipid to its spent-lipid account. Credit usable energy `f * Y` nJ to the current body buffer. Here `Y` is an effective stored-energy yield bounded by ordinary triglyceride combustion and declared as a C hypothesis. `30–38 J/mg` is equivalent to `30,000,000–38,000,000 nJ/µg`. Energy lost before the usable yield is outside this effective-energy ledger. Maintain the existing separate cumulative expenditure. Cap each refill by available material and buffer capacity. A derived buffer target holds up to two refill periods of the stage's declared maximum expenditure, rounded up to lipid quanta, with one quantum of headroom for integer conversion. Count inherited active cost in the bound when applicable. The regular six-hour refill shares the existing physiological pulse for enrolled live ants. It is an engineering discretization. If a buffer reaches zero before its next valid refill, death is a possible outcome even if inaccessible or unused stock remains. Never resurrect a dead individual on a pulse. Crop absorption follows the existing local transaction and pulse semantics. [Lipid combustion context](https://doi.org/10.1007/BF02636185)

The following identities must close at each published cut and after failure or restore. Counts include carried, stored and terminal material where applicable.

```text
introduced lipid = live and terminal lipid stock + spent lipid
introduced protein = existing accounted protein + additional mobile and locked maternal protein
introduced water = existing accounted water, including body, crop and cumulative loss
external legacy E + introduced effective lipid energy + energy realized from crop carbohydrate
    = sum(body buffers) + cumulative dissipated energy + remaining effective lipid energy
```

The final line is world-wide. Embryo/larval transfers cancel at world scope and appear as debit/credit pairs per identity. Initial body energy and the same fuel's effective energy cannot both enter the external-input side. At death preserve stocks in a terminal compartment. Water and protein travel through their own ledgers. No metabolic-water credit or conversion of protein into lipid is claimed.

## Candidate F1 — immediately available finite reserves

1. Allow laying only on the existing reproductive wake and at a valid position. After the declared first-lay delay, allow a bounded opening bout of short-spaced single-egg opportunities for one day. Use the longer inter-attempt interval thereafter. This permits a multiple-egg first census, as reported in B06, with spacing and bout duration as engineering choices. Each opportunity can defer and there is no quota. Fund an egg's own lipid `F_e`, structural protein `P_e` and hydration `W_e` from the mother in one transaction. Debit laying overhead from her currently available body energy. The projected maternal reserve and hydration **after** the packet and overhead must exceed a configurable own-maintenance floor `H * rate`. Use only the queen's own current states and declared rates. No global brood count, queued future feeding or unpaid output enters the guard. Deferred attempts consume zero egg material. Existing geometric placement and care-lease release rules remain active.
2. An egg consumes its own `E/F` and water at egg-specific rates. Its independent clock runs when it is alive, with death at an exact exhaustion tie taking priority over hatch. After hatching the same ID retains all remaining stocks. Carry cost and water integration remainders over boundaries. The initial egg stage receives no external feeding.
3. Use the existing local `FeedBrood` action, with a foundress-only proposal to transfer up to the declared lipid, protein and water limits. The receiver must be a live reachable larva in the same colony, with current room and a component deficit. Use remaining care, transfer and capacity guards. Settle both parties and recheck at action resolution. Atomically debit actual funded components, credit larval lipid and crop protein/water, and commit one typed packet. Its lipid must be converted on the recipient's own pulse. Crop and usable body remain separate. Clip by locally defined component capacity, physical dose, and maternal maintenance headroom. Zero or rejected rations produce an outcome without changing stocks. No egg or pupa receives this packet. Other donors use the existing transfer path.
4. Advance larval elapsed time under larval costs. At the larval clock boundary require the tissue target plus a local reserve threshold for prepupa/pupa and a strict positive remainder at emergence. Compute that threshold from the configured pupal costs. It is a developmental readiness hypothesis, not a prediction the individual ant makes. Otherwise keep the larva alive if possible in a limited-progress state. It continues maintenance, can be helped by a valid subsequent feed, and can die or be right-censored. Freeze elapsed progress at its ceiling while limited. Schedule at the next actual uptake or exhaustion boundary and never spin at the capped timestamp. Determine pupal readiness from remaining modeled stock after settling. Do not credit pending work or unabsorbed crop. Model the B06 prepupa plus pupa interval as one closed, non-feeding stage. Pupal death still wins ties. Emergence remains the actual ordinary stage transition and normal autonomous enrollment. Existing workers and older queens use their ordinary rules outside the explicitly enrolled profile.

The F1 hypothesis is that a single finite maternal access pool, contact-mediated meals and different stage costs suffice for a robust warm founding window. If stored protein remains unused while eligible contact and local delivery are present, inspect the release and absorption sequence. If care-off and care-on have the same offspring state and doses, or if positive transfer occurs without valid contact, the relevant mechanism fails. With no receipts, credit no larval ration. A maternal headroom delay may inhibit a valid birth and its outcomes must be reported. There is no clutch-size or first-worker count target in the policy.

## Candidate F2 — delayed maternal protein release

Use the same F1 compartments, total input, clocks, costs and local transactions. At initialization reserve a declared fraction of maternal protein as temporarily inaccessible. Expose the rest immediately. For the locked fraction, a deterministic linear ramp starts after a bounded post-registration delay and ends after a finite release window. At each relevant schedule or transaction, move `floor(initial_locked * clamp((age - onset) / width, 0, 1)) - already_released` µg into the available maternal pool. The debit and credit occur together. The only additional authoritative values are initial locked amount and released amount, or equivalent exact integer counters. Age is time since the recorded foundress setup. WP-6B would anchor setup to a known flight/registration interval, rather than assume the exact mating time. No new protein enters the world. F1 and F2 both obey the same site, care, energy and stage rules.

The timed tissue release is a C hypothesis suggested by FT01's timing of visible muscle remodeling. The fraction, timing range and conversion to usable protein remain D engineering assumptions. Compare F2 against F1 at equal total stocks and actual care exposure. Its discriminating endpoint is the distribution of early lay and larval protein availability, especially after resource or care perturbation. If accessible protein and timing produce no measurable difference, favor F1's simpler rule. If delayed release improves one timing endpoint while suppressing observed early laying or causing unjustified differences in loss, reject the added assumption. WP-6A requests F1 only for the first pilot. F2 is a later opt-in experiment subject to approval after an observable F1 residual.

## Range construction and executable check

All numeric candidate inputs and stress limits are in [the parameter envelope](FOUNDRESS_PARAMETER_ENVELOPE.csv). The ordinary provisional subset and broader adverse combinations are fixed before outcomes. Use wet mass, energy dimensional checks and event/stock capacities as feasibility constraints. The values are exploratory. For example 5,500 µg lipid at 34,000,000 nJ/µg is 187 J of initial effective substrate. At 2.34 J/d this is about 80 d of resting-only budget. A real queen also allocates eggs, feeds larvae and may spend activity energy or dehydrate. A 3,000 µg reserve at the low yield with high maintenance can fail during the ordinary founding window. Neither scenario establishes a biological survival probability. Small stage-specific costs are bracketed so both insufficient and sufficient embryo/pupal stocks appear within the declared range. Biological data will determine tighter ranges when available.

The provided Python screen computes one-offspring ideal-access budget inequalities and lower bounds on number of actual meals. It carries exact units and does not schedule ant actions. It records both the original warm-budget failure and low-stock/care-off counterexamples. Actual brood formation, comparative rates, a plausible care pattern, executable C++ and independent validation remain future tests.
