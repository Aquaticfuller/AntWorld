## Cross-species and modeling boundaries

**B07-X.** The isotope experiment in §§2.4, 3.3 and Fig. 7 uses `Lasius neoniger` only. It samples pooled homogenized workers and larvae from eight colonies at 24 and 96 h after offering the labeled or control diet. The results concern enrichment by stage and time and do not identify the identity or dose of a gift, a recipient's crop-to-body transition or a fitted delay. The authors' expected relative enrichment pattern was not established. Keep B07-X under the accepted `CROSS_SPECIES_EXTRAPOLATION` context. [Original experiment](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7391535/) [Dryad isotope table and analysis](https://doi.org/10.5061/dryad.g79cnp5n1)

**Method comparators.** Greenwald et al. measured changes in calibrated crop fluorescence during individual contacts in `Camponotus` workers, including direction changes in some bouts, and studied crop-load-linked unloading in `C. sanctus`. These studies show how an assay can link oral contact to changing crop amount and test accessible local explanations. They do not identify black garden ant transfer fractions, cue access or crop-to-body uptake rates. They are additional methods references for Phase 0, outside the frozen accepted evidence classification tables. [2015 contact imaging, Fig. 3](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4519732/) [2018 crop-load study, Figs. 2–3](https://elifesciences.org/articles/31730)

**Core assumptions.** Exact recipient deficit, quarter- and half-capacity gates, bounded candidate sampling, equal weighting among eligible samples, transfer ceilings, component partitioning, pulse rates and yield retain their accepted provenance. Observed differences in oral-bout counts or durations cannot identify a choice law or nutrient transfer per bout. Neither cross-species crop fluorescence nor congener tissue enrichment supplies a focal assimilation rate. The branch introduces no species defaults. | Required measurement | Current status | Acquisition target |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Local encounter and alternatives, resolved recipient or explicit no choice | PARTIAL, worker directed oral events only | Time-aligned focal video or tracking of available partners and contact/attempts with feeding and caste context |
| Actual positive gift and measured zero gift per attempt | MISSING in focal assets | Paired, validated pre/post component measures or an assay with sufficient detection resolution and known direction |
| Uptake after a quantified delivered dose | MISSING in focal assets | Timed crop and separate body/tissue measurements with known exposure, onward flow and water/oxidation loss controls |
| Independent worker, queen and larval validation | MISSING | Registered `VAL-BR-02` source-colony blocks, held out before fits with known group conditions, censoring and specimen lineage |

The Phase 4 intake candidates address other endpoints. Their file receipt is not admission of a matched BR-02 dataset. A focused check of the primary papers, linked supplements and the accepted acquisition register found no full focal exposure, dose and uptake sequence. No outcome can be upgraded to evidence of positive transfer or zero transfer from an unmatched contact or missing row. No authors were contacted in this phase.
