# RM-02 prospective identification and validation protocol

The claim under test is local provisioning and uptake in controlled, active-season `Lasius niger` founder and early-worker groups. Validate the linked sequence from opportunity to actual body intake with its own time and dose endpoints. Run an engineering observer qualification before relying on its outputs. This document freezes the sequence, estimands, outcome categories and decision logic for review. Assay precision, biological margins, sampling windows and colony counts need a prospective pilot and approval before the independent comparison. [BR-02 scope](../s6_4_preflight/INDEPENDENT_BIOLOGICAL_VALIDATION_PLAN.md#br-02--recipient-selection-transfer-and-uptake)

## Data to acquire

| Stage and falsifiable question | Required linked measurements | Controls and eligibility |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Exposure and selection. Does recipient state explain choice after exposure? | Continuous or calibrated-interval tracking of donor, available partners, contact geometry, oral contact and attempted direction, chosen target or explicit no selection. Record actual starting donor diet/crop, recipient stage, caste, crop fullness and independently measured state or cue, along with order, time and visibility. | Start with adult worker groups to bridge B08/N01 and separately observe early queenright groups. Predefine worker-to-worker, worker-to-queen and worker-to-larva strata and track queen-to-larva care separately where claimed. Compare eligible alternatives with matched access. Randomize recipient position and condition within a source-colony block where feasible. Contrast receiver stores and an accessible cue with donor stores and contact exposure held or measured. |
| Transfer incidence and dose. Does oral contact deliver a positive mass and how much? | Pair observation with calibrated pre/post donor and receiver crop component amounts or direct equivalent. Record gross direction(s), net change and uncertainties, mouth-contact start/stop, known no-movement cases, failure cause when observed, detection limits, mixing and concurrent flows. Water, carbohydrate and protein need separate validated measures for claims about those components. | Use known-volume/composition standards and independent checks on crop-to-tracer conversion in each claimed caste/stage and diet. Include offered-versus-received, no-supply, full-recipient and blocked-access controls. Log zero and below-detection outcomes. Keep direction reversal and multiple receivers as paired or nested observations, not independent bouts. |
| Uptake. Given a verified received dose, what enters the body by time? | Time-stamped known transfer and crop dose, initial body stores, and crop versus body/water/tissue observations with sufficient early and late coverage. Record onward gifts, loss, respiration or oxidation where needed for energy claims, deposition and time at risk. Separate crop depletion from labeled material in other compartments. | A documented single-dose window or measured onward transfer is needed for attributable uptake. Use sham/unfed and known-dose controls. Destructive samples are different individuals nested within independent source colonies and sampling times. Differentiate a measurable onset/lag from finite rate, storage capacity and demand. A missing measurement interval cannot identify an instantaneous pulse. |

Acquire source-colony IDs, collection date/population, worker/brood ages or uncertainty, queen state, availability and ingested diet, fasting history, temperature/humidity, group size, geometry, batch and instrument metadata, observation start/end and all exclusions or deaths. Keep individually identified recipient records and raw timestamped dose/compartment readings. Validate recovery, the limit of detection, limits of quantification and the observer's error across the actual assay range. Video can establish oral behavior and its risk set. It cannot establish no transfer by itself. A tracer can quantify the component it has been validated to track, with no assumed quantitative translation to a different component. [Direct and method sources](DATA_READINESS.md)

Ask for documented B08 bout annotations and video continuity/group mapping and for N01 raw event records if available. These improve a development comparison. Search for an unexposed, condition-matched measured cohort or propose new collection for `VAL-BR-02`. Obtain the relevant permissions and identifiers before data use. A paper summary, offered amount, growth series or congener pulse archive cannot fill missing receiver-level columns. The Phase 4 register remains authoritative for receipt and acquisition status. ## Partitions and identification

- **Development comparators.** B08/N01 establish the worker oral-event definitions and conditional patterns to explain. Keep their different thresholds and scan/recording rules when applying a measurement model. R1L adds qualitative recipient contexts. B03/B07 constrain initialization and plausibility of offered-diet regimes. B07-X and the `Camponotus` studies are design context. These sources do not provide a full calibration or holdout.
- **Calibration and instrument pilot.** Use named source colonies disjoint from the validation set. First determine accessible cue/exposure coverage and repeatability, then estimate only supported choice or transfer components. Then use quantified dose and independent uptake/loss measurements to test kinetic terms. Pre-register alternative simple models and retain confounded or interval-only estimates. Without measured alternatives, encounter availability is confounded with selection. Without an independent positive mass measurement, a high observed bout rate can mimic a high dose. Without separate crop and body observations, loss, onward transfer, uptake and release from stores are confounded. Keep the fixture's pulse spacing and fixed maintenance/yield outside biological fits unless independently measured under their own authorized protocols.
- **Independent validation.** Freeze primary endpoints, margins, missingness analysis, sampled caste/condition cells, parameter estimates, Core/assay versions and seeds before opening `VAL-BR-02`. Partition by whole original source colony or independent foundress, including all derived groups and nested bouts and aliquots. If lineages overlap or provenance is unresolved, hold the linked groups together. Samples from the same source colony, reused individuals, repeated pulses and simulator seeds do not supply additional independent biological groups. Changing the law after inspecting a holdout requires a new independent holdout.

Use pilot technical and between-colony variance, measured attrition, and the smallest supply difference consequential to actual recipient intake to set precision and equivalence margins for each admitted caste/diet context. Set the required number of independent colony blocks from the widest primary estimand and expected censoring. Freeze a minimum reportable risk-set coverage and a detection limit below the relevant dose margin before validation. Where dose is only measurable for one component, the claim and gates for other components remain incomplete. Keep measured initial states or their declared uncertainty separate from fitted behavior. All known simulated comparator parameters are frozen in Phase 0.

## Primary comparisons and retention of failures

1. **Opportunity to selected partner.** Use an exposure-conditioned choice analysis with candidate set, donor/recipient state and a no-selection option. Compare incidence of opportunities, selected partner distributions and opportunity-to-attempt time by matched caste and treatment. Test whether the proposed cue adds prediction over geometry and availability alone using separated recipient/donor contrasts. An absent denominator leaves choice unidentified. Inspect both failure to reach and failure after reach.
2. **Attempt to positive transfer.** Use a two-part analysis for probability of a positive verified component gift and dose distribution conditional on a positive gift. Compare per-recipient cumulative verified dose and timing as well as conditional amounts. Preserve observed rejections, known zero transfers, partial gifts, known non-attempts and positively verified transfers with the appropriate exposure denominator. Analyze measured values below the assay limit as interval-censored, with a sensitivity comparison. Missing or unobserved dose stays missing. A completed oral bout with unknown mass stays a bout.
3. **Delivered dose to uptake.** Compare the trajectory and first observed entry into a separate body compartment after the measured crop credit. Use time-to-event or interval-censored models as appropriate, with death or an eligibility-ending stage transition as a separate competing outcome. Distinguish known zero-uptake intervals, right-censored ongoing recipients, missing samples and post-death endpoints. Follow measured loss and onward flow, and compare full-recipient trajectories including recipients who never show uptake. Report body energy separately from converted carbohydrate mass and expenditure.

Use source-colony blocked or hierarchical inference and colony-level resampling for uncertainty. Respect repeated donors/recipients and the shared dyad and time dependence. Model-generated opportunities and biological video have different visibility and timing. Downsample or censor the simulated comparator only by a predeclared match to the empirical protocol, preserving all raw events. Keep lost observation windows, failed checks, competing exits and zero outcomes in the accounting. Report attrition and detection-limit sensitivity by stratum. Aggregate population, survival and total nest food may be secondary outcomes. They cannot substitute for exposure, positive dose, timed uptake or component conservation.

## Decisions before any biological adoption

| Result | Decision criterion |
| --- | --- |
| `ENGINEERING_PASS` | Independent replay of the observer-on/off, cut, rollback and save/restore tests and the frozen performance comparisons satisfies the [observer acceptance design](OBSERVER_DESIGN.md). Core and sidecar evidence remain distinguishable. |
| `BIOLOGICAL_VALIDATION_PASS` | For every claimed stratum the full matched independent risk set, validated dose and uptake are present. Prespecified uncertainty intervals for all primary contrasts are within the predeclared tolerances, including signed control predictions and zero/competing outcomes. Use prespecified simultaneous intervals or an intersection of endpoint equivalence tests for the overall claim. Report the validated population, diet, geometry, timescale and component scope. |
| `BIOLOGICAL_VALIDATION_FAIL` | Adequate, matched independent data give a reproducible discrepancy outside the frozen tolerance or a critical control falsifies the predicted mechanism. Retain the original and candidate results. Diagnose the first divergence in access, local choice, gift incidence/dose or body uptake. |
| `RM02_EVIDENCE_INSUFFICIENT` | Required data, risk sets, discrimination, replication, precision or assay matching are absent. This includes intervals too broad to establish equivalence or a discrepancy, unmeasured compartments and unresolved confounding. Retain the partial claim and keep BR-02 blocked. |

A separate decision is required for an isolated observer implementation and any later model change. Even a successful engineering comparison supplies evidence about simulation behavior under controlled inputs. BR-02 clearance requires the matched independent biological result.
