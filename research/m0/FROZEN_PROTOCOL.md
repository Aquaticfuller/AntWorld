# M0 MicroWorld experiment protocol

Reference: `ef9e5f752f0de68f4106f4b81277313c527297fe`. The reference OP0 demonstrations and their interpretations remain available. The new comparison changes brood placement within the same MicroWorld fixture. A separate comparison to the old OP0 world also changes the geometry.

- Fixtures: one foundress, zero initial brood or workers, 31 × 31 cm playable surface, 30 × 30 × 15 cm soil envelope, 0.5 mm geometry quantum, and a local founding chamber with either a shaft or a dogleg. Initial holes are environment inputs. Only committed geometry edits count as excavation. The 21 °C and 0.8 relative humidity labels are fixture metadata. There is no climate response or ambient water uptake.
- Configuration: use the accepted protected founding profile, with H2 local hatch cues and R1 feeding persistence unless the corresponding factor is disabled. First-feeding preference stays off. Retain finite founding resources, stage clocks, death rules, local nutrition, and the distance-based movement clock. Full balanced, no-protein, and blocked treatments use the same initial colony stores. The blocked treatment seals the underground connector, while the absent treatment supplies no external mass. A reachable-wet control introduces a finite source of 1,200 µg of water at the founding site, which can enter only through the existing paid local ingest and absorption paths. Releases on the surface have a finite 120-day reservoir.
- Main paired panel: Borisovka and St. Petersburg × shaft and dogleg × software seeds 1 and 7 × reference placement (physics 25) and MicroWorld placement (physics 27), all balanced for 90 days. Use the same WorldId and seed within a pair, with distinct archive identities. Retain all pairs. The two population names refer to development profiles, not population-specific validated parameters.
- Local ablations: on the Borisovka shaft, seeds 1 and 7, cross H2 and R1 with the physics 27 placement. Reuse the both-on worlds from the main panel. Also run the physics 27 no-care, absent, no-protein, sealed, wet, and below-nominal maternal-lipid cases for seed 1. The below-nominal input uses 4,500 µg rather than 5,500 µg. These are diagnostic perturbations. Keep all elapsed-time failures and inaccessible sources.
- Demonstration: publish the two seed-1 balanced physics 27 worlds, the matched Borisovka physics 25 world, and the seed-1 no-protein and sealed worlds. Include the terminal state frame, canonical stream, daily samples, authoritative geometry edits, exact inputs and member receipts. Use one temporary directory on D at a time. Keep compact receipts for every case and a lossless archive for each case locally. No large host benchmark or renewed historical qualification is part of M0.
- Slice 1: in a balanced new world observe paid, non-overlapping initial eggs, distinct stage identities, several occupied neighboring places and at least one committed handled displacement tied to a timed bout. Explicitly test lack of space, dead occupants, a real solid wall, no debit for a rejected attempt, and exact checkpoint continuation. Capacity is a local constraint and can defer laying. Physical contact with adults uses existing geometry; the slot model approximates a single supported brood layer and does not model adult-to-adult collision or mandible yaw.
- Slice 2: link all eggs from the first 24 simulated hours of laying to hatch, proximity, selection, start, paid doses, observable absorption, stage changes, death and right censoring. Compare pairwise counts and rates with their denominators and keep missing endpoints. A difference of two or more fewer adults from the first 24-hour egg cohort in a matched pair is a regression flag requiring diagnosis. Treat crop uptake as a separate flux from a mother's payment and never assign later mixed-crop uptake to a specific donor without evidence.
- Slice 3: report surviving and failed trajectories, with at least one balanced demonstration containing verified departure, collection, delivery, positive nutrition transfer, timed brood handling and a material excavation/drop chain. Inspect the first three naturally emerged workers in each demonstration during their first 24 hours and cumulatively through the following three days or death. Count actual movement lengths with known vs. unknown timing, paid-work intervals, task residence, transfers and measured physiology samples separately. The rest or paused program is not a sleep measurement.
- Stop criteria: report engineering checks, paired outcome differences, and any absent demo chain or scientific limitation explicitly. The panel is a small engineering and sensitivity comparison, not a biological noninferiority test. No new biological PASS follows from plausible packing or a visible colony.

The pre-existing B06 `INSUFFICIENT`, nine `BIOLOGICAL_BLOCKED` records, and historical application performance `FAIL` remain separate qualification decisions.
