## Findings that drive the next decision

- Existing worker-free queen care led to 16 completed care actions in six enabled worlds. Enabled and disabled queens laid the same number of eggs, died at about day 7.87 and produced no first worker. Warm-clock eggs with original stocks still die at about day 5.79. Three inherited mothers died with carbohydrate still in the crop. The results separate operation of the current mechanism from the biological evidence needed to revise it.
- A 32-worker compact group excavates and transports material under the local rule. Roomy, isolated and shielded counterparts had zero digs. Changing the water mass per quantum retained identical removed volume. Soil transport and blocked/reopened routes can be verified from actual committed facts and the geometric ledger. A matching CT silhouette would leave the causal and material questions open.
- The independently reconstructed census has 6,862 stage intervals. The recorded endpoints are 2,596 transitions, 1,284 deaths and 2,982 right-censored intervals. After an administrative stop, insufficient larval tissue remains an observed cut state. Whether the animal would eventually complete development is unobserved.
