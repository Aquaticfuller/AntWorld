# WP-5 mechanism diagnosis

The analysis reads 104 accepted WP-4 archives and 139 new WP-5 archives. Software worlds are paired by seed. Multiple descendants, repeated cuts and multiple movements within a world share its history. None supplies a new biological replicate. ## RM-09 cohort survival and stage competition

The full census has 6,862 stage intervals, 2,596 observed transitions, 1,284 deaths and 2,982 right-censored intervals. Recorded deaths include 1,280 energy depletions, three dehydration events and one scheduled fixture interruption. Stage entry is tied to the original event and stable actor ID. Seeded adults contribute time since registration. Later adult transitions have observed time of emergence. Neither value estimates field mortality. Nutrient mass is the realized exposure in the simulation ledger. Actual baseline, stored and absorbed amounts, event-ordered competing outcomes, age in stage and uncertainty in exact pre-death stocks are available separately. [Intervals](../../experiments/s6_4_wp5/analysis/cohort_intervals.csv) [Risk-time aggregates](../../experiments/s6_4_wp5/analysis/uptake_risk_time.csv) [Competing-event estimates](../../experiments/s6_4_wp5/analysis/cohort_incidence.csv)

- In the twelve empty founding cases the queen laid two eggs and died of energy depletion at `680,000,000 ms` (7.870 days) in every case. No world produced its first worker. Enrolled queens completed 16 eligible care actions across six worlds, versus zero without enrollment. The within-seed change was 2.667 per queen (software bootstrap 95% interval 1.667 to 3.667). The event count includes inspection, arrangement, cleaning, feeding and physical custody actions, including transport. It excludes posture and waiting. B01's scan definition is a separate endpoint. The contrast demonstrates an operational care lease and the absence of survival rescue under the frozen reserve and feeding constraints.
- The two original warm-budget controls still die at egg age `500,000,000 ms` (5.787 days), before either observed B06 mean could serve as an assay boundary. The time sentinel and same-tick priority controls retain their separate engineering labels. Of the pooled 1,041 larval intervals, 819 progressed and 173 ended in death. The last comparable cut had sub-threshold tissue in 171 of those 173 deaths. Forty-nine larvae remained censored, 37 of them below the fixture threshold at their last cut. Those 37 are censored observations with a low measured stock. The cut does not establish irreversibility or cause of death.
- All 48 starting workers in the six eight-worker ordinary reference worlds and all 192 in the six 32-worker ordinary reference worlds reached day 21. They also reached day 21 in the corresponding half-worker-reserve contrasts. Despite that worker endpoint, queens reached day 21 in all ordinary reference worlds and in none of the half-reserve worlds. Half-reserve mean day-21 changes in laid eggs were −0.667 (−1.333 to −0.167) for 8 workers and −2.500 (−4.000 to −1.333) for 32 workers. This is a joint energy/water sensitivity, with colony context and host inputs fixed within pair. It does not separate causes such as earlier worker shortage, contact and nutrient routing.
- At the final reference horizon, queens survived in one of six 8-worker depot worlds and six of six 32-worker depot worlds at 180 days. Ordinary 90-day counts were two of six and zero of six. Scarce-protein 90-day counts were two and one. Empty arms had no surviving queens. Absolute starting food depends on initial group size and the arms have different delivery conditions. The data do not identify a smooth demographic curve or a protein effect under equal ingested dose.
- Three energy-depleted mothers had post-death crop carbohydrate of 176, 74 and 55 µg (ordinary 8/97, scarce 8/43 and maternal reserve ordinary 8/7). Their tissue and body stocks, uptake clocks and earlier actual component uptake are available in the per-stage and WP-4 records. Uptake may precede death and also be followed by newly stored food. The pre/post first-uptake split accounts for observed risk time. A survivor contributes post-uptake time only after reaching the pulse. Comparing these risk-time segments as if they were randomized arms would confound early survival with the opportunity to absorb. ## RM-10 autonomous soil work and spatial counterfactuals

- In the 60-second compact cell, the 32-worker cohorts committed 92 digs, removed 848 q³ (106 mm³), and dropped 92 parcels across six worlds. The wet-mass arms committed the same counts, voxel volume and motion schedule. Water mass per q³ was the only material coefficient changed. This is a demonstrated absence of a separate moisture response in the tested configuration. At 4 and 8 workers the compact arms committed no digs. No digs occurred in the roomy, isolated or shielded arms, including 32 workers. This threshold is compatible with the source's local free-space and contact rules. The design changes geometric access and relative occupancy, with no simulated ant body packing or measured soil rheology.
- The two 32-worker joint arms each committed 93 digs and 93 drops and removed 858 q³ across six 21-day worlds. Neither 8-worker joint arm excavated. All joint worlds had zero foraging starts, collections and deliveries. Each of the four group-by-supply cells recorded 12 positive transfers from existing stocks across six worlds. Initial depot geometry is a closed cavity. `can_forage` in this mode requires an available surface step, and the history does not expose the exact eligibility decision at each wake. This is a finite food offer with no observed external intake. No joint world executed a committed dig after its first death. The paired 32-worker soil-volume and deposition differences were zero. The protocol provides no isolated test of feeding effects on sustained construction. Geometry can change autonomously, with no demonstrated maintenance response after colony decline.
- The directed gate is a separate engineering intervention. A real 16 q³ parcel filled the neck at 300 ms. The SQLite midpoint retains the closure and pending crossing. The crossing rejected as `Blocked` at 500 ms. Revision 3 while geometry was at revision 2 rejected as `StaleGeometry`. A second excavation reopened it at 700 ms. A 200 ms 4 mm crossing still failed as `TooFast`; the 500 ms request succeeded at 1,400 ms, with the load held. Drop and pickup then committed at 1,500 and 1,600 ms. The independent geometry replay reproduced closure, material custody, reopened swept clearance and all fact identifiers. The normal autonomous program returns a parcel to the depot; it has no demonstrated policy for planned fill, structural maintenance or learning a new route around permanent blockage. Typed excavation selection and outcome records, actual soil actions, loaded motion, rejections, and program boundaries remain distinct. Cuts include a focal route and its required clearance. Independent six-face cell connectivity describes void, including cells a worker's body might not occupy. Those facts do not reveal unlogged physical work or a latent decision. An apparent resemblance between generated cuboids and a CT silhouette is insufficient to identify initiation, site selection or deposit behavior.

## Comparability

Kramer et al. measured worker survival in queenless, supplied, known-natal-stage cohorts with artificial group assembly. Haatanen et al. measured overwintering with caste- and year-dependent endpoints. Neither measures death risk conditional on the simulation's exact store state. Rasse and Deneubourg's worker-addition study and Toffin et al.'s substrate studies motivate an experiment on initiation and resumption. Leckie and García Ibarra have longitudinal 3D observations, with different groups, substrates and exposures. The obtained Leckie control table is useful for measurement definitions and coarse context. Transported mass and usable raw matched control volumes are still missing.
