The first request should be for biological data, followed by a narrow isolated embryo/reserve experiment only once its condition-specific stocks and outcomes are identifiable. A known-dose uptake timing experiment can proceed in parallel if direct quantities are available. Use separate source colonies or independent data collection for validation. B01/B02, B06 conditional means, already screened Leckie geometry, and software seeds remain development, source-context or engineering evidence. If sources cannot be acquired, retain the currently supported engineering ranges and each open blocker. The proposed focused validation scope is single founding and early worker colonies. It leaves the long-term multi-colony, large 3D-world product architecture intact.

## First biological candidate and approval criteria

Compare the frozen clock and depletion baseline with one candidate that links empirically bounded embryo stock and expenditure to competing hatch and survival in a single matched temperature/humidity condition. Set the cohort clock from actual egg laying or first observation intervals. Assign founding queens to the development and holdout groups before estimating anything. For destructive chemistry measurements use age-stratified sibling subsets with mother identity and a separately followed fate cohort. Keep actual initial energy, usable water and material export on their own ledgers. Counts of viable eggs or mean hatch times alone cannot distinguish larger embryo reserves from slower depletion.

Before requesting code authorization, require stage-specific units, uncertainty and temporal resolution, enough independent mothers to predeclare a margin, and direct records of unsuccessful hatch or missing outcomes. Preserve mother-to-egg material and energy conservation and the original negative budget. A candidate must meet independently frozen margins for both hatch timing and pre-hatch losses, and preserve death ordering, stable identities and exact durable replay. A clock-only match with lost eggs, a viable hatch under sentinel stocks, or a broad interval covering all relevant discrepancies leaves the candidate unqualified. Adult provisioning, maternal allocation and larval ingestion have their own approval and validation stages. Integrate only after the components satisfy their matched endpoints together under common resource exposure. These criteria describe a proposed experiment. They do not authorize edits or biological adoption.
