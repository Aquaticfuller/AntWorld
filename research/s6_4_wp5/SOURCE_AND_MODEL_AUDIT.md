The gate fixture creates the plug with actual transported soil and restores the blocked state with its pending move. Host excavation, fill and crossing are explicit intervention facts. They provide engineering controls for topology, failure and transport. Autonomous volume comes from real edits emitted by self-scheduled workers and excludes initial voids. A dead body with soil, if present, retains its remaining parcel under the existing ownership rules. Its release or recycling would require separate evidence and authorization.

## Minimal proposal for remaining observational gaps

If a later authorized study requires interval-resolved excavation, add a read-only, post-commit record keyed by existing action `ScheduledId`, actor, geometry revision and monotonic simulation time. Separate scheduled start, committed effect, rejection, queue cancellation and interruption cause. Reuse the existing canonical event ID where present, and label derived before/after probes explicitly. A record of a possible target must identify the exact decision cut. It must never query again to reconstruct a past random decision. Any new internal field or save-format change requires separate authorization and exact on/off, failure and replay tests. No such instrumentation is part of WP-5.
