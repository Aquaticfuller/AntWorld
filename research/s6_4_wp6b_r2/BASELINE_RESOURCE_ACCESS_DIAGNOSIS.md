For the 606 R1 primary adults, at pupation the body-water median was 71 µg (67–111), with zero crop water. At eclosion it was 30 µg (24–70), with zero crop water. Pupal maintenance is 3 µg/day in these profiles. The working/basal energy program switch likewise begins under existing adult rules. There is no empirical calibration of those quantitative adult boundaries for *Lasius niger* callows. The adult loss rate is eight times the pupal daily fixture. Pausing the program does not remove that loss.

All 606 died of dehydration with no body, crop, carried, external or nest food water and zero actual adult water absorption. They did not receive a water-positive social gift. The trace records 310,878 committed moves, 312,843 foraging decisions and 4,937 autonomy decisions across these adults. `ForagingDecision` is activity evidence only when linked to a committed action. Separate early-worker worlds have introduced stocks and genuine food paths. The new trial will distinguish absent supply from supply across a physical obstruction, and track actual pickup, delivery, withdrawal, social gift and absorption.
