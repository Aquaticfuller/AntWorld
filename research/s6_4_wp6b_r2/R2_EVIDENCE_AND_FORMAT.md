The local B01 text, Sections 2.1.1 and 2.1.3, differentiates counts per video time from binary ten-second scans in different assays. The inherited R2 day-55 scan is a 50-window model proxy for care and feeding. The paper's 51-window and event-rate endpoints remain separate. R2's same-day stage-resolved counts have different treatment exposure. B06 Methods and Table 2 use daily counts at separate temperatures and the time between first matching counts. Daily and individual-age comparisons are both retained. None is a direct R2 matched feeding or hydration endpoint.

The accepted B2-F04 full text was rechecked on pp. 75–77, Sections 2.1–2.3, Table 1 and Fig. 1. Detrain and Prieur scored antennal contact separately from actual drinking by adult scouts from established *Lasius niger* colonies. For water they report a 4.45 ± 0.9 s mean first bout (SE, 22 observed bouts) and mean acceptance across replicates of 0.024. The time is conditional on drinking. The three-day pre-assay food deprivation with water, source colonies and feeding surface differ from a newly eclosed worker in a founding chamber. The source supports pure-water drinking after local contact and retains nonresponses. It does not identify the model's depot uptake, water dose or adult loss. This is policy B for the R2 application, with its original Atlas A direct experiment classification intact. [B2-F04 primary paper](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jinsphys.2014.03.010)

Kramer, Schaible and Scheuerlein, pp. 19–22, Sections 2.2–2.5 and Figs. 1, 3–4, used six early-stage common-garden cohorts (217 workers) at 22 °C and supplied honey-water and cricket. Workers were collected after the natal colony's first winter. Death accidents were censored. This direct experiment supports a much longer potential lifespan with feeding under those conditions and does not supply first-cohort survival under pure water, age-specific hydration or any larval conversion rates. The relevant unit for comparison is the worker cohort with pooled natal sources. Neither a seven-day simulation endpoint nor the earlier closed-model dehydration diagnoses the natural hazard. [Institutional version of record](https://pure.rug.nl/ws/portalfiles/portal/40659139/1_s2.0_S0531556516303345_main.pdf)

The publisher summary of Baroni Urbani, pp. 229–239, reports that younger *L. niger* larvae could not puncture egg membranes unaided in a laboratory feeding assay. Full methods and event-level records were not obtained in this recheck. It raises the possibility of stage-specific access to different foods, but provides no calibration of first maternal meal timing, lipid accessibility or a liquid dose in worker-free founding. No trophic egg or protein-release mechanism was added. North's publisher summary reports age-related water loss for *Formica rufa*. It is a cross-species research lead and supplies no *L. niger* parameter. [Baroni Urbani](https://doi.org/10.1007/BF01314909) [North](https://doi.org/10.1016/0022-1910(91)90062-5)

## Encoded authority and compatible restore

The R2 checkpoint magic is `AWCKPTH`, its schema is 17, and its physics version is 19. The versioned profile appends two checked bytes to the R1 options. The clock witness appends one eight-byte time after the inherited meal memory. A pending special `CareWake` records the exact locally witnessed larva in its existing payload slot and its source links to the cue. The new canonical fact/block schema is 4 (`AWFACT04`). No SQL table or atomic publication protocol changes. Older checkpoint magics, fact schema versions and encodings continue to dispatch to their original readers.

`FoundingLipidMobilized` (49) records the larval actor, actual lipid debit (µg), resulting energy (nJ), physiological event source and committed time. A same-source physiological record must follow. The payload never includes crop absorption. `FoundingHatchCue` (50) records the queen, funded larval identity, actual hatch source and the scheduled care wake ID. It does not record a selected action or ration. The `CareTick`, opportunity, selection, handling and `FoundingRation` retain their existing identities. Event encoding, participant indices, the history fingerprint and independent budgets link these facts without replaying a choice.

Restore checks profile identity, chronological cue spacing, the source hatch, the shelter and historical clearance, the actual local wake or cancellation, funded emergency conversion and the pending queue. It recognizes a real `InvalidStage` care interruption immediately before a witnessed stage change, so a care interruption can be restored without moving or skipping the transition. The tests exercise both file and SQLite recovery, malformed version/witness, explicit lost admissibility, a funded larva behind a soil plug, and failure after a partial transaction or publication. Observation never changes an authoritative decision, queue entry or reserved resource.
