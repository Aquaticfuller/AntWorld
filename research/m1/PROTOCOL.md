## Outcome definitions

- At days 90, 150 and 240 where scheduled, record queen status and death, live individuals by stage, egg births, hatches, pupations, eclosions, and deaths by last live stage and recorded cause. Keep larvae still alive as censored outcomes.
- Keep deliveries measured as committed transfer totals by ingredient. Count external gathering, deposit, trail cue, observed cue, activation and later food delivery by a recruited actor separately. Standalone water is a water-only load. Record equivalent counts and the last successful delivery if later releases leave resources outside.
- Compute time with an empty carbohydrate or water component of nest stock after the first eclosion by replaying the canonical depot deposits, withdrawals and protein recycling. Count whole simulated days with both external supply and workers alive but no deposit. Count actual excavation and deposition with edit volumes.
- Accumulate recorded adult worker motion duration and length using committed movement and its prior authoritative position. Do not impute an unknown segment duration. Use daily samples of actual adult worker positions for maximum close pairs within the configured body-width proxy. Report that occupancy measurement separately from an actual rejected action.
- Record native wall time, process RSS, compressed history bytes at measurement cuts, all resource ledgers, and SHA-256 of initial native checkpoint, canonical journal prefix and native frame at each retained cut. Keep unsuccessful, blocked and censored runs in the aggregates. Use OS/architecture and binary hash in each receipt.

## Bounds and delivery checks

Predict no population target. A valid candidate must start from real data, keep all ledgers balanced and stop normally at the same committed cut after save and restore. Test carry compatibility and that no cross-wall access appears in the closed control. If paired endpoints disagree across machines, preserve platform-specific digests and report the disagreement. Use paired changes or counts and include unfavorable changes.

Use a 120-second wall budget per run with a deadline checked between daily cuts and a 192 MiB native diagnostic history ceiling. A Core capacity limit or elapsed budget stops the run at its last confirmed daily cut. Report the stop date, reason and completed evidence. For archives, stream the exact little-endian length-prefixed facts directly into ZIP compression. Use no duplicate raw fact export. A single witness may occupy up to 24 MiB on disk. A witness that exceeds the archive budget must have a recorded failure, never a truncated journal presented as complete. Verify hashes and archives independently via the existing OP0 importer.
