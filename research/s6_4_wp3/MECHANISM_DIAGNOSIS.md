# WP-3 foraging and recruitment baseline diagnosis

The frozen matrix completed 174 independent fixture worlds and 54 detached paths across six fixed software seeds. Independent verification read 93,041 canonical facts and reconciled 92,969 projected fact bytes, 4,026 cuts and both checkpoint anchors in every live world. All 72 excluded canonical facts remain in the raw files. The archive contains 702 individual summaries and 1,067 reconstructed trip episodes: 613 delivered, 139 returned empty and 315 open at the horizon. The 72 recorded deaths were scheduled scout retirements after a successful delivery. No natural deaths or rejected actions occurred in these short behavioral windows. The field, resource state, interventions, both checkpoints, source-depot state and event histories remain available for each case. ## RM-07 local search, intake and loaded return

| Frozen comparison | Committed local-food choice / physical arrival / actual collection / actual delivery | Interpretation |
|---|---|---|
| Near, each trail and memory cell | 6/6 for all four outcomes | All workers completed the first trip in this accessible layout. The initial resource was three cardinal cells from the nest and outside the two-cell local reach. |
| Far, each trail and memory cell | 4/6 / 4/6 / 4/6 / 3/6 | Two workers never recorded a local food choice. One worker collected but had not delivered by 120 s. The paired treatment differences for these binary outcomes were zero in this fixture. |
| Open / wall / bypass | 6/6 / 0/6 / 1/6 completed delivery respectively | The spanning wall prevented cue, arrival and collection for all six workers despite continued movement. The bypass was physically possible and found once in six windows. |
| Stock depleted at 20 s / empty | 3/6 / 0/6 completed delivery respectively | All six scheduled fixture debits consumed the remaining source. Three workers collected before the debit, continued search, and recorded one memory discard each. The other three had no learned route at depletion. |

For the trail-absent, memory-on comparator, the median across paired worlds of first positive collection was 22.019 s near and 56.150 s among the four successful far worlds. The median matched collection-to-delivery time was 5.505 s near and 40.737 s among the three successful far worlds. Across all delivered trips the median loaded leg was 4.404 s and the median summed loaded endpoint displacement was 30 mm. These times are conditional on completing the relevant event. The analysis preserves all denominators, temporal bounds and sources rather than assigning an arrival to censored trips. [Groups and conditional denominators](../../experiments/s6_4_wp3/analysis/group_metrics.csv)

The near no-trail arms gave 36 same-source collections after a prior delivery with memory enabled and 6 with memory disabled across the same six seeds. The paired difference in the fraction of first deliverers making any same-source repeat was +33.3 percentage points, bootstrap 95% interval [0, +66.7]. The corresponding paired difference in summed movement was −98 mm, interval [−183, −23]. There was no change in initial collection time. The trail-by-memory interaction for any repeat was +16.7 points, interval [−50.0, +83.3]. The far-arm repeat comparison has only three eligible pairs. These are sensitivity results for the fixture, with exposure after first delivery and finite remaining stock as part of the realized trajectories. [Paired contrasts](../../experiments/s6_4_wp3/analysis/paired_contrasts.csv)

After depletion, the 20 s scheduled debit was 8,400 µg in three worlds and 10,000 µg in three worlds. Five additional physical visits to the now-empty feeder cell by the focal worker were retained across the three previously successful cases. Visits and positive transfers are different endpoints. The actual Core returns the remembered path under load and can drop an empty remembered route during outbound search. Static barriers and removal of stock did not test a dynamically invalidated loaded path. The source audit finds no general local repair policy for that case. The empty and partition controls supply unambiguous negative delivery evidence within their horizons. [Exact inputs and movements](../../experiments/s6_4_wp3/baseline)

## RM-08 marked route, departure and delivery

All 72 scout/density worlds recorded a first scout delivery and its subsequent retirement. Pre-release field strength matched between present and blind cases for each seed and age. At release the fresh present fields ranged from 2.925 to 26.778 effective units summed across cells. The 120 s arm retained a quarter of the matched field to the precision of the recorded snapshots. The 600 s arm had no active field at release. Newly started workers could deposit fresh marks later, so that arm isolates launch conditions and cannot guarantee a field-free remainder.

| Newcomer pathway | Fresh present | Fresh blind | Fresh absent |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Host-started departed | 12/12 | 12/12 | 12/12 |
| Host-started positive collection / delivery | 11/12 / 11/12 | 8/12 / 8/12 | 8/12 / 8/12 |
| Ordinary policy selected Forage and departed | 6/6 | 6/6 | 6/6 |
| Ordinary policy positive collection / delivery | 6/6 / 5/6 | 5/6 / 5/6 | 5/6 / 5/6 |
| Passive departure / delivery | 0/6 / 0/6 | 0/6 / 0/6 | 0/6 / 0/6 |

Among host-started workers, the paired fresh present-minus-blind delivery difference was +25.0 percentage points, interval [0, +58.3]. The first positive collection contrast among the five seeds with an event in both arms was +18.497 s, interval [−6.826, +43.600]. Fresh signaling can affect routes among already-enrolled workers in the implementation. The direction of the conditional time comparison cannot measure activation or imply a population-wide speed benefit. The delivery counts in present declined to 7/12 and 7/12 for host starts after aging and expiry, and the policy delivered 3/6 and 5/6. All passive workers stayed without a departure in their observed windows, and all policy-enrolled workers departed across the ages. The Core's ordinary policy has no explicit scout-triggered departure channel. [Group and age outcomes](../../experiments/s6_4_wp3/analysis/group_metrics.csv)

In the density arms, the median living-neighbor count near the policy worker at release was 2, 7 and 31 for sparse-8, dense-8 and dense-32. The median summed candidate work reported by committed policy wakes was 3, 18 and 48 with a median of 18 policy decisions per arm. Host-started and ordinary-policy delivery counts were unchanged (11/12 and 5/6 respectively). The extra workers were passive; this comparison changes spatial opportunity and policy query work while keeping the active pool fixed. It cannot estimate behavioral crowding among dozens of moving foragers.

In the detached 24-step assay the absent and blind paths were identical for each matching seed and age, while blind choices could retain a marked-edge label. Fresh present-minus-blind time on the line was +0.83 steps, interval [−4.0, +6.5]. The difference in the initial consecutive streak was −0.67 steps, interval [−3.83, +2.67]. These seeds do not establish sustained following. The earlier, larger S2.2 negative social-recruitment result stays intact. [Prepared path receipts](../../experiments/s6_4_wp3/analysis/prepared.csv) [S2.2 results](../../S2_2_RESULTS.md)

The sensor cuts expose potential local substrate cues even for a passive ant. They provide neither its actual receptor exposure nor its unrecorded perception and response. A committed choice, its eventual movement, actual debit and actual delivery are separate records. `FollowTrail` labels a chosen marked edge in both response arms. Host starts are interventions. Policy departures are observed outcomes, with no identified causal scout signal. These distinctions keep the RM-08 recruitment mechanism diagnosis separate from its engineering completion.
