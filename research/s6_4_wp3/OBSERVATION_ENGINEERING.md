# WP-3 host observer engineering

WP-3 adds an application scope to the accepted RM-02 Sidecar. The exact canonical codec and all existing plan identities are retained. The opt-in scope reads committed forager starts, choices, movements, resource debits and deliveries, lifecycle, field maintenance and relevant spatial or nutrition facts. It uses the existing asynchronous publisher, cursor validation, durable commit, checkpoint binding and duplicate handling. The physiology, motion, RNG, authority transactions and on-disk world format are the accepted WP-2 trees.

| Evidence | Provenance and boundary |
|---|---|
| Choice, event reason, actor and resource, scheduled source, mark, result, real nutrient components | `COMMITTED_FACT`. The `ForagingDecision` records the selection, and each successful action resolves separately. A resource on a decision may be the first recorded available cue. `LocalFood` identifies a directed choice. |
| Current living neighbors, legal local steps, available food, current path and learned cells, effective trail field, current food and individual stores | `CUT_SNAPSHOT`. Each row is keyed to the owner-thread time, stable WorldId/actor and canonical fact cursor. Field values and neighborhood counts expose spatial availability at a completed cut. |
| Physical departure, feeder-cell arrival, loaded nest arrival, length, trips and cohort totals | `RECONSTRUCTED` from consecutive actual committed endpoints, cargo transfer and seed facts. The analyst's feeder coordinates remain outside the motor. Censor windows and competing death are retained with event IDs. |
| True sensed stimulus, direction-specific encounters, unseen legal candidates at a decision, actual random draw, and cue cause of activation | `UNOBSERVED`. A cut query after a decision cannot fill the gap. The observer never reruns a historical policy. |

The app writes bounded cut projections and separate source-state receipts. Each live case pairs an authoritative segmented SQLite midpoint with exact checkpoint bytes and the Sidecar anchor. It reopens the Sidecar, verifies duplicate suppression, and continues. A matched observer-off run uses the same seeded state and interventions and produces byte-identical final world state and history. Independent replay of the archived records verifies canonical fact coverage, ordering and digests, exact cut joins, the store integrity check, spatial cue availability, and nutrient, energy and soil ledgers. The app retains unmatched and excluded authoritative facts in its canonical export. Tests force a publication error before commit, verify the old durable cursor and world bytes, and retry; independent checks reject a corrupted digest and an auxiliary cursor change. Auxiliary CSVs are bound to the final archive hashes and cuts. An interrupted process can leave them incomplete; the verifier rejects incomplete case receipts. Durable cursor continuation applies to the Sidecar.

For stronger causal observation, an independently reviewed future extension could emit a small read-only decision receipt only after the matching world commit, with WorldId, wake ID, actor, legal-cue mask, effective trail use and proposed ScheduledId. This would associate a choice with its later resolution and distinguish arithmetic participation from a marked-edge label. A separate policy receipt would be necessary to measure in-nest encounter-based eligibility if a future model implements it. The proposal requires exact on/off canonical, state, RNG and checkpoint equality, bounded optional recording and unchanged actor selection. This sprint leaves those internals uninstrumented. A receipt would report model execution and would still require empirical validation.

The case archive and tests cover actual positive and partial collections, delivery, no reachable target, zero delivery, inventory depletion, endpoint censoring, an explicit retirement, on/off continuation, split advancement, rejection of a mismatched plan, and SQLite recovery. No rejected action or natural mortality occurred in the frozen behavioral matrix. These outcome classes remain supported by the fact reader and the inherited transaction tests.
