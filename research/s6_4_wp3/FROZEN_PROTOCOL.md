## Frozen case matrix

The executable matrix is `tools/s6_4_wp3_cases.py`. Each of six fixed software seeds (1, 7, 19, 42, 43, 97) occurs once in every cell. Retain all cases, including failures. Each world uses one 21 by 15 planar grid with 10 mm spacing and known nest at cell (10,7). No application supplies a route or an out-of-range resource target to a worker's behavior.

| Family | Cases | Design and observable endpoints |

| Scout | 54 | Train one scout in a near layout for at most 240 s. Advance to its first committed delivery, then retire it 1 ms later, following the established S2.1 comparison. World windows use start, 5 s regular cuts and an exact middle anchor after release. Scout setup and release are explicitly timestamped. Training may advance to pending action boundaries to detect a committed delivery and it is part of observed history. Report elapsed times from the documented release or first acquisition; a scheduled retirement is a fixture death and excluded from newcomer survival. Compare matching seed/profile contrasts for trail and memory, including the difference in trail effect by memory treatment. Report event-probability denominators, conditional latency summaries, horizon-censored and competing death counts, and 95% paired bootstrap intervals across six seeds (4,096 draws, fixed seed 64207). Ants and repeated trips in a world are clustered at world/seed. A trajectory length is the sum of committed endpoint displacements and excludes coordinates lacking a preceding committed position. Positive delivery is a distinct terminal event. Aggregate masses cannot substitute for these endpoints.

## Controlled performance and engineering gates

Run Debug, Release, ASan/UBSan and cross-platform CI, with on/off and restored/uninterrupted evidence. Formal observer benchmarking uses inherited dense-512, dense-2,048 and depot-32 inputs and workload/count/hashes. Three admitted ABBA/BAAB sessions, four warm-up rows, four off/off rows and twelve four-row timed blocks per cell per session. Use the accepted native collector, pinned binary, 60 one-second admission samples and the frozen [v2 instrument](../../docs/55_S6_3_3_1_Performance_Instrument_Protocol.md), valid HWiNFO frequency, temperature and power. At most three attempts per session and at least 300 s after a rejection. Do not repeat a started session or overlap work on the host with formal timing. Apply P50 ≤1.10×, P95 ≤1.20×, off/off P50 0.90–1.10, foreground cut P95 ≤5 ms and max ≤20 ms, paired incremental RSS ≤16 MiB. Include actual SQLite writes and terminal drain in total and report both separately. Maintain the established TD-01 and TD-08 ≤6× gates on their original workloads, not on unequal new fixture rows. Preserve reference and observed results independently.

The native comparison covers the WP-3 canonical publisher. Per-case time, query/serialization time, bytes, queue and terminal drain measure the supplemental host cut view with its costs included in the whole-case wall time. Where an admitted test cannot run, keep receipts and report `NOT_RUN` or `INCONCLUSIVE`. Matched unadmitted comparisons, if collected, are descriptive and cannot replace the gates. Keep all outliers and adverse outcomes. Report formal qualification and descriptive costs separately. Scientific comparison never uses software timings as biological observations.
