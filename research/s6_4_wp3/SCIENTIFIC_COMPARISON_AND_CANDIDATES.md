# WP-3 biological comparability and candidate models

The current observation supports a local computational route response under some fixture conditions. BR-06 and BR-07 remain `BLOCKED`. The independent data register distinguishes feeder arrival after a worker has entered a bridge, choices of a marked arm, behavior inside a nest before departure, and completed collection and loaded return. Each requires its own experimental denominator.

## What is directly supported

- Local food and prior experience influence foraging. The primary assays manipulate sucrose conditions, known paths, landmark availability or maze confinement. They support responses within each apparatus. They leave the Core's exact local stock query, deterministic visibility radius, perfect reversal of a loop-erased route, five-minute expiry and uniform distance tie mechanism without matched parameter data. One marked route assay improved an unmarked later test and another did not detect that improvement. Papers F01/F03 share trajectory material and count as one underlying trajectory source wherever they overlap. [Batch 2 conflicts](../s6_4_preflight/BATCH2_EVIDENCE_CONFLICTS.md) [Route learning P03](https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0149720)
- Adults choose or remain near marked substrates in controlled assays. P04's published marked-arm choices (348/398) concern a forced choice. Its marked arena assay did not show a significant speed or straightness benefit. Prior departure eligibility, long-distance discovery and returned nutrient mass were not tested. In the Core, `FollowTrail` records selection of a marked edge; the same label can occur in the zero-response arm. [P04](https://doi.org/10.1007/s00040-026-01106-9) [WP-3 source audit](SOURCE_AND_EVIDENCE_AUDIT.md)
- Adult marks depend on the experienced situation. P06 reported deposition variation by runway position and source distance and did not find an increase after the initial wrong turn in its assay. P15 estimated behavioral efficacy by exposing another colony to aged markings and fitting an exponential to choices. Neither provides a universal per-cell amount, a chemical half-life or an intra-nest activation threshold. The new interferometric preprint measures physical deposits on glass. It is a one-source-colony study, and deposit volume and loss phases do not measure antennal concentration or behavioral efficacy. [P06](https://doi.org/10.1007/s00040-024-00995-y) [P15](https://doi.org/10.1007/BF01201674) [Interferometry preprint and raw archive](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.18079) [Dataset](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.20697871)
- P09 observed departures after the return of a single scout, contact strata and starvation treatments. The feeder was disconnected after the initial scout, so a departure could not be linked to acquisition or loaded return. The separate Sakiyama and Wada bridge assay conditioned on introduced workers and used arrival at the distal bridge edge, not a nest departure or a recorded food transfer. Its workbook provides a candidate held-out arrival endpoint within that apparatus. It cannot validate an entire recruitment chain, and some non-arrivals are represented by normalized estimates in a separate sheet. [P09](https://doi.org/10.1007/s00040-011-0177-7) [Sakiyama and Wada](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-025-02058-z) [Data screen](DATA_ACQUISITION_AND_VALIDATION_READINESS.md)

The active trail field, caps and decay are `ENGINEERING_FIXTURE`. The same-colony gate differs from the different-donor/recipient assay in P15. Vision and species-specific contact sensing are absent from the foraging choices; real physical barriers and local position are implemented. Congener and other-ant studies may inform experimental methods. They cannot supply *L. niger* estimates for the motor or recruitment rule. [Accepted source classification and mapped parameters](MECHANISM_TRANSITION_PARAMETER_MAP.csv)

## Smallest research candidates

| Candidate | Minimal mechanism to compare after authorization | Falsification and holdout |
|---|---|---|
| BR-06 local discovery | Keep the current reachable candidate set and physical collection transaction. Consider a local detection-time hazard with a visibility-distance term and finite attention clock; compare to a fixed reach mask. Keep food quantity known only on committed contact if the data support that boundary. | First match geometry, feeder accessibility, odor/landmark conditions and time origin. Collect all approaches, missed opportunities, true first contact and dose, with blind observers and independent colonies. A range law is unidentifiable from pickup time alone. Reject if it fails held-out missed-approach and detection latency distributions or shifts pickup/return errors into another stage. |
| BR-06 memory and route recovery | Compare exact cached route to the smallest local stale-edge rule. On a genuinely blocked chosen predecessor, preserve cargo, record the failed edge and allow bounded local search with a separately measured forgetting rule. Keep an explicit unresolved route terminal and movement clearance. | First obtain marked outbound, blocked-edge, and loaded-return trajectories of the same workers, with no-change and empty-source controls. Separate a food cue disappearing from the motor's physical route failure. Evaluate alternate route probability, extra turns, delay and non-return by held-out source colony. Do not supply a global nest route or guarantee delivery. |
| BR-07 scout-driven departure | Compare the current ordinary departure process to one extra bounded, decaying eligibility signal from an observed local scout encounter or experimentally isolated entrance cue. Keep departure, directional response and dose acquisition as separate transitions. A zero signal gain is a valid comparison. | Use randomized scout/no-scout and trail/sham treatments with familiar territory, controlled feeding and deprivation, marked passive recipients, and linked contact, exit, choice, collection and returned mass. Hold out whole source colonies. Reject a proposed mechanism if the pre-departure hazard or direction changes fail the registered contrast even when final food increases. Never encode the feeder coordinates in the activation event. |

Start with a matched BR-06 post-departure bridge assay as the smallest partial validation opportunity after confirming the treatment geometry and endpoint. It can test a motor-level encounter effect on travel time separately from BR-07. Preserve the Sakiyama and Wada workbook as evaluation material if this model is developed from other sources. If it informs parameter estimates, designate a different cohort for validation. The highest-priority BR-07 change after new matched data is a local departure signal. Existing data are insufficient to adopt it or choose a dose-response coefficient.

Implementations and any new Core instrumentation require independent authorization. An eligible future experiment would report completed trajectories, lack of response, failure, death and right-censoring at the individual level. Calibration and held-out scoring use separate source colonies, and neither uses software seeds as biological repeats.
