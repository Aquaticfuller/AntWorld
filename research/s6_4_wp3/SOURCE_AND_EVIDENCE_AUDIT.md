## Evidence classes and predictions

The papers above give direct species data for their own observed endpoints. The accepted source map carries cross-species and engineering records separately. Exact local contents, home-cell trace, deterministic memory priority, behavioral aging cutoff, site-specific deposition and the scalar weights remain assumptions or `ENGINEERING_FIXTURE`. No parameter or prior evidence classification changes. WP-3 tests whether exact local access produces overly prompt cue-to-collection behavior, whether a known route changes repeated visitation after a depletion event, and whether trail-following can alter results among workers that are already searching without an endogenous scout-driven departure mechanism.

Do not equate visits with contacts, the order of adjacent events with causality, total food collected with food delivered, or conditional time to discovery with the population discovery probability. Use paired treatments for effects within a simulation and independent colonies for biological inference.
