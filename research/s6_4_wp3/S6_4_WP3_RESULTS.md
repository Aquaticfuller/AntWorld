The direct outcome differences occur at different stages. All near foragers collected and delivered, while the far profile produced four collections and three deliveries per six-seed cell. Memory increased same-source revisits after a completed trip in the near fixture, with no measurable first-trip incidence change. A static wall prevented discovery and delivery despite movement. Depletion produced observed repeat search and memory discards without positive post-depletion acquisition. The recruited group was separated into host starts, ordinary policy activation and passive subjects. Fresh trail responsiveness increased observed delivery incidence among host starts from 8/12 to 11/12, paired 95% interval for the difference [0, +58.3] percentage points. Ordinary policy departure incidence did not change and passive workers stayed inactive. The result does not establish that a scout cue induces departure in this model or in the species.
