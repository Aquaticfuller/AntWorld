# WP-3 independent data acquisition and readiness

The access state in this report describes a specific file or source. `VERIFIED` means that the listed bytes, published endpoint, or limited catalog scope was inspected. Scientific eligibility is separate. Prior received material retains its accepted status and history. No full matched `VAL-BR-xx` set was established. Use one source colony as the biological clustering unit unless a specific assay establishes independent founders or colonies by design. The published bridge workbook gives completed-arrival means ± SD of 17.13 ± 7.12 s (inward traffic, 42 workers in 4 colonies), 23.88 ± 15.87 s (trail and no traffic, 50 in 4), 26.74 ± 16.66 s (neither treatment, 43 in 4) and 22.87 ± 11.26 s (bidirectional traffic, 36 in 10). These are descriptive recalculations of the exact workbook rows. They leave the original mixed-model inference separate. The sensitivity sheet retains four extra inward and two extra bidirectional rows estimated by multiplying time per observed centimeter by 30 cm. Those values are projections for non-arrivals, with a different interpretation from a measured arrival or a raw censor time. The authors also excluded retreat, failure to enter the bridge, falls and treatment-specific encounter failures. Each target worker had one trial; multiple workers from the same source colony are clustered, and within-trial frames are repeated measurements. The observation window starts after introduction at the apparatus. No stimulus-to-nest-exit contrast is available from this workbook.
