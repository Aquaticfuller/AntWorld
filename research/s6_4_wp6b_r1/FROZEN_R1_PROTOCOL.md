## Single candidate rule

F1-R1 remembers the stable recipient ID, time and EventId of the founder's last positive committed F1 ration. One slot per world suffices for the single enrolled founder. At a later ordinary care decision, a valid remembered larva can be selected for FeedBrood with probability 750,000 parts per million, conditional on passing the existing care admission. Check it only within the same local contact and candidate set used by ordinary care, including reach, shelter, life stage, custody and own lineage. The normal payment calculation must have a positive payable protein dose. A distinct deterministic individual decision substream supplies the additional draw. Failed repeat draws use the unchanged ordinary draws. The original competing actions, including worker contact, retrieval and return, keep precedence. Ration calculation, committed payment, handling duration, locking and scheduled timing are inherited.

The memory window is 172,800,000 ms (48 h). Elapsed time greater than or equal to the window expires the preference. The nominal six-hour cadence gives about eight chances per window and retains a 25% chance of using ordinary selection at any eligible decision. A paid feed switches or renews the slot. Death, exit from the larval stage, explicit disabling of founder autonomy or interruption of feeding to the remembered target clears it. Other locally detected ineligibility clears it at the next care wake. A temporarily unsampled witness cannot confer a preference. R1 has no global hunger ranking, advance knowledge of successful development, extra care wake or stock injection. A wider allocation, faster search, altered laying or new nutritional rule would require a separate experiment.
