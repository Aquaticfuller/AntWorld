# Prospective available-crop uptake timing ablation

In the initial durable dietary probe, a balanced Borisovka queen laid 33 eggs with explicit body-energy payment and remained alive past day 120. At death on day 120.142735 she owned 150 µg carbohydrate in crop, 20 µg crop protein, 5,397 µg body water and zero body energy. At the fixed 8,000,000 nJ/µg yield this carbohydrate could have provided 1.2 J. The existing clock rejects a depleted animal before the ordinary uptake pulse. A corresponding N-only nominal probe persisted to day 150 without renewing the original lipid packet. Preserve both probes and the full independent N/E comparison.

## Candidate U

Permit an event-driven use of already owned crop at an enrolled adult's exact resource boundary when the missing component has a positive ordinary uptake allowance. Only the exhausted compartment may receive a transfer. Credit actual lost crop mass to absorbed water or oxidized carbohydrate and the existing energy ledger. The one-millisecond minimum is not added to death. If all depleted compartments cannot be supplied together, do not change either and commit death under the existing tie priority. A normal pulse at the same time consumes the eligible dose once under its existing combined allowance. A boundary between pulses uses the exact elapsed time since the last assimilation for that component, with integer rates and remainder. Retain independent original ordinary pulse alignment, so a paid earlier portion cannot also spend the same elapsed rate interval at the next pulse. A zero crop, blocked source and actual supply exhaustion can still be fatal.

This is a continuous-uptake approximation for available stock with an event boundary implementation. Mechanism grade C, numerical timing and rates D. Absorption timing under starvation is still unmeasured for the target population. It does not create nutrient, extend a lifetime without material or apply to eggs, larvae or pupae. In particular a maternal ingestion fact earlier in the history is a necessary predecessor in the diagnostic death case, and a later donation cannot rescue it.

## Experiment and safety

Test U over the full 84 nominal and 192 factorial supplied worlds against N+E and its controls. Test U at the same 4 and 12 million nJ/µg endpoints and in the exact closed-stock original 960. The same horizon, six seeds, factors, measured actions, B06 reader and gates apply. Require actual boundary crop depletion and tracked receipt, conserved energy and water, restored timing, determinism, no repeated allowance, no recovery with insufficient ingredient, and unchanged earlier encodings. Give U an explicit version and witness. Report additional costs and any failed pairs. This change does not by itself guarantee adequate stock or a replacement worker.
