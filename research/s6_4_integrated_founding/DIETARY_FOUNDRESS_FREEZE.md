# Prospective founding depot and reproductive allocation ablations

The P+Y nominal balanced cohort at C++ revision `c7981e6` completed all twelve runs. All twelve queens had actual carbohydrate and water uptake after real delivery. Across the queens 521 worker gifts supplied 5,826 µg carbohydrate, 240 µg protein and 33,311 µg water. They absorbed 4,963 µg carbohydrate and 29,872 µg water. All twelve died of energy exhaustion. Several queens died with positive carbohydrate in crop and actual available carbohydrate in the nest. The unchanged funded egg rule requires original lipid and mobile protein, even after repeated food deliveries. R2 and P+Y remain explicit controls.

## New independently selectable changes

- N, queen access to physical nest food. A queen at her reachable own nest samples the existing delivered food store on an already scheduled six-hour pulse, immediately after that pulse's ordinary absorption and reserve conversion. She can ingest a bounded amount if physically in contact. It becomes ordinary crop content for a later absorption pulse. No remote access, direct assimilation, free water, special survival allowance, guaranteed intake or new task wake. Bound the demand to maximum declared basal/active maintenance through two ordinary pulses, subtract available body and crop resource, and cap by actual store, crop room and existing transfer limit. A dietary egg candidate can also request one egg's protein in crop. Keep legacy local worker-to-queen gifts separately observable. This represents access to a delivered liquid pool in the protected apparatus. A shared pool is an engineering approximation, and experimental trophallaxis does not establish this precise geometry, dose or rate. Policy C mechanism, D `ENGINEERING_FIXTURE`.
- E, dietary egg funding. When a founder has actually assimilated dietary carbohydrate, use the funds already in her body, water and crop or mobile protein. Permit an egg backed by the same energy equivalent as its original lipid allocation. Debit and record energy export, overhead, crop and/or mobile protein and water before birth. The new egg retains exactly that energy in its own body and no lipid from this payment. Check two ordinary pulses of post-payment maintenance in the founder's actual body plus retained lipid for energy and actual body water for hydration. The shorter guard applies only to the paid dietary route. With no actual dietary carbohydrate assimilation the original ten-day reserve rule and lipid egg remain. A nutrient-poor or exhausted queen can still be unable to reproduce or die. Energy equivalence is a model assumption, not a claim about embryo substrate composition or measured maintenance times. No carbohydrate-to-lipid mass creation. Policy C for use of dietary energy and protein by provisioning, D for the two-pulse and egg-packet rules. [Direct founder provisioning, Methods and 49-day endpoints](https://doi.org/10.1093/biolinnean/blad149) [Post-emergence diet and context, Methods 2.1.1](https://doi.org/10.1111/1365-2435.14536)

Keep P, Y, original H1/H2, stage clocks, maturation requirements, dose limits, depletion priorities and source budgets. N and E select a new version-5 profile and independent checkpoint and block identifiers. In N-only, the queen can load a real dietary dose and still stop laying when her original lipid or mobile protein is exhausted. In E-only, the workers are the source of any queen crop input. No host offers queen food directly. Earlier encodings, stock packet events and version-4 histories remain exact. Paid body-energy eggs carry a new companion fact. The ordinary scheduled depletion boundary still preempts food paid after death.

## Design and falsifiers

Run N, E and N+E in all 84 nominal environments and N+E in all 192 frozen factorial environments. Repeat N+E with 4 and 12 million nJ/µg in the twelve nominal balanced worlds. Run the exact closed-stock original 960 cases with the final candidate and compare first-worker and B06 gates using the original reader. Report any difference due to interaction among workers after first emergence. Keep ordinary adverse controls and actual reserve, crop, time, attempt, competing exit and movement facts. The main N test is timely actual C/W receipt and uptake following worker delivery. The main E test is energy- and protein-backed subsequent birth and its whole outcome. The frozen 150-day world and factorial gates still apply. Terminal survival without a resource and reproduction chain does not qualify.

Restore N and E pending original clocks and validate local authorized meal, source debit, actual subsequent uptake, dietary birth, and both energy ledgers. Test exhausted, disjoint, unavailable and competing cases. No assay changes are introduced in response to these ablations. The known precision and time-scale limitations of the B06 measurement remain.
