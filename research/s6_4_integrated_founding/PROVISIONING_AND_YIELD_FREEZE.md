# Prospective local provisioning and sugar yield experiment

The R2 environment-only pilot at `7818e20` executed all 84 declared nominal worlds. Each of the 12 balanced worlds produced actual external trips and returns, and across these worlds workers delivered 93 parcels. Three queens received an actual worker gift. The queens jointly absorbed 58 µg carbohydrate and 239 µg water. All twelve queens died of dehydration. This separates loaded navigation from intranidal receipt and metabolism. Water-only and carbohydrate-only each deliver actual resources. All blocked and absent worlds have zero collection and zero delivery. The complete pilot is archived. Keep the approved gate, all prior evidence and the original four-corner parameter envelopes.

## Orthogonal candidates

- P, local provisioning. At a completed depot return, use the existing sampled local need, physically present nest stock and adult's actual crop to offer the ordinary Nurse program when a non-urgent worker can serve a contact. An urgent worker keeps the existing self-first policy. The behavior and actual dose still resolve through the existing nutrition proposal and transaction. At adult eclosion, an enrolled worker's first autonomy evaluation takes place at the next valid boundary. This avoids applying a six-hour task tick to a newly starting individual. A later nutrition opportunity uses a one-hour adult behavior interval for this assay. A donor protects its own projected carbohydrate and water for the next scheduled absorption boundary, based on current body stocks, owned crop and declared expenditure. Restrict gifts at proposal and commit. Preserve the existing limit and encounter draw, the opportunity to decline, stock depletion, local range and clearance. Ordinary care, queen behavior and six-hour absorption stay in place. A matched no-supply or obstructed world receives no resource. The hypothesized effect is a larger fraction of actual nest-delivery worlds achieving subsequent queen and larval receipt and uptake. Hypothesis grade C, contact timing grade D `ENGINEERING_FIXTURE`.
- Y, sugar availability. Treat the carbohydrate fraction of this explicit assay as a dry soluble sugar. The gross combustion scale of sucrose is about 16.48 J/mg, or 16,480,000 nJ/µg. The original effective yield is 1,000,000 nJ/µg. At that yield a nominal 2.34 J/day queen requires 2,340 µg/day even before laying or nursing costs. The balanced source releases 1,500 µg carbohydrate/day and shares this with workers. Test 8,000,000 nJ/µg (about 49% of gross), with 4,000,000 and 12,000,000 as fixed sensitivity endpoints. The colony consumes mass through exactly the same existing crop absorption. This is grade C as a physically constrained effective model and grade D `ENGINEERING_FIXTURE` as a numeric efficiency in ants. A positive actual dose is still necessary. Do not interpret the gross heat as effective ant metabolism. [NIST sucrose thermochemistry, solid combustion](https://webbook.nist.gov/cgi/cbook.cgi?ID=C57501&Mask=2B&Units=CAL) [Detrain and Prieur, Results and Table 1](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jinsphys.2014.03.010)
