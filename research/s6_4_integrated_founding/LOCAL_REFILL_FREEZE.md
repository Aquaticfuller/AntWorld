## Candidate F

At an already scheduled ordinary depot decision, an eligible adult with a past successful collection may repeat independent foraging when its own projected carbohydrate or water shortfall, or a sampled adult nest contact's projected shortfall, cannot be covered from the current reachable depot. Use the maximum eligible individual shortfall by ingredient. Count the current body resource, owned crop and mobile lipid that can still pay the energy bill. Project through two existing autonomy intervals and subtract the current local stock. Continue to use the existing action duration, eligibility, self-feeding precedence, motion and pickup constraints, exploration, local cues, stored navigation trace and physical return. Do not add wakes, reach, sensing, target coordinates, material, designated workers, recruitment, changes to lifespan or extra donor rights. An empty source can still be searched unsuccessfully. A wall can still block all collection. An unexperienced worker still uses the existing policy. Prior successful contact is taken from the worker's own historical foraging record. Any contacted recipient is sampled through the existing spatial mechanism. No exact future consumption or brood outcome enters the choice.

The two-interval demand window is a D-grade `ENGINEERING_FIXTURE` carried over from the two-interval buffer rationale. It is not a measured starvation window. A local resource threshold and repeat trips have bounded biological plausibility (C). Mailleux et al. filmed independent scouting in standard incipient *Lasius niger* nests and measured first departures, drinking and repeat source visits. Their smallest group had up to 25 ants and mean colony age near 11 months. Manipulating colony size affected group foraging, and scouts returned without the focal colony receiving the scout in the individual assay. These observations constrain the existence of repeat trips and independent foraging (B for use here). They do not identify the nest-stock proxy, a two-interval forecast or the response of newly emerged workers. No global colony-size response, trail signal or particular loaded speed is inferred. [Mailleux, Deneubourg and Detrain 2003, Methods §§2–4, Table 1, pp. 25–28](https://doi.org/10.1007/s000400300004)
