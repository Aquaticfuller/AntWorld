## Source constraints

Majidifar et al. §2.1.1 reared founding queens in water/cotton tubes without food and, after worker emergence, transferred the colony to an arena with mixed food. The authors manipulated workers and measured behavior at 21 °C and 80% RH. Their diets and observation windows do not measure callow doses or queen uptake rates for the 25 °C contexts. Grade B for this use. [Majidifar et al. 2024](https://doi.org/10.1111/1365-2435.14536)

Detrain and Prieur, pp. 75–77 and Table 1, separate antennal contact from water and sugar drinking in established-colony scouts. Retain zero drinking after contact and the mismatch in age, arrangement and hunger. Grade B for adult ingestion after resource contact. [Detrain and Prieur 2014](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jinsphys.2014.03.010)

The tagged trophallaxis study followed one 60-minute post-supply window per subcolony after a four-day fast. The five subcolonies were queenless and broodless with at least 50 workers drawn from established colonies. Direction was assessed from behavior and actual dose was not measured. It supports the existence of spatially localized sharing, with rates and a founder-specific recipient model unmeasured. Grade B for the behavioral pathway, C for extrapolation to a founder's provisioning. [A spatiotemporal analysis of food dissemination, Methods and Figs. 2–7](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-019-52072-2)
