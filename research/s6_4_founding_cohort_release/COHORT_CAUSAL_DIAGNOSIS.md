# First larval cohort causal diagnosis

The primary panel completes 384 paired physics-25 worlds. First-ration preference is the direct cause of the measured first-cohort regression under these declared simulator inputs. It is applied after the existing care decision and overrides an ordinary candidate on selected wakes. Any positive ration can update the single R1 recent-meal target. Broader first provision therefore redistributes later repeated care. The realized cohorts receive more first doses and fewer complete protein allocations. The timing of each legal action, crop payment and uptake can be inspected independently. The first-census eclosion difference is −8.33 percentage points in Borisovka (simulation seed-cluster 95% interval −20.83 to +8.33) and −12.50 points in St. Petersburg (−20.83 to −4.17). Water deaths increase by 45.83 points in each population. All first-census larvae have at least one recorded maternal opportunity after hatching. Individuals without a ration lacked a recorded feeding selection in these runs. Every selected first-census recipient subsequently started and received a ration. Every first-census recipient with positive paid protein or water had a positive uptake for that component. These observations do not reveal the private draw, eligibility of each unchosen candidate or whether a ration would have been possible at every opportunity. Daily state samples are strictly earlier than a reported event. [Worlds, paired effects and individual traces](../../experiments/s6_4_founding_cohort_release/primary)

For Borisovka seed 1, shaft and blocked local water, the first hatched larva (ID 5) receives 65 µg of protein and 100 µg of water in five paid rations without first-ration preference. The same input and larval identity receive 48 µg of protein and 60 µg of water in three rations when preference is enabled. Each quantity is assimilated. The former animal pupates and ecloses on day 45.4. The latter dehydrates on day 35.97. The missing 17 µg of protein separates the second history from the declared tissue threshold. The actual terminal cause is dehydration. Unfed peers can die earlier of energy depletion. Do not assign a hidden physiological outcome to a larva that died before its next opportunity. [Frozen raw and event witnesses](../../experiments/s6_4_founding_cohort_release/primary)

In that matched first-preference world, the day-35 sample preceding larva 5's death records 2,505 µg of maternal body water, no maternal crop water, 2,531 µg of lipid, 178 µg of mobile protein and 1.232 J of usable energy. These sampled reserves establish that a live mother still held material before the loss. They leave exact eligibility, contact and expendable stock at the terminal instant unresolved. Across the paid-activity worlds, median maternal paid handling changes from 172 to 176 s in Borisovka and from 178 to 188 s in St. Petersburg. The configured charge records 172,000 → 176,000 and 178,000 → 188,000 nJ. Median remaining mobile protein at day 55 changes from 318 to 0 µg and 284 to 0 µg, respectively. Those differences accompany redistribution and higher depletion. They do not by themselves isolate a resource-mediated causal path. The paid-activity and recruitment switches each have exactly zero first-census effect under this factorial input. The finite reachable source delivers 1,200 µg to the crop in each of 192 accessible worlds and zero in the equal-stock isolated arms. Total paid maternal water to all larvae increases from 120,827 to 130,124 µg across the matching 192-world halves. That manipulation has zero first-cohort eclosion or death-cause effect. Adult body water availability, the realized care schedule and child intake are distinct. The measurements support a distribution and repeat-care limitation in this panel. They do not establish that water access is generally irrelevant. The separate 96-world cross confirms that H2 and R1 interact. Without R1 no first-census larvae eclose in either H2 arm (0/48 per cell and population). With R1 on, H2 off gives 8/48 and 10/48 eclosions in Borisovka and St. Petersburg, and H2 on gives 2/48 and 0/48. This contrast keeps the old first-meal preference on. It does not support changing H2 in the selected protected profile. Larval reserve mobilization remains enabled in both branches. Physics 26 suppresses only the existing hatch cue in the exploratory comparison. The protected candidate uses physics 25. B01 observed care in laboratory founding queens supplied via wet cotton. B03 measured brood output under manipulated foundress diets. Both leave hatch-to-dose selection, component intake and individual mortality unidentified in these simulated pockets. B06 uses dark 40 mm chambers, regularly moistened cotton and no added food. The 1,200 µg contact source has a finite initial stock and leaves cotton water availability, humidity and substrate outside the model. First-dose and repeat-feeding probabilities remain engineering fixtures.
