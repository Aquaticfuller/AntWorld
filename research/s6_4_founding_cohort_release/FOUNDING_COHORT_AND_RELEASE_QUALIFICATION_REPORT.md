Recalculation retains both frozen daily count readers for 444 prior worlds and 1,686 first-census larvae. In balanced corrected worlds the median of successful individual stage medians (egg, larva, pupa, whole) is 16.7, 10.2, 14.5 and 41.4 days for Borisovka and 15.7, 10.6, 13.6 and 39.9 for St. Petersburg. Living colony counts also reflect early deaths and which later individuals are present together. The B06 source uses replenished wet cotton, near-daily scans and no added food.
