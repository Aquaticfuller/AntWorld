## Local clearance under physics 95

A resting worker near a thirsty founder can consider a short, legal
repositioning when her known wick has no current route. The bounded
look-ahead keeps the other bodies fixed and tests whether this worker's
new position and heading can open a route. On the actual movement
boundary the nearby founder wakes and checks the new committed
positions. The look-ahead grants access only to a movement proposal.
Drinking still requires her own paid motion and actual mouth contact
with the funded source. A viable oral route remains eligible and uses
the existing contact and transfer transaction.

Offer selection now requires a complete plan for a local founder
encounter. An isolated step found by a different search previously
occupied the decision slot, even when the executor would reject it.
Releasing that slot lets the actor consider the next local opportunity,
including a real feeder trip or an attainable wick. The geometry
search bounds and the yield decision are engineering fixtures.

The day-63 continuation reaches a paid wick sip of 25 µg, 17.131
simulated seconds after the cut. Its founder first moves through five
committed legs. Worker 9 next offers 74 µg sugar and 26 µg water in one
mouth transaction. The founder receives 752 µg of oral sugar and
199 µg of oral water through the old deadline and absorbs 604 µg of sugar
and 199 µg of water. Starting at day 61.75 gives 1,036 µg of oral sugar,
202 µg of oral water, and survival through the same deadline. The older
seed-2 sugar replay retains worker 8's 358 µg of paid oral sugar and
the founder's 369 µg of sugar uptake.

## Surface workers with residual social crop

The two-year physics-99 seed-17 control has a last sugar receipt on
day 69.45 and energy death at 7,297,877,685 ms. At the start of its last
six hours workers 10 and 14 have 441 and 725 µg of sugar in their crops.
Both carry zero external cargo and neither obtained its sugar in the
current trip. Three workers commit 720 rests each at fixed surface
positions in that window. Worker 9 makes 216 movements, repeatedly
reaching the same point below the surface before reversing. The mixed
feeder still holds 124,851 µg sugar at the last living cut.

A local probe captures the rejected legs. Workers 10 and 14 each aim
for the feeder cell occupied by worker 8. Worker 8 aims toward the
return berth occupied by worker 14. The ordinary outward legs fail
the body traffic check. The old return condition also requires a
current-trip liquid load and a body at the exact connector entrance.
Both requirements matter for the difference between workers 10 and 14.

Physics 100 permits a worker to return with a currently payable crop
when a local food approach is physically blocked. The worker must
already know the sugar source and its own entrance. It can use the
connector directly, a locally accessible berth, or a reachable home
step from its own one-leg surface trace. The actual movement still
uses the same body clearance, support, travel clock, and connector
transaction. The crop still belongs to the worker until a later
actual oral transaction debits it. This choice is an engineering
fixture with no change to the metabolic budget.

In the first prototype only the local-range return option was enabled.
Worker 14 paid 430 µg of sugar, while the other visible donor stayed
outside. The founder survived day 86 and was dead by day 90. With the
private one-leg trace option worker 10 reached its own entrance and
the modeled workers resumed real collection and underground travel.
Worker 14 paid 376 µg of sugar in nine mouth transactions and the
founder absorbed 186 µg before the original deadline. Other donors
paid a further 55 µg of sugar in the same six-hour window. Founder
body energy at the deadline was 1,503,010,638 nJ. In the longer cut,
the founder survived day 90 and died before day 110. This extension
addresses a verified lock and retains the remaining supply failure.

## Capacity-driven trips with an intact sugar crop

The extended physics-100 seed-17 continuation receives 585 µg sugar
in days 90–92. In the next interval it receives just 32 µg and dies at
8,085,593,130 ms. At day 92 worker 10 has 389 µg of crop sugar. The same
worker still has 338 µg at the last living millisecond. Three surface
workers stay at their positions and the mixed feeder still has
137,239 µg sugar at the day-94 observation. Worker 10 commits
5,930 movement legs, including 5,640 connector moves. They cluster at
29 positions. There is no new positive sugar collection in that cut.

A prototype that lets entrance workers yield to a returning liquid
carrier has identical results in this replay. It is an unsuccessful
candidate for these positions and cues. The separate refill test
changes one condition in underground job selection. Physics 101 needs
both crop room and meaningful demand before starting the remembered
refill. Demand is an empty sugar crop or the existing projected
sugar/water deficit after accounting for body and crop stores. The
other collection, donation, and terminal physiology checks remain the
existing funded transactions.

The worker makes 49 committed moves in the matched window under this
policy and pays 375 µg sugar and 78 µg water into the founder's crop.
The founder retains 1,809,566,270 nJ at the old deadline. Replaying
past that deadline still ends with founder death by day 96. This
experiment isolates the cost of restarting a supply trip solely on
available crop space. A complete supply chain also requires the next
collector to acquire resources and reach an eligible recipient.
