## From a stimulus to a committed action

An individual owns its outstanding wake, physiology boundary and work through handles in the existing world event queue. The queue orders execution by simulation time, phase and sequence. An internal need may forecast a future boundary within the current upkeep interval. A committed cut can update that forecast when activity or inventory changes. An available local worker may then sense the recipient and review an offer. This follows the scheduling model in [gem5 event-driven programming](https://www.gem5.org/documentation/learning_gem5/part2/events/).

| Input | Scheduling source | Conditions at execution |
|---|---|---|
| Hunger | First rising edge of the existing upkeep and crop deficit | The adult can consider a local source or a payable offer. |
| Thirst | First rising edge of the existing water shortfall | The adult can seek a real drink or interact with a nearby donor. |
| Brood provision | Hatch with outstanding demand or a larva crossing its upkeep threshold | A local eligible carer must detect the larva and meet the existing care, space and resource requirements. |
| Mouth contact | Completion of an actual movement with a compatible local offer and reachable recipient | Both adults must still be at legal contact and have capacity and payable stock when handling ends. |
| Positive oral receipt | Commitment of a positive adult transfer | An eligible idle donor can repeat the same handling action while the current guard remains satisfied. |

The one-millisecond queue separation orders a reaction after the triggering transaction. Walking, turning, manipulation and nutrient uptake retain their own physical clocks. Generic exploration reviews still exist. Resting and nursing workers in the new experiments no longer start a recurring nutrition wake without a local intake opportunity. The model retains existing climate and trail mechanisms. Dynamic light, vibration and fatigue have no new event input in this work.

The near-field constraint is an engineering approximation. Black garden ant feeding studies document heterogeneous local exchange in groups of workers. Those groups lacked queens and brood, which limits the use of their measurements for founding physiology. Video-based activity recordings also show that brood and nestmates change the amount and spatial distribution of motion. They support examining local work and its context, with the duration and type of inactivity measured separately from successful or missed feeding. [Planckaert et al., 2019](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6821819/) · [Oh and Cremer, 2026](https://doi.org/10.1111/2041-210x.70277)

## Reproduction

Build the native observer in Release. Run the following from the repository root.

```powershell
$core = 'build/m0-live/antworld_m0_live.exe'
py -3 -B -m tools.audit_founding_activity --core $core --verify-final-leg
py -3 -B -m tools.audit_founding_activity --core $core --stimulus --stimulus-version 90
py -3 -B -m tools.audit_founding_events --core $core --physics 90 --verify
py -3 -B -m tools.audit_founding_activity --core $core --verify-archive --stimulus-version 90
py -3 -B -m tools.measure_first_meal --core $core --model continuous-meal --seed 2 --supply nearby --days 730 --feeding-continuity --trace-flux --include-water
```

Use seeds 1, 2, 3, 17 and 29 for the nearby feeder and seed 2 for absent supply. The route-only comparator uses `--model joint-meal-route`. The historical comparator is the physics-80 subset of `SUSTAINED_SUPPLY_TRIALS.jsonl.gz`. For the floor-clearance experiment, build commit `e1a5fdeb` and run the matched-cut and archive checks with `--stimulus-version 91`. The registered seed-2 cutoff gate failed for that experiment. The current delivery keeps physics 90 available as an explicit experimental treatment.
