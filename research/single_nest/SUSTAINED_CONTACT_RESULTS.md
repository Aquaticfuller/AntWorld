# Founder feeding across the final millimetres

The original seed-2 founder died at 7,867,222,430 ms (day 91.06). Replaying from birth reproduced the published frame hash one millisecond before death and canonical death event 469357. In the last six hours, workers 8 and 9 owned payable sugar and completed approach steps toward her. Their mouth gaps at the relevant reviews were 4.4851 and 3.2911 mm. Both had twelve steps left and another legal move. The continuation reselected individual 24. Neither worker paid the founder before her energy was depleted. The 0.65 mm contact requirement and the existing crop, uptake and mortality transactions were retained throughout.

## Remaining failure in the new world

The failure differs by seed. Founders 17 and 29 received positive sugar transfers within roughly one and two hours of death, respectively. Receipt of a dose does not establish that enough was absorbed in time to cover expenditure. The panel measures transfer to the crop and verifies individual examples of uptake; it does not attribute each worker or founder death to one missed visit.

In physics 67 seed 2, the last living founder had 10 nJ of body energy, 1,770 µg of body water, zero crop sugar and 85 µg of crop protein. Worker 8 held 483 µg of sugar. Their mouth gap at that instant was 1.34 mm. In six hours this worker completed 22 approach segments aimed at the founder. Several returned to the same poses. None was a positive transfer. During five of the founder's six ordinary reviews both parties were visible, with a genuine sugar deficit and a payable gift, but the planner found no next approach by either stationary partner at those instants. A feasible step at the last living millisecond does not imply an earlier contact.

One millisecond before the last ordinary review, a separate read-only geometry scan tested eight worker headings on the 62.5 µm lattice over the same-height mouth contact region. No standing pose met the existing mutual mouth, body and substrate constraints with the queen fixed. Nine candidate legal queen steps opened room for at least one hypothetical worker pose. These calculations do not move either ant or establish a path between the poses. A longer donor path search produced the identical failed frame. A receiver-side step intended to open room increased completed approaches from 22 to 58 and produced zero payments. The founder still died, 21 ms earlier. Both diagnostic implementations failed their prospective endpoints and are excluded from the installed models.

This separates missing local target persistence, an entrance traffic jam, premature completion of the return journey, and a remaining two-body movement problem. A future joint encounter must demonstrate a reachable sequence of **independently executed**, collision-checked steps for both participants and payment before their reserves expire. Testing against all four terminal cuts plus independent from-birth worlds is necessary to avoid choosing a mechanism on its own rescued cut.
