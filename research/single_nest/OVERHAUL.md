Its 250 µm terrain quantum and 62.5 µm laying and approach coordinates serve different purposes. Motion segments use continuous positions. The soil cap, wick, depot, room dimensions, approach distances, material coefficients and timing are engineering fixtures. The finite wick and external feeders are a protected environment. ## Versioned state and rendering

The schema 32 and Physics 39 discriminators select the new rules. The continuation format retains absolute event and actor identities, state, existing care ownership and the committed heading. Movement evidence encodes the heading in bits 16–23 of its low word for this version. The low 16 bits contain turn milliseconds and the upper word contains total milliseconds. Original readers of earlier physics retain their original movement evidence. Independent full replay checks anatomical birth, clear mouth contact, the movement envelope, carried brood, and the original nutrition and soil partitions.

`BODY_STATES` is a local read-only native observation. Parts are expressed using offsets and half axes in metres. Caste, position, body orientation, current care and held identity belong to the same cut. The GPU reuses material faces per observed revision and instanced ellipsoids per frame. Labels and selection always reference the native cut. The observer clips the roof and can expose the interior through transparent overburden. Existing orthogonal section views remain available.

The default save is a compact continuation with a recent canonical suffix. Explicit archive saves retain full SQLite history if the world still has that prefix. The source formats and old experimental archives retain their meaning.

## Assay definition

Compare both physics versions at 25 °C, 80% RH, 1,955 s half-life, the same initial reserve and external supply schedules, and seeds 1–3. The pocket, depot location and spatial rules are intentional treatment differences. Matched absence arms retain the finite founder wick and omit external feeders. The headless assay captures days 0, 10, 30, 60, 90, 120 and 150. It scans up to day 60 and at most 131,072 early canonical facts per world. A missing event in this bounded prefix is an unobserved event after the scan cut. Death and final stock are evaluated at state cuts. An individual may hatch, be fed, and still die from an unmet energy or water budget.

Qualification is evaluated in sequence from a closed initial pocket through paid birth, head-contact care, same-identity pickup/transport/placement, natural emergence, actual up/down movement, soil edit and deposition, and actual delivery. An isolated result is identified by seed, model and supply. A full ecological claim requires the sequence in normal autonomous worlds across seeds and sustained stocks and survival. Unit geometry probes do not contribute autonomous ecology outcomes. The older failure records remain part of the comparison.
