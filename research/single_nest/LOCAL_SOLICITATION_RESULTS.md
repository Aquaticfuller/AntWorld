# Why the founder missed a funded meal

The final model comparison appears under [Physics 73 from founding](#physics-73-from-founding). The short encounter and refilling results below establish which operations actually completed in each model.

## The last six hours

Two archived seed-2 deaths answer different questions. In physics 63, workers 8 and 9 ended approach segments with mouth gaps of 4.4851 and 3.2911 mm. The continuation reselected individual 24. On a fresh replay, the founder died on day 91.06 (event 469357) with no sugar payments in the window. On that same input physics 68 and 69 each paid 231 µg in 16 oral transactions. Physics 70 paid 430 µg in six transactions. All three crossed the old death time alive. Each test replayed the donor debit and recipient credit.

The physics 67 seed-2 founder died of energy depletion at 7,088,150,452 ms (day 82.04). Her body still held water. The daily samples and the terminal cut identify two separate supply interruptions.

* From days 47 to 66 there was no committed collection by worker 8. For days 49 through 57 the worker ended each day with 13 µg of sugar in its crop and its foraging program disabled. A new departure required a recognized berth, available route and a task review. Having made a collection previously did not, by itself, qualify an experienced non-excavator for a refill.
* On day 80 the depot held 6,879 µg of protein and zero sugar. Worker 8 held 505 µg of sugar. In the terminal six hours there were six founder reviews and 22 completed approaches, but zero oral sugar payments. At the last sampled review the mouths were still about 0.915 mm apart. Both bodies had to make room to reach the existing 0.65 mm contact envelope. The donor still carried 483 µg at the terminal cut. A rule that only increases the priority of feeding cannot make a blocked stance usable.

Physics 68 tests a bounded joint search over local body poses. A proposed encounter may move the donor or recipient. Only one participant starts a real, paid movement at a time. A visible partner can request another review after that movement. The existing scheduler, body sweeps, ownership checks and oral transaction determine what actually commits. An additional scheduled review has a 1 ms dispatch delay. This is a scheduling convention. The movement and manipulation still cost their ordinary duration. The beam width (12), depth (5) and candidate step lengths are engineering assumptions. Other ants may invalidate the predicted later legs.

Physics 69 also lets an unloaded worker with a personally sampled carbohydrate source check for a legal departure on an ordinary review when its crop holds less sugar than one transfer limit. This removes dependence on having excavated the original departure site. The worker must still be near the known origin, see it, have a completed surface trace, and pass real route clearance before leaving. A funded offer already at mouth contact and immediate drinking retain priority.

The first refill implementation allowed the worker to register its current feeding position as a new route origin. Compact restore correctly rejected that origin in seeds 3 and 29. The three remaining supplied trials were stopped. The corrected implementation walks back to its remembered berth (or departs from an already valid berth). The decoder and its historical origin checks remain intact.

Physics 70 is a headless screening treatment. The ordinary adult request can be suppressed until an energy level below 0.5 J when water and protein are adequate. The founder can spend 0.5 J in about five hours. This candidate uses her existing founding-meal demand for the sugar and water she can accept on local contact, including an offer of water alone. An adult donor still keeps its maintenance reserve and cannot pay more than the crop-sharing gradient allows. In the same terminal cut it delivered the 238 µg of sugar in five payments. The founder already had adequate body water and received zero water in these payments. This test alone does not establish rescue from dehydration.

On the day-82 six-hour input, physics 68 and 69 each produced 17 transfers by worker 8. The founder received and absorbed 238 µg of sugar and received 218 µg of water. The measured mouth separation was 0.625 mm. At the original death time her body held 1,982,686,823 nJ and 1,990 µg of water. The replay independently checked the two crop deltas for every component, the food and soil ledgers, and the continuation with a pending meal saved and reloaded. The fork changes three declared configuration/version identifiers plus checksum in a disposable copy of the old research snapshot. Player saves retain their original semantics.

The fresh two-year baseline identified a throughput problem as well. Before the founder died, worker 8 collected 4,303 µg of sugar. The founder received and absorbed 3,009 µg. No other worker collected sugar during her lifetime. A further 20,369 µg was collected by workers 23 and 24 only after her death. At the baseline rate of 2.34 J/day and yield of 0.008 J/µg, a queen living on sugar alone would require 292.5 µg/day even before work and reproduction. The actual 4,303 µg collected in the 36.6 days from first collection to death represents about 118 µg/day for the whole colony. Endogenous reserves make this a diagnostic balance, not a new feeding threshold.

## Empty returns in the funded environment

At the end of the physics 70 seed-17 screen, 13 half-hour observations showed worker 11 back at its berth with the same residual 13 µg of carbohydrate. The committed history between those cuts contains 24 departures, 480 outbound search decisions, 480 home-trace decisions, 24 local-food decisions and 960 completed movements. There were zero positive collections by that worker. It reached a local feeder cue and turned around before collecting. An independent diagnostic of its decisions produced the identical predeath frame hash. The worker's private memory of a previous collection plus a payable amount of old crop sugar satisfied the return rule on a new outing.

Physics 71 records whether a forager actually ingested carbohydrate on the current excursion. Successful liquid intake sets the bit. Home arrival, cancellation and death clear it. Both the surface home-trace rule and the occupied-feeder retreat test it. Normal navigation, return of external cargo, self-maintenance and food payments remain active. Schema 64 uses the high bit of the serialized forager mode byte starting with physics 71. This adds no bytes per forager. The full-history validator reconstructs the bit from committed events. Compact restores preserve it directly.

Starting at 6,209,509,931 ms, the corrected worker made one departure and collected and ingested 395 µg of sugar. The founder received 408 µg of sugar from nestmates during the window and survived its end. None of those 408 µg was paid directly by worker 11. The old founder received 13 µg and died with 17 nJ one millisecond before event 750405. Worker 8 was in mouth contact with only 1 µg of payable sugar and feeder 4 still held 113,200 µg. Source acquisition and final delivery have separate witnesses. A secondary replay verified the loaded worker's compact continuation, and a fresh seed-2 world passed a full SQLite save/load including the newly acquired crop at event cursor 12784.

## Protected worlds through two years

Physics 71 failed the prespecified colony-level endpoint. In the five supplied worlds the founders died between days 58.95 and 81.89. Each world retained one worker at day 730. None had a worker eclose during year two. Four founders died of dehydration and one of energy depletion. The absent-food control received no sugar. Keeping the same starting inputs across versions made it possible to separate the immediate effect on a failing encounter from the consequences of running the new behavior from founding.

Seed 2 illustrates the difference. Physics 67 died on day 82.04, physics 68 on day 87.54 and physics 71 on day 62.53. The latest queen actually received 1,797 µg of sugar over nine days. Her last positive sugar payment preceded her death by 81.69 hours. One millisecond before death she had 1.163 J of body energy, 1 µg of body water and zero crop water. The finite wick held 1,499,950 µg of water. The two nearby workers had 470 and 544 µg of crop water. Their mouths were 2.165 and 4.100 mm from hers. All thirteen half-hour observations placed the wick in her sensory neighborhood, outside immediate oral reach. Nine individually legal micro-steps at the terminal cut do not certify a complete path to a drink.

Seed 3 died of energy depletion eleven minutes after receiving sugar. All 2,297 µg received over her lifetime were absorbed (2,033 µg at ordinary pulses and 264 µg at energy boundaries). The longest interval between positive sugar meals exceeded nine days. Receipt of a final small meal can coexist with an energy deficit accumulated over days. Engineering success on the restored encounters does not make this model eligible for default play or sustained-colony acceptance.

## Why the founder missed water

A successful approach event records that a movement finished. The terminal six-hour physics 71 trace contains 325 and 312 completed approaches by workers 8 and 9. Worker 8 used only three arrival positions and reversed to its previous arrival 302 times. Worker 9 used two and reversed 310 times. The queen had six reviews and both donors had at least 24. There was zero oral water transfer, source debit for a founder drink, or founder water uptake. At the last cut the checkpoint carried 2 µg as of the last physiological update. The read-only projection at that exact time gave 1 µg, followed by the committed dehydration event one millisecond later.

Two separate perturbations failed. Preferring a funded water donor over a walk to the wick changed no events or final bytes. On each founder review there was no usable wick step to outrank. She requested worker 8. Including other visible adult bodies in every look-ahead reduced worker 8's immediate reversals to zero and worker 9's to 283, but neither delivered water. This rules out inadequate review frequency as a complete explanation for this cut. The six-hour time series and both failed diagnostic patches are available alongside the successful trial.

Physics 72 searches local founder poses when approaching a visible feeding patch or wick. A route must end at a place the mouth can actually reach. One physical movement commits before the next observation. On the identical cut the founder completed five approach legs to the patch. She drew 25 µg from the finite wick, received 179 µg of water from workers and absorbed 202 µg altogether. She retained 203 µg of body water at the former death boundary. The stock debit included all draws from the wick. Restoring a compact snapshot one millisecond before the first drink gave the same subsequent state. These data establish physical access and receipt in this encounter.

## Matched two-year decision through physics 72

The five supplied physics-72 founders died between days 57.87 and 86.71. Three exhausted their body energy and two dehydrated. Four worlds had one living worker at day 730 and one had none. No worker eclosed in year two. Zero of five worlds met the registered three-world sustained-growth threshold. The absent-food world had zero founder sugar receipts and no workers at day 730. Physics 72 is available as a selectable experiment.

In seed 2, workers 8 and 9 collected 5,811 µg of sugar before founder death. They paid her 2,356 and 1,706 µg respectively in 350 oral exchanges. The founder absorbed all 4,062 µg across ordinary and energy-boundary uptake. There were payments on 23 days. The longest gap between positive payments was 209.72 hours. Worker 9 made five positive sugar transfers in the final six hours: 13, 6, 3, 2 and 1 µg. The last 1 µg was paid at event 744052, absorbed at event 744219, and bought only a few minutes at the current rate of expenditure. Death followed 10.76 minutes after payment. Ten other transfers in that window contained water alone. Worker 8 completed no sugar delivery in that window. Its foraging decision count was 1,090 throughout the 13 half-hour samples and its foraging route was disabled at each cut. The measured result supports a targeted future comparison of committing to a refill versus continuing the same nearly empty sharing cycle after a perceived shortfall. That comparison must count newly collected sugar through return and subsequent founder intake. One millisecond before death the founder had 16 nJ of body energy, 192 µg of body water and zero crop sugar. The nest stock had zero sugar. Feeder 4 still held 129,189 µg. Worker 9 was in oral reach at 0.625 mm with 1 µg of sugar and zero payable sugar under the current rule. Worker 8 had 37 µg with 18 payable and its mouth was 4.074 mm away. Physical access and delivery throughput continue to limit these worlds after the demonstrated encounter fixes.

The seed-1 dehydration had a different resource distribution. All three worker crops were dry at the death cut, their bodies held 1,381 to 1,758 µg of water, and the wick held 1,499,975 µg. A separate offline perturbation let a worker respond to a local nestmate's water deficit by drawing from the finite wick. Worker 9 drew 16 µg in two transactions. No worker delivered water to the founder. She drew 125 µg herself and absorbed 75 µg. This failed the registered worker-relay endpoint and was excluded from the playable model. The six-hour results cannot establish rescue over a lifetime.

## Why the experienced collectors could not leave

The two physics-72 workers at the founder's final death in seed 2 had already collected from source 4 and were inside the valid departure region. Both failed the read-only route query in all thirteen half-hour samples. At both endpoints native tracing identified the exact rejection. The initial sweep from the worker's continuous body position to the nearest grid point was blocked by traffic. The old search returned immediately. A clear alternative at the shaft entrance never reached its decision logic. Worker 8 stood at (1.6875, -4, 1.3125) mm, only 0.476 mm from its remembered berth, and worker 9 stood 0.690 mm from its berth. Checking hunger more often would run the same failed route test at those poses.

Physics 73 allows an experienced worker to inspect the eight immediate horizontal neighbors of that grid point if the original connector search fails. A candidate must remain within the body's local sensing range and within one terrain quantum of the true starting point. The first movement and every following leg must clear terrain and traffic and retain their time and resource costs. Only the local connection changes. The worker must still navigate outside, pay and collect at a real source, return, reach the founder's mouth and transfer from its own crop.

The six-hour paired replay began at 7,470,209,616 ms. Under physics 72 there were no new collections, five positive sugar payments totaling 25 µg, and founder death at event 744221. Physics 73 completed six collections totaling 394 µg. Workers 8 and 9 ingested the full collection. The founder received 152 µg and absorbed 115 µg during the window. She remained alive at the original death boundary. Each of six mid-trip compact save/load continuations reproduced the uninterrupted end frame and the ledgers. The absence of a death at this single cut gives no estimate of lifespan.

The first implementation allowed neighboring attachment candidates farther than the existing checkpoint decoder permits. Six successful terminal reloads did not cover that geometry. Code inspection caught the mismatch. The five preliminary supplied runs were interrupted and excluded, the absent-food run was repeated, and all six final worlds used a first-leg bound matching the persistence contract. The bounds fix reproduced the six-hour event quantities.

## Archive regression found during qualification

A complete SQLite save at the first observed alternate shaft attachment initially failed. The storage writer assigned the older AWFACT09 envelope to physics 71 through 73. That envelope cannot represent a resumed foraging origin. After selecting AWFACT10 for those exact schema/version pairs, the historical reader still rejected a real founder transfer using the old adult demand formula. The reader now verifies the same founder request against the historical recipient, donor offer, crop capacity and balances. The corrected reader accepts genuine early AWFACT09 archives for the three affected historical version pairs. The world/version identity, digest chain, recorded transfer validation and replay remain mandatory.

Physics 72's original day-one SQLite file is retained compressed. The native reader and independent OP0 importer reproduced its 61 records. The full physics-73 case captures worker 8 departing at event 15893 and taking the alternate first leg at event 15900. The SQLite archive was 2,297,856 bytes and contained 15,902 records. Independent ingestion, byte-identical restore, two further minutes of continuation and ledger verification passed. The full temporary database was discarded after measurement. ## Evidence and limits

Holman et al. identified a queen cuticular hydrocarbon in *L. niger*. Their experiments measured worker ovarian responses and aggression toward treated targets. A founder's current sugar deficit and the order of feeding queen versus larvae were outside those endpoints. The simulator's local meal request remains an explicit modeling assumption. [Primary chemical-cue experiment](https://doi.org/10.1098/rspb.2010.0984)

Casillas-Pérez et al. monitored *L. niger* colonies through their first year. The authors reported one sham-control queen dying before winter in Figure 4d. Live samples used for other measurements were censored. The figure and public catalog were checked here. The Dryad download returned an access page and its API required a token, so a new censor-adjusted estimate was not calculated. The association between winter survival and worker aggregation around the queen was observational. The study included winter and differed from our continuous 25 °C simulation. Treat the repeated simulated early deaths as a protected-world failure. [Original paper](https://doi.org/10.1111/ele.13907) · [Figure 4d data catalog](https://doi.org/10.5061/dryad.7pvmcvdtj)

Planckaert et al. measured oral food exchange among *L. niger* workers after sugar deprivation. Their groups lacked a queen and brood. The observations support local exchange while leaving founder-specific solicitation distances, task priority and time constants open. Szabó et al. measured 49-day founder output under feeding treatments. The endpoint combines pupae and workers. Neither paper provides a measured rule compelling every worker to feed the queen before brood, nor a two-year simulation target. [Worker food sharing](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-019-52019-6) · [Founder feeding study](https://academic.oup.com/biolinnean/article/142/4/397/7342048)
