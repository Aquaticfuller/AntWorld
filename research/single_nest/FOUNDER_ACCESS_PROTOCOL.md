# Founder access and portable nest movement

Baseline: commit `75e34136a694bf958f58bea98ebc14cdd6e5051f`, Physics 39. The three finite worlds delivered food but registered zero founder crop meals. This protocol keeps those worlds and the previous stock, water, mortality, reproduction and absorption rules as the controls.

## Paired experiment

Start one queen, zero workers, a sealed pocket, 25 °C, 80% RH and a 1,955 s trail half-life. Run software seeds 1, 2 and 3 with the finite feeder and seed 2 with the external feeder absent. Compare old and opt-in physics at days 0, 4, 5, 12, 30, 60, 90, 120 and 150. Keep the same world ID for both members of a pair. Report all four cases. A clock ending with living individuals is administrative censoring.

The primary access endpoint is an ordered first positive worker delivery, founder meal debited from the nest, and a later positive founder carbohydrate uptake. Report the actual quantities, times, identities and stock remaining. Count zero meals and death even if delivery succeeded. Require this chain in at least two of the three finite seeds and zero depot meals in the absent arm before calling access demonstrated. The independent ecological endpoint is a living queen and at least one living worker at day 150 in at least two finite seeds. Failure of either endpoint remains reportable. No parameter fitting or seed substitution.

Test food in reach and beyond reach, direction and obstruction, failed access and occupied stances. A local check may use the animal's own shortfall and sensory evidence or experience of a previously visited site. Consumption requires an unobstructed mouth contact, available stock and crop room. Track forager delivery separately from worker and queen consumption. Preserve physical movement, claim resolution, mortality ordering and the full partition ledgers.
