# A first meal at the worker's own exit and paid feeding of the founder

Physics 63 is an opt-in experimental treatment. The spatial defect reproduced in Physics 62 and the first collection by the two late diggers have independent checks. Persistent colony growth has a separate, prospectively defined endpoint.

## What changed in the Core

An unloaded worker can depart from its own excavated site after it has dug, carried and deposited a soil parcel and can reach the surface. This site supplies a private route origin. A worker samples actual liquid at an actual source. A private berth has no communal food balance. The worker can retrace its surface and connector route after drinking, carry liquid internally, revisit a familiar site, or seek a nestmate by ordinary local contact. A crop carrier can receive the existing traffic yield inside a passage. An occupied passage, inaccessible source and interrupted trip remain possible.

For an adult feeding approach, the motor can make a short sideways or backward stance correction while retaining a physically legal heading. Both mouths still need a line of sight and mutual contact within 0.65 mm. Selection, completed movement, the timed payment and later uptake remain distinct events. The local step lengths and choices are engineering hypotheses. They have no independent species calibration.

The native application and observer recognize Physics 63 and its checkpoint/schema pair 56/63. The SQLite reader now dispatches canonical event blocks for this pair. Physics 62 and earlier saves retain their formats and model behavior.

## Failure investigations

Seed 17 separates an absent local donor from a failed handoff between two nearby ants. Immediately before the founder's energy death, worker 9 was visible and held zero sugar. Digger 11, which held 26 µg, was outside her sensory field at a mouth separation of 9.24 mm. Its six-hour trace contained fifteen movements and ended at the same location; its lifetime collection count remained one. The colony pantry was empty. A donor in the world is insufficient to establish food available at the founder's mouth.

We separately challenged whether repeated switching between a return to the natal chamber and a visit to a known exit explained this failure. A diagnostic build reserved more ordinary reviews for a repeat departure below a transfer-sized load, retaining local feeding priority. On the same seed at day 75 it produced only one collection by digger 11 and zero sugar payments to the founder. Her energy death occurred at exactly the original time. The change failed its two preregistered endpoints and is excluded from the installed model. Its exact source change is recorded in WORKED_EXIT_REFILL_CHALLENGE.patch.

In seed 2 the final return correction got solvent donors inside. Immediately before fact 469357, worker 8 had 464 µg of sugar at a mouth gap of 4.49 mm and worker 9 had 447 µg at 3.29 mm. Both had line of sight, payable sugar and at least one legal next step on both sides. Neither was in mouth contact. The founder had 11 nJ of body energy, 2,716 µg of body water, no crop sugar and 85 µg of crop protein. The communal stock had no sugar. On day 90 to 91 the founder completed four approaches toward worker 9, and worker 9 completed four toward the founder. One valid next step does not establish a complete payable encounter. At day 150 ten workers were alive in this world after the founder's death. All were dead by day 730.

The last-millisecond cuts differ across seeds. In seed 1 the worker in actual mouth contact could offer zero sugar and two solvent workers were outside perception. In seed 3 three donors were visible with 7, 5 and 1 µg payable, respectively. No pair was in mouth contact. These receipts distinguish geographic access, voluntary transfer and survival. The observations do not identify natural mortality rates.

### Three independent tests of the last few millimetres

In seed 2's last six hours no sugar passed to the founder. At 192 and 135 seconds before death, workers 9 and 8 each completed an approach step toward her. The last-millisecond mouth gaps were 3.29 and 4.49 mm, respectively. Worker 24, which had no sugar at the final cut, had also moved toward her. The receipt contains the last 24 approach observations and every positive founder sugar payment in this six-hour window.

We tested three changes independently from the same installed starting model and supply. Each had to pay the founder sugar after day 91.055815 and keep her alive at day 100. A different event ID at the same death time did not meet the endpoint.

None of the three diagnostic binaries is used for normal play. Their diffs, executable hashes, initial and final state hashes, payments and deaths are retained with this report. The tests rule out each change alone as sufficient in this world. A viable future encounter model would need to demonstrate local cooperation across the full approach and payment under passage traffic, and return/repeat collections before reserves expire. A single visible step, increased candidate counts or higher lifetime food intake cannot establish this. The evidence does not identify a validated rule for keeping all five founders provisioned.

## Reproducing the tests

Run from the repository root with the Windows live binary built as described in README.md. Each paired run begins with one actual founder. World IDs distinguish models while terrain, seed, supply, reserves and physiology are checked independently. The observer streams canonical facts, checks the food and soil ledgers, and discards audited in-memory history pages. Full-history exports are unnecessary.

```powershell
py -3 -B -m tools.measure_first_meal --model worked-exit --seed 17 --days 730
py -3 -B -m tools.measure_first_meal --model local-crop-return --seed 17 --days 730
py -3 -B -m tools.verify_worked_exit --panel research/single_nest/WORKED_EXIT_TRIALS.jsonl
py -3 -B -m tools.verify_first_meal research/single_nest/WORKED_EXIT_TRIALS.jsonl --seed 2 --direction queen_to_worker
py -3 -B -m tools.verify_first_meal research/single_nest/WORKED_EXIT_TRIALS.jsonl --seed 2 --direction worker_to_queen
```

Repeat the first two commands for nearby-feeder seeds 1, 2, 3, 17 and 29 and for seed 2 with `--supply absent`. To inspect the last six hours before a recorded death, pass one complete JSON receipt to `python -m tools.inspect_worked_return`. The canonical traffic labels in the receipts are actor/kind/foraging mode/reason/outcome, with enum order from op0/codec.py. Growth requires a living founder, a second-year eclosion, and more workers on day 730 than on both days 73 and 365 in at least three of the five supplied seeds. These software replicates are engineering comparisons.
