## Mechanism and disconfirmation

Test an adult worker carrying transferable crop material approaching the mouth of a locally perceived adult. A transfer requires two compatible, unobstructed mouth positions, stationary partners at resolution, available donor surplus, recipient capacity and the existing nutritional demand. Eligibility never uses a distant recipient's private reserves. A local approach may fail because of orientation, terrain, another body, an intervening action or loss of the recipient. Keep the old intake and absorption amounts, mortality, food sources, seeds and all published outcomes.

Count a completed worker-to-founder offer only when a canonical positive transfer names the founder as recipient. Record carbohydrate separately from water. Require a funded positive gift followed by a later founder carbohydrate uptake to claim that this path actually supplied carbohydrate in an ordinary world. Keep direct nest intake and social gifts as separate endpoints. An engineering test of mouth geometry alone cannot establish ordinary-world use or colony persistence. A candidate that impairs the existing direct access gate or lacks an ordinary funded founder gift remains a diagnostic result.

Direct species observations support food distribution through oral exchanges among workers. The cited experiment used five queenless, broodless worker groups. Founder approach and acceptance, distance, directional tolerances, timing and dose remain bounded simulation assumptions. [Study and methods](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-019-52019-6)
