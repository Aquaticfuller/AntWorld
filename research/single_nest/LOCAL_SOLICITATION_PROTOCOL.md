## Component-specific request screen

Seed 17 in physics 68 died of dehydration. The local encounter gates check sugar explicitly, including for a donor with water. Seed 2 in physics 67 had 1.235 J of body energy and no crop sugar at the day-80 cut, below one day of basal cost. The general adult threshold can suppress a request until 0.5 J, only about five hours of basal demand.
