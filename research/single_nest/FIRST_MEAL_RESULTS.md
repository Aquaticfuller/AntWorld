# First external meal and within-nest feeding

**Decision.** Physics 62 reproduced a paid first collection in all five supplied worlds, up from four in Physics 56. The collection preceded founder death in three worlds, up from two. Both adults moved towards one another before a verified oral transfer. One later-cohort worker survived to day 730, up from zero. Sustained growth failed in all five pairs. The branch is suitable for observing and investigating these mechanisms. It does not qualify as a sustained-colony gameplay default.

## Why access failed

The supplied pair starts with the same founding budget, a 1.5 mL finite water wick and a nearby surface feeder 5 mm to one side of the origin, with identical finite releases scheduled through day 730. The feeder side is determined by the seed. The queen must obtain water through the existing ingestion and absorption rules. In the absent-feeder control the wick and maternal starting stores are retained.

* In Physics 56, seed 1 produced adult workers but never collected sugar. An empty depot alone could not put a worker at a load-bearing dig site. In a wider diagnostic on Physics 60 day 55, worker 10 had five reachable candidate stances outside the original narrow sample. Swept movement, turning, support, excavation and carrying clearance are distinct requirements.
* In Physics 61, seed 1 worker 10 reached the edge of visibility of the common handoff. On day 55 the handoff was out of sight and three locally accessible candidate stances remained. During the next day it completed one approach to a nestmate and 37 inactive bouts. The old policy required the handoff to be visible even for this worker to inspect nearby soil. Physics 62 permits a private empty sugar crop and a perceived work face to justify renewed local inspection. A sealed world with an absent feeder can still produce an exit and zero carbohydrate collections.
* In Physics 56, seed 2, one millisecond before founder energy death, the six workers held 1,727 µg of crop carbohydrate. The closest sugar-bearing mouths were 2.715 and 2.831 mm away. The founder held zero crop sugar and 26 nJ of body energy. The depot held 29,322 µg of protein and zero carbohydrate. The founder previously waited for worker initiative. The new model lets a founder re-evaluate locally sensed donors, make a physical approach and prompt a contacted worker to reconsider a meal. The donor must have a payable load and release it through the existing delayed, contact-checked transaction. In a separate Physics 60 day-82 cut two workers carrying 966 µg in total were on the surface. Their sugar was physically outside the founding chamber at that instant.
* A worker can fill its crop as part of its own feeding stop. Previously its external cargo and program label determined the surface homeward choice. Physics 62 checks for a discretionary crop load on a previously sampled carbohydrate trip. A worker outside its own home cell can retrace its recorded route. Entry into the home cell, arrival at the handoff, feeding a nestmate and subsequent uptake are recorded separately. None of these choices plans a route to a remote queen.

Opening a tunnel still does not guarantee a first foraging trip. In seeds 17 and 29, workers 11 and 10 respectively went above ground with soil around day 46 and returned underground. On both days 46 and 48 their native access cuts found a usable connector, an empty crop, no visible handoff and no eligible departure berth. In the intervening 48 hours they completed 95 and 106 inactive bouts respectively. Neither began foraging. The first actual foragers were subsequently worker 9 on day 71.396 and worker 8 on day 68.247, after their respective founder deaths. This separates the period before forager enrollment from outdoor search time. The mechanical restriction is explicit in `at_foraging_home` and `prepare_forager`. A locally usable excavation route can still be ineligible for first foraging enrollment. A separate departure site and an independently verified food return site are needed to remove this restriction safely. Simply treating the dig site as the food depot could credit resources at the wrong location.

## Evidence and limits

At the seed 2 death boundaries, the mixed exterior feeder still physically held 120,302 µg of sugar in Physics 56 and 123,565 µg in Physics 62. These masses are separate from the zero sugar at the handoff and the individual sugar crops inside the nest. In Physics 62, the wick still held 1,499,975 µg of water. The terminal inventories can be reproduced from the initial command and the canonical death time in [the local cuts](FIRST_MEAL_LOCAL_CUTS.jsonl).

The separate day-90 cut in Physics 62 seed 2 recorded 3,853 µg of gross sugar crossing into the queen's crop, 837 µg into larvae, 599 µg into workers younger than seven days and 526 µg into older workers. Larva 20 received 38 µg at event 120346 and registered positive uptake of 38 µg at 120495. Larva 21 received 76 µg at 137201 and registered 76 µg of uptake at 137283. Both later emerged as workers, IDs 20 and 21 throughout, and were alive on day 90. Their later fate is included in the two-year analysis.

The other terminal cuts show different failures. Seed 1 had received a first meal but its founder later ran out of energy with 14 nJ, zero lipid, 127 µg body water and zero crop sugar one millisecond before death. The two adult workers then held just 5 and 10 µg of sugar. Seed 3 lost the founder to dehydration with one microgram of body water, zero crop water and 1,208,198,531 nJ of body energy at the preceding millisecond. Its full 1,500,000 µg wick remained physically present, and the three living workers had no crop water. Removing sugar from a feeder, transferring it to an individual and drinking sufficient water over time are independent requirements.

In Physics 62 seed 2, between days 82 and 84, worker 8 completed 102 approach bouts targeting queen 2 before her death. Her last paid worker meal was on day 74.014. At one millisecond before death her body had 18 nJ, zero lipid and zero crop sugar. Five workers still held 230 µg of sugar. The two workers actually visible to her were 8 and 21, at mouth gaps of 0.978 and 1.410 mm respectively. Both had discretionary sugar and a positive receiver request. The same bounded local motion planner returned no admissible next step for either participant in either pair at that cut. The depot had zero carbohydrate and 20,229 µg protein. Replaying the next millisecond reproduced energy death at event 350402. This pins the remaining failure to oral access at this boundary, with inadequate sugar deliveries in the preceding interval. It does not prove that every geometrically possible multi-ant rearrangement was impossible, or that the terminal geometry persisted for all nine days. A local yielding or turning maneuver would need a swept, collision-checked positive witness in this geometry before use.

The locality, delayed exchange, posture and reserve constraints are engineering approximations. A study of five queenless, broodless groups of at least 50 *L. niger* workers directly observed spatially structured food sharing. It cannot calibrate queen solicitation distance, recipient dose or founder survival. Another experiment observed foragers and nest workers among first-generation workers and removed larvae after the first month. The age groups, food exposure and diapause regime differ from a constant-temperature two-year world. Neither paper supplies an hour-by-hour first-meal target or a matched two-year population curve. [Food distribution study](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-019-52019-6) · [Age and social environment study](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-022-26515-1)

Transfers are gross recipient-specific fluxes. Subsequent intake in an individual can include sugar obtained from more than one source. Short temporal pairing alone cannot identify a molecule or demonstrate a causal nutritional benefit. A software seed is one paired simulation, and the two-year outcome is conditional on this finite feeder and wick. All nine historical biological blockers, the B06 comparison and historical performance qualification retain their reviewed status.

There is a meaningful external comparison for the scale of the growth shortfall. Kramer et al. report 18 ± 8 workers in their source colonies at about eight months and 250 ± 79 at about 20 months after two winters. They provided water, honey-water and crickets and sampled young workers. The two-year Core trials used constant environmental conditions and finite provision. These observations show that sustained growth is biologically achievable under managed feeding. They do not identify a contact probability or prescribe a worker count for this feeder. [Original study, section 2.3](https://www.rug.nl/research/gelifes/tres/_pdf/kramer_etal_expgerontol_2016.pdf)

## Engineering failures retained

Two intermediate runs stopped with `excavation memory no longer ends at the worker position` (Physics 59 seed 2 and Physics 60 seed 3). The choice could restart a previous excavation after the worker had physically left its remembered endpoint. The corrected selection preserves the draw, retains the itinerary and permits a locally clear return to the work site. A follow-up test at the bilateral meal found `invalid care state` during compact restore. The decoder validated the founder's ID before reading the founding block. The final reader checks the actor's role while loading care and checks the exact founder ID after loading both blocks. It preserves the wire format and the simulation trajectory. The positive witness exercises this transition with a real paid meal.

## Repeat the comparison

The final local Windows Release suite passed 292/292 in 184.85 s. All 45 native live-session tests passed in 1,093.29 s. An earlier concurrent run had a timeout in `FND_IF_BOUNDARY_COLONY`, which passed alone at the original deadline and again in the final full suite. A previous live test assumed an asynchronous seek could never progress between two controller requests. It observed an actual 12-hour slice before cancellation. The immediate-cancellation test now serializes that specific before-slice case under the same controller lock. Runtime cancellation semantics are unchanged.

From the checkout root, build with `py -3 -B -m tools.build_m0_live --jobs 3`. Each registered trial contains its full initialization command, Core version, executable hash, initial and final frame hashes, and the number of committed facts.
