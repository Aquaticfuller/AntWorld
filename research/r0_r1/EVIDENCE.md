# R0-r1 evidence and implementation decisions

## Primary observations

Toffin et al. (2009), Methods, Figures 1–3 and Table 1, used worker-only groups of *Lasius niger* from 11 source colonies, without food or brood during a 90-hour planar digging assay. A transition out of compact expansion was identified in 12 of 20 groups of 50 and 29 of 33 groups of 300. The observed late rise in perimeter relative to area motivated comparing compact growth and amplification under different colony loads. The paper's activity density is digging rate divided by excavated area. The locally visible body count in R0-r1 has units of individuals and measures local space demand. [Original article](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC2773997/).

Toffin, Kindekens and Deneubourg (2010), §§2b–d and 3a, Figures 1–2 and Tables 1–2, used groups of 50 workers under two 42 cm glass plates with a 2 mm layer. The two substrates shared 15% water by dry mass. The paper reports both transitions in 24 of 25 cohesive trials and 12 of 24 granular trials. Differences in threshold area and group outcomes are evidence that the material matters. The current voxel model has explicit terrain and load resistance to obstruction. Quantitative soil cohesion and fine-grain extraction remain outside the simulation. [Original article](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC2982035/).

The opening R0 audit pinned and ran the AntSimulator worker updater and identified local probes and rate-limited heading as relevant design ideas. The SebLague review describes a local steering vector and terrain tests in the original source. The R0-r1 implementation uses the existing AntWorld solver and integer turn sectors. Ethology sources, including open-canvas tracks and nest detections, differ in geometry, available load and movement detection. The experimental body turning clock here is an engineering fixture. [R0 evidence and reference pins](../r0/REFERENCES.md).

## Implementation decisions

- Physics 33 / schema 26 encodes independent worker motor and excavation scoring flags. R0 physics 32 uses the original path and byte layout. The motor stores the latest committed coarse facing per autonomous worker. A turn takes 40 ms per horizontal 45° sector. Vertical transitions between horizontal and vertical facing take 80 ms and full reversal takes 160 ms. These rates use the inherited distance and load clocks and the existing activity cost ledger. They remain ENGINEERING_FIXTURE.
- The motor owns scheduled segment endpoints. Physical obstruction checks and position revisions govern real moves as in the earlier transaction. Endpoint ranking observes local bodies and in-flight claims for resting destinations. Body overlap is permitted at active hand-offs and in some narrow transit. Within a segment the indexed production position remains at its last committed knot. A contact request to an in-flight nutrition recipient is deferred by the local reach rule. The viewer shows the open path and progress as a lease. Archive slow motion reserves the observed turning interval before reconstructing only a completed segment. Normal biology reads production coordinates.
- Integrated workers can observe and handle nearby brood from an off-depot site. The first review after emergence can approach the actual local depot. Further approaches require local need and bounded routes. The route uses up to 96 short candidate stances with obstacle, support and body checks. Access to distant sites outside the sensed patch still depends on a real connector, the inherited forager memory or the current excavator trip. Physics 33 supports the independent 15-minute timing fixture.
- The excavation score uses six horizontal nearest-center neighbors in a derived pitched hex layer and explicit swept-body portals between layers. A center footprint measures neighboring opening cells. Geometry scores compete alongside path cost, genuine prior local cuts, roominess and, in the full treatment, local adult and brood bodies. A worker alone owns the elapsed action, physiological payment, exact parcel and carry/drop route. The last successful cut is read from a worker's actual soil transaction and verified during checkpoint restoration.
- The independent floor analysis projects actual ordered voxels at the starting chamber's mid-plane, then computes loaded body clearance and immediate floor support. It keeps the reachable component seeded at the depot. A wide core requires at least four plane points with chessboard distance at least twice the loaded half extent plus one quantum from wall. Narrow paths can connect two separately detected wide cores in one continuous cavity. The centerline of the supported mask is thinned and filtered by spurs of up to two voxels. Junctions are grouped neighboring degree-three-or-higher narrow points. The measure is plane-dependent. Digs above or below the chosen layer remain in the volumetric edit history.
