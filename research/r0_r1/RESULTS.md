# R0-r1 true motility and nest growth results

Physics 33 is an opt-in engineering experiment. A worker can execute a real local motion lease with orientation, turning cost, travel duration, terrain checks and recorded arrival. Actual excavators choose among locally sensed fronts, pay for a cut, and dispose of real soil. The candidate misses the R0-r1 motion realism and multiple-chamber goals. Its 730-day closed-loop ecology has a material survival regression.

## Worker motion

Each cell uses a single queen, natural adult emergence, Borisovka, seed 1 and the same finite food releases within its pair. The 90-day comparisons enable the motor independently of the scoring changes. The continued-supply shaft reproduces the historical 946 equal-coordinate pairs on day 730 with 54 workers. The candidate has no equal-coordinate pairs among 30 workers at the same time. The lower population and earlier halt to reproduction prevent treating the spacing change as a controlled same-population improvement.

Just before the death event, the checkpoint retains the last scheduled physiological state. It is a sampled stored state, with the elapsed debit applied at the actual event. This traces local delivery and uptake failure in this assay. The motor, repositioning and altered access history co-vary in the long comparison. The evidence does not identify which changed decision caused the missed delivery.

## Excavation and morphology

Only workers are initialized here. Initial cavities, rock, geometry, stored energy, hydration and the work clock match within each seed. Light conditions and moist experimental substrate mechanics were outside the assay. The candidate has four independent modes: the original random-boundary control, six-neighbor score, local reinforcement, and reinforcement with occupancy and nearby-room suppression. The separate motor flag remains off for this matrix. Excavation feedback reuses each worker's own prior paid cuts and nearby recent voxel edits. Its shape score is `24 - 2(6 - 2N)`, before the finite transport and feedback terms, with `N` equal to open hex neighbors. This favors regular expansion when jobs can be reached immediately around the original depot. The full rule also rewards busy tips and penalizes nearby roomy sections. Neither the grid overlay nor the analysis edits physical geometry.

The independent analyzer measures one registered plane through each colony depot. Narrow degree-three centerline junctions and broad area cores are separate features. A counted chamber core must fit the loaded envelope on supported reachable floor and exceed the declared width threshold. A projected narrow neck toward a wall or into an unoccupied end does not establish a distinct chamber. Voxel cuts at other levels remain in the geometry ledger and can be inspected at different section heights. This protocol cannot conclude the absence of every three-dimensional branch from one floor projection.

At 50 workers and 90 h (seed 1), the full rule yields 1,618 excavations and 1,613 deposits, with net volume 9,456 voxel quanta. The control yields 564 excavations and deposits and net volume 3,920 quanta. Both retain one wide reachable core. The full score produces a measurable late neck without a new connected room in this run. All numbers are tied to genuinely transported parcels and closed soil budgets. They measure different work distributions under identical inputs. The positive spatial-feedback signal in the full rule amplifies local work in these conditions. The actual return anchor and directional reuse still focus most cuts around a single growing cavity.

The Toffin 2009 groups of 50 and 300 provide direct species evidence for different frequencies of compact-to-branched transition over 90 h in their planar setup. R0-r1 uses a three-dimensional shaft with finite loaded reach and different substrate, initial space and worker transport rules. The observed short traces offer no fitted reproduction of their transition test. Toffin 2010 shows that granular and cohesive substrates have distinct transition outcomes. R0-r1 represents blocking earth and rock and does not resolve granular cohesion. The reference data support competing mechanisms, with no validated map from their fit parameters to the weights used here. ## Engineering, publication and limitations

- The comparison witnesses start from real `initial.chk` and expose an ordered `facts.bin` plus a checked `final.frame`. The native application validates food, water, energy and soil ledgers at every reported cut. The independent verifier checks canonical order, identity, edit provenance, state overlap and a recorded timed segment. Representative 90-day runs repeat across SQLite save and restoration with identical checkpoints, canonical streams and final frames. Native tests also restore an in-flight movement. Replay updates no RNG and cannot modify live world state.
- Physics 32 preserves the original byte layout. The long matched reference reproduced its historical census. Physics 33 has its own checkpoint/physics marker. It stores headings at motion knots and a duration and turn interval in genuine movement facts, with no per-frame world facts. Movement between knots is still a bounded scheduled segment, so continuous microcontact, free yaw and full multi-agent traffic are open. Native route and historical validation follow the authoritative segment clocks.
- Same-time occupancy and contact allow biologically reasonable proximity, with some artificial overlap retained at depot hand-offs. Layers use projected local routes and true body clearance, with limited side attachment for vertical access. Automated falling soil, humidity and structural failure are outside the tested ground model.
- Archived `*.json.gz` diagnostics contain sampled states, per-worker summaries, failures, edits and canonical SHA-256 receipts. Only preselected cases publish full browser witnesses. The longer cases can be rerun via the checked-in runner. The renderer marks the timed interval of a true recorded segment and never adds canonical positions or behavior events.
- The historical B06 comparison is `INSUFFICIENT`, the inherited application performance qualification is `FAIL`, and all nine root biological issues remain `BIOLOGICAL_BLOCKED`. No observations in this release establish a new calibration or qualify the candidate for promotion.
