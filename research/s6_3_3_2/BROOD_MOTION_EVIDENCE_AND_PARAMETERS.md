# Brood motion evidence and parameters

The [Evidence Atlas](../../ANT_BIOLOGY_EVIDENCE_ATLAS.md) remains the project reference. This supplement adds the source locators and parameter rules for the frozen brood-motion profile. Source papers and scan images stay local. The tracked tables retain numerical observations, missing values and derived values separately. The [source register](SOURCE_REGISTER.csv) specifies the scope of each use.

## Movement and custody

- Khuong et al. used top-view tracking of freely walking workers at multiple inclinations. The previous analysis used all 69 horizontal trajectories from three colonies, sampled at 25 Hz. At one-second coarsening, median path rate was 37.677 mm/s and median net displacement rate was 4.684 mm/s. Those statistics depend on the tracking window and metric. The underlying tracks contain no brood load and no queen. [Methods and Dataset S1](https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0076531.s005), [prior analysis](../../experiments/s6_3_3_1/independent_movement).
- Carthy, Part I, pp. 276–281 and Figs. 1 and 3–7, traced marked workers in a rotatable arena connected to a plaster nest. The published tracks include exploration loops and repeated empty-outbound and loaded-return journeys. Returns became more direct across practice, with strong individual variation. Red-light and screen treatments sometimes lengthened or disoriented returns. Rotation of the washed glass and the sensory interventions separate substrate cue, brood recognition and visual orientation questions in this apparatus. The author reports a nonsignificant aggregate red-light comparison on p. 291 alongside seven of twelve significant individual series. These experiments support a directional role for visual cues in trained workers. They leave natural brood-trail recruitment and queen navigation unresolved. [Part I full citation](https://doi.org/10.1163/156853951X00304).
- Carthy's pp. 292 and 296 Tables II and IV have timed paths for workers 17 and 27. The [raw transcription](../../experiments/s6_3_3_2/literature/carthy_loaded_returns.csv) preserves the printed column and missing or incomplete observations. The speed heading reads inches per second, although many values match inches per minute numerically. Some rows disagree even under that interpretation. Table II prints a row numbered 5 while the caption excludes it and a row numbered 6 is absent. Series II lost its time records. Table IV includes incomplete and switched-lighting intervals. Use `path_mm = distance_in * 25.4` and `derived_path_rate_mm_s = path_mm / time_s` without changing the raw cells. The inch conversion is exact. Under normal illumination the complete timed paths span roughly 11.75–29.90 mm/s across the two individuals. Treat repeated journeys within an individual as repeated measurements. The 4 mm/s worker base and 1–12 mm/s sensitivity were fixed before receiving this scan. The evidence shows their limitations in this trained-worker context and does not establish a population constant, a load slowdown factor or a queen speed. [Table source](https://doi.org/10.1163/156853951X00304), [inch definition](https://www.nist.gov/pml/owm/si-units-length).
- Lenoir, pp. 154–157, records repeated worker departures after delivering larvae and heterogeneous retrieval participation. The protocol did not record individual transport timing. In pp. 161–164, Tables 3–5, summer first trials showed no systematic origin preference under the small one-off comparison. Repetition and season altered preference. Post-transport rejection and later nursing outcomes distinguish pickup from acceptance. The suggested role of a chemical trail in recruitment is a hypothesis. [Author full text](https://www.dictionnaire-amoureux-des-fourmis.fr/Publications/1981-Lenoir-Sociobiol.pdf).
- Okrutniak et al. pp. 3–4 and Figs. 1 and 3 provide an external colony-level time scale. The protocol used disturbance plus a lit/dark boundary, adult workers and 25 pupae. Of 46 retained colonies, 38 started transport within one minute and 43 finished within 20 minutes of the first transfer. One colony with relocation over two hours was excluded by the original authors. The body-size-variation association was nonsignificant. These times cover organization and transport in groups. Neither individual loaded speed nor a matched B02 endpoint is available. [Article](https://doi.org/10.1111/ens.12549), [university full text](https://bazawiedzy.uwm.edu.pl/seam/resource/rest/download/UWM0af26f66548745c18fd44a62e41c2a66?cid=8667586&entityId=UWM8dd0b8abf17b4ce2964ab1e6a9ed7d4c&entityType=article).
- Parmentier and Wybouw separately tested *L. niger* in their own-larva rescue controls, with three nests and one trial per nest. Section 3.2 reports the positive rescue endpoint at 120 minutes. Their detailed trajectories and aphid-cue mechanism concern *L. flavus*. The *L. niger* controls supply no matched pickup, release, or inter-trip distribution. [Methods 2.3 and Results 3.2](https://doi.org/10.1098/rsos.250217).

## Timing model

`care_internal.hpp::care_duration` implements a segment duration in integer milliseconds. It takes the ceiling of 1,000 times path length in meters divided by the smaller of caste/load preferred speed and the world limit in meters per second. A translating segment commits a position only when completed. Motion rechecks geometry and custody at the segment boundary. In real 3D it checks the carrier and brood trajectories with the loaded extent. A small segment integrates piecewise motion while leaving direction changes, start/stop acceleration and within-step observability unresolved.

Perception, voluntary initiation, pickup, carrying, placement and subsequent search have separate clocks. The fixed handling durations and heading-free bounded search have sparse direct evidence and use the frozen engineering values. The most relevant unknowns are caste- and load-conditioned speed, search turning persistence, first-contact to pickup latency, actual manipulation duration, unloading and reacquisition under matched settings. Higher movement speed alone does not imply improved search or contact.

The [parameter provenance table](PARAMETER_PROVENANCE.csv) records each new quantity and the structural choices that affect estimates. Each numeric coefficient retains grade F. The published datasets give grade A measurements for their observed endpoints, while qualitative behavior does not quantitatively identify the code coefficients. Current stage outputs fit zero coefficients. No raw videos with paired loaded and unloaded **queen and first-generation worker** tracks in B02 conditions were found in the sources reviewed. Future work can use separately annotated paths, trial order, stages and blocked cases as independent constraints.
