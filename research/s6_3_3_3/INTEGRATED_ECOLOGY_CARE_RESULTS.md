# Integrated ecology and care results

The headless program follows 72 worlds for 21 game days. It crosses seeds 7, 19 and 43, 4 and 16 initial workers, finite balanced food and no external food, three spaces, and care enabled/disabled. All contain a queen and seeded egg, two larvae and a pupa. In care-enabled arms the queen can opt into the existing local care lease, while workers retain ordinary autonomy. The controls keep worker autonomy, physiology and reproduction and leave the care lease off. This contrast measures the combined integration configuration. Initial stores, resource inputs and other clocks are fixed between paired cases.

Absorption, ordinary task and nutrition decisions use six game hours, water loss uses one hour, and egg, larval and pupal base durations are two, three and two game days. Reproduction has a two-day opportunity interval with the inherited reserve rules. The ordinary forager and existing sub-second care motor retain their original clocks. These are inherited ENGINEERING_FIXTURE values. There are no accelerated biological intervals, scripted transfers, imposed life transitions or survivor guarantees in the matrix. There is finite substrate and a real open vertical shaft in the mixed 3D chamber. The primary matrix makes no manual edits.

## Committed behavior

- Across all arms, 4,551,386 facts include 5,064 positive transfers, 2,845 transfers to brood, 281 food returns, 175 births and 218 emergences. Forty-four in-world offspring reached adulthood. The birth/transition rows keep maternal linkage and one entity ID. Of the 2,845 recorded feed completions, seven are timed care acts and 2,838 are the ordinary nutrition path with unknown action duration. Both join an actual positive `NutritionTransferred` fact by scheduled source, donor and recipient. Their mass is counted once. They carry no invented legacy duration.
- In seed 7 with four workers, a 1 mm grid and finite food, worker 4 places displaced larva 9 (event 424), gives 60 µg carbohydrate and 37 µg water to brood 7 (event 3209), and later delivers 2,594 µg carbohydrate, 263 µg protein and 1,143 µg water (event 11575). There are five workers across the matrix with a placement, a brood transfer and an actual food return. Fifteen have placement, brood transfer and a distinct rest, forage or excavation program. A resting program is a role change, with physical activity recorded separately.
- In that same world queen 2 starts care before and after a birth. Birth event 2704 is followed by another care lease at 2706. Offspring 12 has egg, larva, pupa and adult evidence at events 2704, 3891, 6344 and 8031 (days 2, 4, 7.25 and 9.25). It has the same ID and mother in durable history. The event sequence demonstrates interleaving under the scheduler. It does not imply that these phase rates describe *Lasius niger*.
- All 12 integrated fine-grid worlds and all 12 integrated mixed chambers place the displaced target. None of the 12 integrated coarse-grid worlds does. In the chamber the brood's committed carried positions include y = −3 mm and −2 mm after starting at −4 mm. Its final range-based placement is still under the entrance. The physical relocation occurs across vertical segments and uses actual loaded clearance.
- Scarcity is material. Initial body and crop reserves can fund limited transfers and early births even with no external food. All 36 empty worlds had zero living individuals at day 21 and two emergences per run on average. Across all worlds 916 deaths were recorded as energy depletion. Only three queens survived to day 21, all in control arms. The experiment does not identify a biological mortality rate or isolate egg allocation from local nutrition, activity and configuration.

## Geometry controls and limitations

The matched 10 mm/1 mm surface probes use identical positions, a 10 mm target separation, 1 mm stride and 6 mm/12 mm radii. With 6 mm there is no initial geometric cue and no approach. With 12 mm the coarse-grid policy selects approach but `care_toward` returns the current position since the cell is larger than the stride. The action commits a Blocked interruption. In the fine-grid 12 mm probe eight approaches complete and the queen picks up. One loaded move succeeds. At the second segment the intended 2 mm grip offset is computed as 0.0090000000000000011 − 0.0070000000000000001 m. It exceeds 0.002 m by floating-point roundoff and the begin check records OutOfReach. This is a separate representation defect, not evidence of natural loss of grip. The other fine ecology worlds can place when their approach and carried offsets differ. For a future correction, verify rigid grip invariance and collision/ownership separately at exactly representable and adjacent thresholds. An independent subcell/portal motion design is needed for coarse cells. Increasing stride solely to pass the coarse control would confound spatial and movement scales.

The three-dimensional interruption probe physically excavates one parcel, acquires a brood load, and fills a passable slice using that same soil while the load has an active segment. The geometry transaction interrupts the segment with Blocked, clears custody and retains the prior brood position. It is the declared intervention outside the 72-world matrix. Two geometry edits and the soil ledger reconcile through recovery. The primary mixed worlds demonstrate vertical transport and local feeding, but no food deliveries. At the depot the 10 mm surface steps cross the solid rim around the 4 mm-wide shaft. `foraging.cpp::sense` requires a swept-clear next cell and these worlds never select Forage or emit ForagingStarted. Food releases 21 times and remains outside access. At day 21 the available carbohydrate is 84,000 µg for four-worker balanced runs and 88,000 µg for 16-worker runs. This is a fixture-specific surface-to-chamber routing limit. The test does not prove a sustained supply chain in a general 3D nest.

## Determinism, identity and accounting

All 72 cases reach the same checkpoint bytes after single advance, regular six-hour advance and SQLite day-10 recovery followed by irregular intervals. Daily and six-hour partitions produce byte-identical run, individual, ledger and event-row exports. At the final checkpoint all ordered durable event payloads compare equal to the in-memory canonical encoding, including source, payload and observer fields, across the 4,551,386 facts. The CSV projection records all event rows with the relevant positions, IDs, stages, action types, times and resource amounts. The durable check compares the complete encoding. Runtime and durable identity have the same creation time and stage. Daily replay also verifies a contiguous history and monotonic stage progression for each ID. For each nutrient, introduced mass equals accounted mass. Body energy plus dissipated energy equals accounted input. The soil volume ledger matches in the configured worlds and at the fill intervention. The [72 manifests and daily stocks](../../experiments/s6_3_3_3/ecology), [partition receipt](../../experiments/s6_3_3_3/verification/ecology_partition_replay.json) and [event sequence extract](../../experiments/s6_3_3_3/ecology_sequences.csv) expose those checks.

Day-scale integration is **CONDITIONAL PASS** under the actual tested fine-grid and chamber capabilities. Coarse surface transport, robust held contact at the reach boundary, chamber-to-surface foraging, and long maternal provisioning still prevent a general readiness claim.
