# Current care state and evidence audit

This supplement tracks the reviewed runtime and the experimental harness separately. [The Evidence Atlas](../../ANT_BIOLOGY_EVIDENCE_ATLAS.md) defines grades A–F. The earlier [P01/P10 audit](../s6_3_3_2/BROOD_CARE_STATE_MACHINE_AUDIT.md) remains part of the baseline. A direct observation of an act does not confer grade A on the implementation of its trigger or latency.

| State or transition | Current source and actual condition | Evidence and closure finding |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Eligibility | `care.cpp::enable_brood_care` admits live adult queens and workers, with a caste-specific profile and firsthand nest position on enrollment. `ecology.cpp::resolve_autonomy_wakes` and `care.cpp::local_care_available` govern the separate opt-in integrated lease | B01/B02 directly observe queen and worker care. Caste, developmental experience, ambient conditions and eligibility states remain distinct. The age field is contextual metadata. Integrated queens receive the founding context; established-state physiology remains open |
| Local cue and true opportunity | `geometry.cpp::collect_care_neighbors` enumerates indexed neighbors under radius and geometry. `care.cpp::resolve_care_wakes` emits per-target Opportunity only after local and contact checks. Neutral context can carry displaced larvae/pupae from another owner | Direct experiments support carrying both origins in B02. Grade F radius and eligibility boundary. The neutral context leaves donor ownership and adoption independent. Visibility after release and within search are separate from close opportunity facts |
| Voluntary onset and inactivity | `care.cpp::resolve_care_wakes` checks maternal or worker reserves, local cue and the voluntary draw. Inactive consumes a decision interval. Dedicated no-cue trials walk short independent cardinal steps in bounded bursts. Integrated enrollment away from shelter requires a prior shelter experience and a valid step back | First handling can be nearly immediate or delayed by multiples of 60 s. The data identify an interaction between initial cue, draw, timing and repeat encounters. These rules provide no age-conditioned or heading-persistent search model |
| Approach and handling | A visible displaced target persists until lost or no longer eligible. `care_internal.hpp::care_toward` chooses one valid decreasing-distance cardinal neighbor in surface-only mode or one continuous step under geometry. `care.cpp::execute_care_action` validates approach geometry and reach before and after scheduling | Contact proxies are event-defined. With a 10 mm cell and 1 mm stride a visible target is unable to trigger actual approach movement. With a 1 mm cell the matched probe approaches and picks up. Cue availability and movement representation have separate failures |
| Pickup and exclusive custody | Within touch distance, timed displacement chooses PickUpBrood. At completion exclusive ownership and the private pickup site are stored. Contesting actors can lose the reservation | Validated local engineering primitive. No scored inspection requirement, pickup latency distribution or uncertainty in grip after a successful pickup. Caste-specific intent and handling skill are uncalibrated |
| Loaded motion and placement | Compute a direct vector from brood to the remembered shelter. Validate the moving body and brood extents. At each segment commit preserve the brood offset. Placement succeeds within shelter range and releases custody | Carthy supports loaded orientation and practice effects in trained workers. The model can schedule a nearly straight path with millisecond handoffs. A controlled 1 mm surface case interrupts at OutOfReach after an exactly 2 mm intended offset rounds above the test boundary. The 3D fill probe confirms interruption and release before any blocked motion commits |
| Revisit and return | Dedicated timed care waits the fixed post-placement pause and may revisit one pickup point, prioritizing eligible visible brood. Integrated care clears the retrieval memory after a placement or settled act and tries to return to the known shelter before ending the lease | Lenoir observes repeated departures and inter-individual variation. The memory capacity, route geometry, timing and restart probabilities are fixtures. No direction arrow or pheromone-based brood recruitment |
| Settled manipulation and feeding | Local undisplaced candidates trigger inspection, arrangement or cleaning. A larva with matching need and donor crop can add FeedBrood to the choices. `physiology.cpp::resolve_nutrition_action` authorizes the actual transfer and updates donor/receiver mass | Distinct act and transfer facts. B01 scans identify presence of manipulation over a window. They do not identify the model's equal categorical draw or per-act durations. Day-scale evidence distinguishes a timed FeedBrood from the legacy trophallaxis observer |
| Live contact and suppression | Reachable same-colony workers may take a queen's decision slot. At successful CloseWorkerContact completion the last contact time is set. The gain multiplies linearly decaying recency during a later voluntary initiation draw | B01 supports a live-worker treatment and disfavors a static proximity proxy. Mesh can allow antennation. The model has one contact type, no endocrine mediator or independently timed withdrawal measurement. Six-seed clock and pathway interventions are archived |
| Coexistence with foraging, rest, excavation and reproduction | `ecology.cpp::resolve_autonomy_wakes` uses existing local task/stores/position checks and honors the care lease. `long_term.cpp` retains the existing reproductive opportunity and defers an overlapping care lease. `physiology.cpp` owns activity-dependent accounting and lifecycle | Real switches and births are proven for specified fixtures. Loaded and feeding work can compete for scarce donor opportunities. Six-hour autonomy and nutrition clocks are engineering values. World geometry and local experience still constrain food access |
| Invalidations and history | `care.cpp::interrupt_care`, `validate_active_care`, `checkpoint_care.hpp` and the existing journal maintain ownership, cancellation and recoverable continuations. Observation stages are independently committed facts | Validated engineering constraints with stable actor/target/bout IDs. A held load interrupted by a real wall fill stays at the last committed position. Death and maturation regressions remain in the suite |

See the [parameter register](PARAMETER_PROVENANCE.csv) for applicable numerical scopes. Potential changes must separate cue sensitivity, spontaneous search, handling latency and physical representation, then validate each against distinct observations. The current closure exports diagnose these mechanisms without changing any Core transition.
