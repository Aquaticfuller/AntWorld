# Biological validation and uncertainty

## Walsh B02 conditions and experimental units

| Condition | Original experiment | Frozen simulation representation |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Unit | One queen or three workers together. Paired own and conspecific donor trials, order randomized, 20 min back in the nest between trials | One queen or a group of three, one paired seed per pair of origins. Each run begins from a fresh initial state. Trial-order carryover NOT IMPLEMENTED |
| Subjects | Incipient queens and approximately 30-day-old marked first-generation workers. Later-generation trios form a separate cohort | Adult caste and enrollment context. Morphology, experience accrued with age and group heterogeneity APPROXIMATE or NOT IMPLEMENTED |
| Brood | Six medium or large larvae and/or pupae, transferred by brush | Six stable identities, three modeled larvae and three pupae. Exact developmental substage composition UNKNOWN |
| Arena | Circular 50 mm Petri dish, neutral surface, red light, 3 min cylinder acclimation | Circle radius 25 mm, same release interval. Familiarity accrual during acclimation and light-dependent orientation NOT IMPLEMENTED |
| Shelter | Humid 1.5 mL Eppendorf tube left in the subject's home nest for three days | Position and 2 mm acceptance zone learned at enrollment. Tube entrance, scent and moisture cues NOT IMPLEMENTED |
| Placement | Disclosed schematic arrangement in Fig. 1 | Line, split, ring and offset are explicit engineering possibilities. The wall is a blocking control. Exact starting coordinates, headings and trajectories UNKNOWN |
| Origin | Own or a different conspecific colony | Preserve the donor ColonyId. Neutral assay allows locally detected displaced conspecific larvae and pupae to be carried. Post-retrieval nursing, ownership change and natural adoption are outside this contract |
| Endpoint | Number retrieved in 10 min, recorded first contact and retrieving markers, duration and rate | Count distinct identities at completed placements within 600 s after release. Validated manipulation start proxies first contact. Placement is a transparent comparison to the raw first retrieving marker. Frame-level alignment UNKNOWN |

The original Methods report 79 queens, 67 first-generation groups and 50 later-generation groups. The deposited raw table has 79, 70 and 50 origin pairs respectively. The 67 queen/first-worker matched colonies and 61 productivity analysis units remain distinguishable from the pooled 70 first-worker labels. The comparison reported in the review uses all deposited trial pairs and excludes the later-worker stratum. A bootstrap resamples real colonies and simulated seeds, averaging the two origins within each. The original paired retrieval-rate tests found no significant origin effect for queens, first-generation or later-generation workers (p = 0.5027, 0.5152 and 0.8146). Failure to reject that contrast does not establish equivalence or imply unconditional acceptance. In the same fixture a known sheltered foreign item remains outside eligibility for routine care. [Walsh pp. 10411–10413, Methods 2.2–2.3, Fig. 1 and Results 3.1](https://doi.org/10.1002/ece3.4475), [deposited paired trials](https://doi.org/10.5061/dryad.qm114cj).

The two count errors are contradictions under the reviewed line representation. Contact has an observable definition gap, and only the worker proxy interval excludes zero. The timing-marker error is sensitive to event definition in addition to behavior. The 360 additional runs explore the size and direction of error under unknown layout. They give no likelihood for which layout the animals experienced. No averaging over layouts, fit to observed means or promotion of a matching layout to validation is justified. Example at 6 mm, the split moves the queen count to 4.50 while leaving the earliest measured placement mean at 12.52 s in the matched six-seed cohort. The ring puts both endpoints far below the line. Changing one unknown can change the sign of the count error without reproducing the timing distribution. ## Majidifar B01

Use the paper's active-care scan definition. Three focal observations compare an absent-worker control, 24 h after worker addition and 48 h after worker removal. The deposited 16 units per focal treatment give 6.500%, 0.125% and 6.250%. The reviewed 12-seed model scores 2.833%, 1.000% and 6.667% with the same primary 50-scan denominator. This agrees with lower care in the interacting-worker condition and recovery after worker loss, with differences in levels and recovery relative to the absent control. The prior bootstrap and the six-seed interventions are separate. Rare positive scans and short observation windows matter, especially for the interacting group. The model's physical separation controls prevent its single close-contact category. A mesh that admits antennation, transfer between tubes with a corresponding sham, nurse-role differences between callow and older workers, and age-dependent maternal physiology remain APPROXIMATE or NOT IMPLEMENTED. [Majidifar Methods 2.1.4 and 2.2, Fig. 1 and Fig. 2c](https://doi.org/10.1111/1365-2435.14536), [Dryad](https://doi.org/10.5061/dryad.pnvx0k6wd), [reviewed errors](../../experiments/s6_3_3_2/analysis/b01_paper_errors.csv).

## Independent experimental constraints

The papers below concern *Lasius niger*. They are independent of Walsh's observations. None supplies a matched, independent queen/three-worker six-item endpoint with an aligned placement ethogram. No coefficients were fitted in this batch. B01/B02 remain development datasets, including when new model seeds are used.

## Prospective comparison

For a future fit, specify the cue stimulus and pre-exposure separately from the positions and define handling, loaded onset, shelter entry, release and censoring before observing outcomes. Keep queen and colony/trio as the sampling units and preserve order. Estimate rates with times at risk, joint endpoints and colony-level variation. Hold back independent colonies and trials using the same ethogram and apparatus. The independent perturbations above can constrain hypotheses about learning, age and maternal context, with each marked out-of-domain until its own relevant conditions are represented.
